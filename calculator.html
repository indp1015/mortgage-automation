How to Use This Calculator

Enter your target home price and down payment. The calculator automatically applies the 2026 FHA and Conforming loan limits for your selected county. Monthly payments include principal, interest, property tax, insurance, and (for FHA loans) mortgage insurance premiums.

Use the loan type comparison to see how FHA, VA, USDA, and Conventional loans differ in total cost. VA and USDA loans offer 0% down payment options for eligible borrowers.
