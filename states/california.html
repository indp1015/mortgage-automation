Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum FHA loan limit in California?

The highest 2026 FHA limit in California is $811,931 for a single-family home.

What is the maximum conforming loan limit in California?

The highest 2026 conforming loan limit in California is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this threshold are considered "jumbo" and require stricter qualifications.

How many counties are in California?

California has 56 counties, each with its own loan limits published annually by FHFA and HUD.
