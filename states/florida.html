What is the maximum FHA loan limit in Florida?

The highest 2026 FHA limit in Florida is $643,597 for a single-family home.

What is the maximum conforming loan limit in Florida?

The highest 2026 conforming loan limit in Florida is $990,150 for a single-family home. Loans above this threshold are considered "jumbo" and require stricter qualifications.
