HELOC & Home Equity in California

Homeowners in California have an estimated average of $261,200 in home equity (35% assumption for mortgaged homes). Across 56 counties, here's the breakdown.

💡 Rule of thumb: Home equity ≈ home value × 0.35 for mortgaged homes. HELOC capacity ≈ home value × 0.20 (85% max CLTV, minus current mortgage balance). Actual equity depends on your specific loan.
