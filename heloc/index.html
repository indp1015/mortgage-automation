Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HELOC?

A Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC) is a revolving credit line secured by your home. You can draw funds as needed during a "draw period" (typically 10 years), then repay over an amortization period (typically 20 years).

How is HELOC interest calculated?

Most HELOCs have variable rates tied to the Prime Rate. Your rate equals Prime + a lender margin (0.5% to 2.5%). During the draw period, you typically pay interest-only; during repayment, principal + interest.

Do I need good credit for a HELOC?

Most lenders require a 620+ credit score and a debt-to-income ratio below 43%. The best rates (Prime + 0.5%) typically go to borrowers with 760+ scores.

Is HELOC interest tax-deductible in 2026?

Interest may be deductible if funds are used to buy, build, or substantially improve the home securing the loan. Other uses generally do not qualify. Consult a tax professional for your situation.
