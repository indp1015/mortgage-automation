📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Eastland County is ample: the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 equals 402 % of the median home value of $134,800, and the conforming limit of $832,750 represents 618 % of that price, confirming that both programs exceed typical purchase costs. With a median household income of $53,549 (monthly $4,462), an FHA loan requiring 3.5 % down on a $130,082 principal generates a P&I payment of $900, consuming 20.2 % of income. A conventional loan with 20 % down on a $107,840 principal yields a $746 payment, or 16.7 % of income. The current 7.40 % rate thus makes the FHA payment roughly one‑fifth of median income, a level that, while high, remains below the conventional 20 % threshold for affordability. Down‑payment requirements are modest for FHA—3.5 % of $134,800 equals about $4,700—often achievable through savings or assistance, whereas conventional 10 % and 20 % down payments demand $13,500 and $27,000 respectively, presenting significant barriers. If rates were 1 % lower, the FHA payment would fall to approximately $770, reducing the income share to 17.1 %, illustrating the strong sensitivity of affordability to interest rate changes. The 17.7 % five‑year price CAGR indicates sustained appreciation, yet the FHA limit continues to cover the median price well above its 2017 level of $59,800. Given the price‑to‑income ratio and the low down‑payment need, FHA financing is the most suitable option for typical buyers in Eastland County. It aligns with the median home price, limits the payment burden to just over 20 % of income, and circumvents the large cash outlay required for conventional loans. Prospective purchasers should therefore prioritize FHA loans, leveraging the minimal down payment to secure homeownership despite the region’s rapid price appreciation. This approach enables buyers to overcome the 17.7 % annual price increase while keeping monthly payments below one‑fifth of their household earnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Eastland County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Eastland County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Eastland County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Eastland County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Eastland County qualifies.
