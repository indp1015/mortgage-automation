📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Shelby County, Texas, presents a distinct financing landscape where national loan limits significantly exceed local price points. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 533% of the median home value of $101,500, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 820%. Consequently, price is not a barrier to accessing standard mortgage products; virtually all properties in the county fall well within both tiers. This ample coverage ensures that typical buyers do not require jumbo loans or specialized high-limit products, simplifying the underwriting process and expanding lender availability. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though interest rates exert significant pressure. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down on the median home results in a principal and interest payment of $678. This figure represents 16.3% of the median household income of $49,776, a ratio that aligns with conventional debt-to-income guidelines but leaves less room for other expenses compared to lower-rate environments. Conversely, a conventional loan with 20% down yields a payment of $562, or 13.5% of income. The shift from the 2022 price peak of $107,400 to the 2024 median of $101,500 has slightly reduced the absolute loan amount required, but the elevated interest rate has kept monthly obligations higher than they would be in a sub-5% rate environment. For most buyers, the FHA loan is the most accessible entry point due to its 3.5% minimum down payment requirement, which amounts to approximately $3,553 on a median-priced home. This low barrier is feasible for qualified applicants, particularly given the high owner-occupancy rate of 74.0%. However, buyers with stronger credit profiles and savings may benefit from conventional loans to avoid mortgage insurance premiums, despite the higher upfront capital requirement. The primary practical takeaway is that while Shelby County’s low median home value provides a strong foundation for affordability, the current 7.40% interest rate is the dominant factor in monthly costs. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing the lowest possible rate, as even a 0.5% reduction would lower the median FHA payment by over $30 per month, improving long-term cash flow stability without altering the fundamental purchase price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Shelby County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Shelby County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Shelby County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Shelby County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Shelby County qualifies.
