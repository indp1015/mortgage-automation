📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price in Aransas County by more than 200%, confirming that the typical residence is fully within FHA eligibility. The conforming conventional limit of $832,750 therefore covers more than three times the median home price, underscoring robust financing coverage for most buyers. The median‑priced home requires a loan of $256,111 for a 3.5% down payment, resulting in a P&I payment of $1,773 per month, which equals 30.6% of the median household income of $69,466. A conventional 20% down payment reduces the payment to $1,470, or 25.4% of income, indicating that lower down payments increase the affordability burden. Saving $9,300 for the 3.5% down payment represents roughly 13% of the median annual income, a manageable but non‑trivial target for most households, whereas the 20% down amount of $53,080 equals about 76% of annual income and is generally unattainable without additional assets or assistance. Given the current 7.40% rate, the monthly payment consumes nearly a third of income; a drop to 6% would lower the FHA payment to approximately $1,500, reducing the income share to 26% and enhancing affordability. While VA and USDA programs are not detailed in the data, they could provide alternative low‑down‑payment options for eligible buyers. Consequently, FHA financing is likely the most suitable option for typical buyers, as it requires the smallest down payment and aligns with the median income share. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for at least a 3.5% down payment and monitor rate fluctuations, as even modest reductions can improve monthly affordability and expand purchasing power.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Aransas County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Aransas County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Aransas County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Aransas County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Aransas County qualifies.
