📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a single‑family unit is more than **193 %** of Mohave County’s median home price of $281,000, while the conforming ceiling of $832,750 reaches **296 %** of that price. Both limits therefore comfortably exceed the typical purchase price, eliminating loan‑size constraints for the average buyer; the primary financing consideration is the borrower’s ability to meet down‑payment and debt‑service requirements rather than ceiling caps. At a 30‑year fixed rate of **7.18 %**, a median‑priced home financed with an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $271,165 loan) generates a principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment of **$1,836 per month**. This represents **38.2 %** of the median household income ($57,684 annually or $4,807 monthly). A conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($224,800 loan) reduces P&I to **$1,522 per month**, or **31.7 %** of monthly income. Both ratios exceed the traditional 28 % affordability threshold, indicating a moderate payment burden for the median household, especially for borrowers with limited cash reserves. Given the price‑to‑limit relationship and the high P&I share of income, conventional financing with a 20 % down payment offers the most cost‑effective path because it eliminates mortgage‑insurance premiums and yields the lowest monthly outlay. FHA financing remains viable for buyers who cannot amass a 20 % down payment, but the added insurance cost pushes the payment burden above 38 % of income. VA or USDA loans could be attractive for eligible veterans or rural‑area purchasers, as they require no down payment and typically lower insurance costs, further improving affordability. The minimum cash required to purchase at the median price is **$9,835** (3.5 % FHA) or **$28,100** (10 % conventional). For a median‑income household, the 3.5 % down payment is more attainable, yet the resulting payment‑to‑income ratio remains high. The 7.18 % rate inflates monthly obligations by roughly **$300–$400** compared with a 5 % environment, underscoring the sensitivity of affordability to rate movements. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a 20 % down payment to secure a conventional loan, which reduces monthly debt service to below one‑third of household income and avoids the additional FHA insurance expense that otherwise strains affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Mohave County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Mohave County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Mohave County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Mohave County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Mohave County qualifies.
