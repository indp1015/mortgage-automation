📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Kendall County’s housing market is well-supported by current federal loan limits, ensuring that typical buyers do not face financing ceilings. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 168% of the median home value of $322,400, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 258%. Consequently, the median-priced property falls comfortably within both standard financing tiers, eliminating the need for jumbo loans for the average resident. This robust coverage indicates that regulatory limits are not a primary barrier to entry for the typical household in this region. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though they represent a significant portion of disposable income. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment on an FHA loan with 3.5% down is $2,154. This figure equates to 23.2% of the median household income of $111,601. For conventional buyers putting down 20%, the payment drops to $1,785, or 19.2% of income. While these ratios are generally within acceptable lending guidelines, they leave less room for other expenses compared to lower-rate environments. The substantial price appreciation, including a 12.8% jump in 2022 and 7.9% in 2024, has increased the absolute cost of entry. The minimum down payment for an FHA loan at the median price is approximately $11,284, a feasible threshold for many households given the high owner-occupancy rate of 84.0%. Regarding loan type recommendations, conventional financing is likely the optimal choice for buyers with sufficient savings, as it avoids FHA mortgage insurance premiums and offers lower total costs over time. However, for first-time buyers or those with limited cash reserves, the FHA option provides a viable path with a low down payment requirement. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability; had rates been lower, the monthly payment burden would have decreased proportionally, potentially improving debt-to-income ratios. Prospective buyers should note that the high 5-year price CAGR of 8.5% suggests a competitive market where waiting may result in higher entry costs. A concrete insight for buyers is that securing a pre-approval for a conventional loan with a 20% down payment, totaling $64,480, offers the most financial stability and lowest monthly obligation, provided the buyer has the liquidity to cover this upfront cost without depleting emergency reserves.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Kendall County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Kendall County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Kendall County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Kendall County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Kendall County qualifies.
