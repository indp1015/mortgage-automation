📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a single‑unit property is **186 % of the median home price** ($291,300), while the conforming limit of $832,750 is **286 % of the median price**. Both limits comfortably exceed the typical purchase price, confirming that financing caps are not a constraint for most buyers in Pueblo County; the median home sits well within the FHA ceiling and far below the conforming ceiling. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $1,915 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down) and $1,588 for a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment. With a median household income of $64,010, the monthly income is $5,334. The FHA payment represents **35.9 % of monthly income**, while the conventional 20 % payment is **29.8 %**. Both ratios exceed the traditional 28 % affordability threshold, indicating a moderate to high payment burden for median earners, especially for borrowers who cannot achieve a 20 % down payment. For typical buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment yields the lowest P&I burden and stays below the 30 % income‑to‑payment guideline, making it the most suitable option when sufficient cash is available. FHA financing remains viable for buyers with limited savings, but the higher payment‑to‑income ratio (35.9 %) may limit discretionary cash flow. VA or USDA loans could be considered for eligible veterans or rural‑area purchasers, but the median home already falls well within FHA limits, reducing the need for these niche programs. The minimum cash required to purchase at the median price is $10,196 (3.5 % down for FHA) or $29,130 (10 % down conventional). The FHA down‑payment represents **3.5 % of the purchase price**, a level that many first‑time buyers can accumulate, whereas the conventional 20 % down payment ($58,260) is substantially higher and may be a barrier for a sizable portion of the market. A 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $200–$300 compared with a 5 % environment, compressing affordability margins. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a larger down payment to reduce loan size and mitigate the impact of elevated rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Pueblo County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Pueblo County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Pueblo County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Pueblo County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Pueblo County qualifies.
