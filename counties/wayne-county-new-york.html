📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Wayne County’s housing market exhibits robust financing coverage, as the 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 318% of the median home value of $170,000, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 490%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this county faces loan limit constraints. The primary financial challenge stems from interest rates rather than loan caps. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the monthly principal and interest (P&I) for an FHA loan on the median-priced home is approximately $1,135. This amount represents 18.3% of the median household income of $74,506, a ratio that remains within the 28% threshold generally considered affordable by underwriting standards. However, this calculation excludes taxes, insurance, and potential mortgage insurance premiums, which would increase the total housing expense. For conventional loans with a 20% down payment, the P&I drops to $941, or 15.2% of income, offering a more comfortable margin for additional housing costs. Loan type selection depends heavily on down payment capacity. The FHA option requires a minimum down payment of $5,950 (3.5% of $170,000), which is feasible for many local households given the median income level. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down requires $34,000, a significant barrier for first-time buyers in this market. Given the low entry cost and adequate coverage, FHA loans are likely the most accessible vehicle for typical Wayne County buyers, particularly those with limited savings. VA and USDA loans are also viable options for eligible candidates, potentially reducing out-of-pocket costs further if the property meets rural or military eligibility criteria. Rate sensitivity significantly impacts affordability. The current 7.40% rate is notably higher than the sub-4% environments seen in previous years, effectively increasing the monthly payment burden by nearly 50% compared to those historical lows. This rate environment compresses the price range buyers can afford; for instance, a buyer with a $2,000 monthly housing budget can purchase a home valued approximately $25,000 less than in a 4% rate environment. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that while the county’s low median home price of $170,000 remains attractive, the high interest rate necessitates careful budgeting. Buyers should prioritize maximizing down payments to reduce loan principal and mitigate the impact of high rates, or consider fixed-rate strategies to lock in current terms before potential market shifts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Wayne County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Wayne County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Wayne County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Wayne County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Wayne County qualifies.
