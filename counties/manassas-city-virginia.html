Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Manassas city?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Manassas city is $811,931. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Manassas city?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Manassas city?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Manassas city qualifies.
