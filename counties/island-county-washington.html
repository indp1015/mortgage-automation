Mortgage Guide: Island County, Washington

📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Island County is limited for FHA borrowers, whose 1‑Unit limit of $541,287 represents only 91 % of the 2024 median home value of $593,300. Consequently, most median‑priced buyers will exceed the FHA cap and will need a conforming loan, whose 1‑Unit limit of $832,750 covers 140 % of the median price. This makes the conforming loan the only standard program that fully accommodates typical purchases without requiring a jumbo mortgage. Monthly payment burden is considerable. A conventional loan with 10 % down on the median home (loan amount $533,970) carries a principal‑and‑interest payment of $3,697 per month at the current 7.40 % rate. This amount equals 48.7 % of the median household income of $90,551 (monthly $7,546). The FHA scenario (3.5 % down, $572,534 loan) yields a $3,964 payment, or 52.5 % of income, while a 20 % down conventional loan ($474,640) reduces the payment to $3,286, or 43.5 % of income. Thus, even with a 20 % down payment, the median buyer allocates nearly half of their monthly earnings to housing costs. Given the limits and payment levels, a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment is the most practical fit for typical buyers, as it respects the conforming limit and keeps the payment near the upper bound of affordability. The required down payment—$59,300 for 10 % or $118,700 for 20 %—poses a significant barrier, especially for first‑time purchasers. At 7.40 %, affordability is markedly lower than at rates below 6 %, where the same loan would drop by roughly $400–$600 per month, highlighting the impact of interest‑rate changes on budgeting. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a 10 % down payment and securing a conforming conventional loan to align financing with market limits and maintain a manageable debt‑to‑income ratio.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Island County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Island County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Island County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Island County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Island County qualifies.
