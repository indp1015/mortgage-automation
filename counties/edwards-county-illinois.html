📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Edwards County’s housing market remains affordable relative to federal loan limits. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $100,000 by 541 %, while the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 833 %. Both limits comfortably cover the typical home, eliminating financing‑coverage constraints for most buyers. Monthly payment burdens are moderate but notable. The FHA estimate of $653 per month for a $96,500 loan (3.5 % down) represents 12.9 % of the median household income of $60,519, or roughly $5,043 monthly. A conventional loan with 20 % equity ($80,000 loan) reduces the payment to $541, which is 10.7 % of monthly income. The 10 % down scenario ($90,000 loan) yields $609, or about 11.9 % of income. These ratios fall within acceptable debt‑to‑income thresholds, though the FHA payment approaches the upper edge of typical affordability guidelines. Given the low price point and the FHA’s minimal down‑payment requirement, FHA financing is likely the best fit for typical Edwards County buyers. Conventional loans are viable for borrowers who can muster a larger cash reserve, while VA or USDA programs could further reduce costs for eligible veterans or rural borrowers, but FHA remains the most accessible. The minimum down payment to acquire a median‑priced home is $3,500 (3.5 % of $100,000) under FHA terms. This amount is well within reach for a household earning $60,519, representing roughly two months of gross income after typical living expenses. The current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 % elevates monthly obligations compared with historic lows (e.g., 5 %). At a 5 % rate, the FHA payment would drop to roughly $570, improving affordability by about $83 per month. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing financing before rates rise further, as the higher cost environment compresses purchasing power. **Practical takeaway:** With a median price of $100,000, FHA financing offers the lowest entry barrier at a $3,500 down payment, but the 7.18 % rate adds roughly $83 to monthly costs versus a 5 % scenario, making early rate locking essential for optimal affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Edwards County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Edwards County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Edwards County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Edwards County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Edwards County qualifies.
