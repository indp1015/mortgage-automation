📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Grayson County, Kentucky, presents a distinct contrast between robust loan limits and tightening affordability metrics. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 329% of the median home value of $164,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 506%. Consequently, loan limit constraints are not a barrier for typical buyers in this market. The primary challenge lies in monthly payment burdens relative to local income levels. With a median household income of $50,757, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan is $1,099, representing 26.0% of monthly income. This figure sits at the upper threshold of conventional underwriting guidelines, which typically cap housing expenses at 28-31%. In comparison, a conventional loan with 20% down requires a $911 payment, or 21.5% of income, offering a more comfortable margin but demanding significantly higher upfront capital. Given the local price points and income levels, FHA financing is likely the most accessible option for the majority of prospective buyers. The 3.5% down payment requirement translates to approximately $5,761 for a median-priced home, a feasible barrier for many households compared to the $32,920 required for a 20% conventional down payment. While USDA loans may also be an option depending on specific rural eligibility criteria, the FHA program offers broader availability and lower entry costs. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts affordability. Compared to lower-rate environments, this rate increases monthly payments by hundreds of dollars, effectively reducing the purchasing power of the median household. For instance, a 100 basis point drop in rates would lower the FHA payment by approximately $75, directly improving the debt-to-income ratio for marginal buyers. A critical practical takeaway is that while the median home value of $164,600 is well within standard loan limits, the 10.0% five-year price CAGR has outpaced income growth. Buyers must carefully evaluate their debt-to-income ratios, as the 26.0% payment-to-income ratio for FHA loans leaves little room for other monthly obligations. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a 3.5% down payment and consider FHA loans to minimize initial cash requirements. Additionally, locking in rates promptly is advisable, as the current 7.40% environment makes monthly payment predictability essential for long-term financial stability. The market supports ownership through accessible loan products, but affordability hinges on managing the elevated cost of borrowing relative to the $50,757 median household income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Grayson County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Grayson County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Grayson County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Grayson County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Grayson County qualifies.
