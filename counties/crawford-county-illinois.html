📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Crawford County’s housing market exhibits strong alignment between local property values and federal financing limits. The 2026 FHA limit for a one-unit property is $541,287, which covers 447% of the median home value of $121,100. Similarly, the conforming loan limit of $832,750 covers 688% of the median price. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market will encounter loan limit restrictions, ensuring that standard financing products are fully accessible without requiring jumbo loans or high-cost county adjustments. This ample coverage provides significant flexibility for buyers to finance homes well above the median without facing regulatory caps. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on the median home is $809. This represents 13.5% of the median household income of $71,674, a figure that generally falls within prudent debt-to-income ratios. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers the monthly payment to $670, or 11.2% of income. These figures indicate that the current interest rate environment, while higher than historical lows, does not render median-priced homes unaffordable for typical households. The 7.9% five-year price CAGR has increased entry costs, but the stable income base supports continued purchasing power. FHA financing is likely the most suitable option for the typical Crawford County buyer due to its lower down payment requirement of 3.5%. This translates to a minimum cash outlay of approximately $4,239 for the median home, a barrier that is more feasible than the $12,110 required for a 10% conventional down payment or $24,220 for 20%. While USDA loans may be available given the rural nature of the county, the FHA’s broad eligibility and competitive coverage make it a primary contender. The rate sensitivity is notable; a 7.40% rate increases monthly costs by roughly $150 compared to a hypothetical 6.0% rate, yet the payment-to-income ratio remains healthy. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing pre-approval with an FHA lender to leverage the low down payment threshold, as this structure minimizes initial capital requirements while maintaining a sustainable monthly obligation relative to local earnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Crawford County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Crawford County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Crawford County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Crawford County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Crawford County qualifies.
