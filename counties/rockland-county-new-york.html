📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Rockland County’s median home value of $596,900 exceeds the 2026 FHA limit for single-family homes, which stands at $541,287. Consequently, the FHA program covers only 91% of the typical local property value, forcing most buyers to seek conventional financing. The conforming loan limit of $1,209,750 covers 203% of the median price, ensuring that standard conventional loans remain the primary vehicle for purchasing homes at the market median. Because the county is not designated as high-cost, no special adjustments apply, and the gap between the FHA ceiling and the local median represents a significant structural barrier for low-down-payment buyers. The monthly payment burden is substantial relative to local earnings. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the principal and interest payment for a conventional loan with a 20% down payment on a median-priced home is $3,306. This amount consumes 36.1% of the median household income of $109,959. For buyers utilizing an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment on a property near the limit, the payment rises to $3,988, equating to 43.5% of median income. These figures exceed the traditional 28% front-end debt-to-income ratio guideline, indicating that typical households face tight cash flow constraints. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly amplifies this burden; compared to lower-rate environments, the higher cost of debt reduces the maximum affordable home price by a considerable margin, further narrowing the pool of eligible buyers. Conventional financing is the most appropriate recommendation for typical Rockland County buyers, as it aligns with the conforming limit and offers flexibility in down payment structures. The minimum down payment for a conventional loan at the median price is $59,690 for a 10% down scenario, or $119,380 for a 20% down scenario. While a 10% down payment is more accessible, it still requires a significant capital reserve. The 20% down payment avoids private mortgage insurance, which is critical given the elevated monthly obligations. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a minimum 10% down payment to enter the market, while recognizing that the high interest rate environment necessitates a robust income buffer to meet debt service requirements without compromising other financial obligations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Rockland County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Rockland County is $786,337. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Rockland County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,209,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Rockland County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Rockland County qualifies.
