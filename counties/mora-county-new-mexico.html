📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Mora County exhibits a high degree of financing coverage, with the 2026 FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 covering 442% of the median home value of $122,600. The conforming loan limit provides even broader coverage at 679%. Consequently, the typical home in this market is well within the thresholds for all primary government-backed and conventional loan products, ensuring that financing availability is not restricted by loan ceilings. The monthly payment burden remains relatively low compared to median household income. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan results in a principal and interest (P&I) payment of $819, representing 18.3% of the median monthly household income of $4,472. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this burden to 15.2% ($679/month). However, the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% increases the cost of borrowing compared to historical lows, tightening the monthly budget for lower-income households despite the low median home price. Given the rural nature of the county and the low median home value, USDA loans are likely the most optimal fit for eligible buyers, as they often provide 0% down payment options. For others, the FHA minimum down payment of 3.5% requires approximately $4,291, which is a feasible barrier for most residents. Conventional 20% down payments require $24,520, which may be prohibitive for a significant portion of the population. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that while the median home price is highly accessible relative to federal loan limits, the primary challenge is the current interest rate environment. Despite a low price-to-income ratio, buyers should prioritize low-down-payment programs like USDA or FHA to preserve liquidity, as the P&I payment for an FHA loan is nearly equivalent to the median monthly rent of $887, making ownership a viable alternative to renting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Mora County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Mora County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Mora County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Mora County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Mora County qualifies.
