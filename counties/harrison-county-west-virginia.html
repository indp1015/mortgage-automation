📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750 both exceed the median home value of $166,200, covering 326% and 501% respectively, so financing coverage is adequate for typical buyers. Given the price point and down‑payment requirements, FHA financing is the most suitable option for the typical buyer. A 3.5% down payment on a $166,200 home equals roughly $5,823, which is more attainable than the 10% ($16,620) or 20% ($33,240) down payments required for conventional loans. VA and USDA programs are not indicated in the data, but the low down‑payment requirement makes FHA the practical choice. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing to minimize upfront cash needs while staying within the county’s loan limits, and monitor rate movements to gauge monthly payment affordability. This approach ensures buyers can enter the market without excessive cash outlay while maintaining manageable debt ratios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Harrison County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Harrison County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Harrison County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Harrison County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Harrison County qualifies.
