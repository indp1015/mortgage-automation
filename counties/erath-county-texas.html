📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Erath County, Texas, presents a favorable financing landscape where loan limits significantly exceed local median home values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 201% of the $269,100 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 309%. This substantial coverage ensures that virtually all standard residential properties in the county fall well within federal loan parameters, eliminating price-based restrictions for typical buyers. Consequently, the choice of loan type is driven less by limit constraints and more by down payment capacity and credit profiles, with both FHA and conventional options remaining fully accessible for median-priced homes. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to the local median household income of $65,115. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down requires a principal and interest payment of $1,797, representing 33.1% of monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $1,490, or 27.5% of monthly income. While the FHA ratio exceeds the traditional 28% debt-to-income guideline, it remains within the 31% maximum often accepted by lenders when other debts are minimal. The conventional option offers a safer margin for borrowers with additional liabilities. Regarding down payment barriers, the minimum FHA requirement is approximately $9,419, which is feasible for qualified buyers. In contrast, the conventional 20% down payment of $53,820 represents a significant capital hurdle, making FHA or low-down-payment conventional products the most practical entry points for the average household. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical low-rate environments. A 100 basis point decrease would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $118, highlighting the sensitivity of fixed-rate mortgages to rate fluctuations. For prospective buyers, the primary strategic insight is that the low median home value of $269,100 provides a buffer against high interest rates. Because the absolute loan amounts are modest, the monthly cash flow impact remains contained even at elevated rates. Buyers should prioritize securing pre-approval for FHA or low-down conventional loans, as the combination of accessible loan limits and moderate home prices allows for manageable entry costs despite the current rate environment. This structural advantage positions Erath County as a viable market for first-time and move-up buyers who can navigate the initial down payment requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Erath County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Erath County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Erath County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Erath County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Erath County qualifies.
