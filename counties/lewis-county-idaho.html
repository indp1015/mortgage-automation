📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price in Lewis County. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 is 240 % of the median value ($225,300), while the conforming ceiling of $832,750 is 370 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that meets the purchase price without seeking a high‑cost or jumbo product. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, a median‑priced home financed with a conventional loan and a 20 % down payment ($180,240 loan) produces a principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment of $1,221 per month. This represents 28.4 % of the median household income ($51,615 annual or $4,301 monthly). An FHA loan with a 3.5 % down payment ($217,414 loan) yields a $1,472 P&I payment, or 34.2 % of monthly income. Both ratios sit below the conventional 28‑30 % affordability threshold for P&I alone, but when property taxes, insurance, and utilities are added, total housing costs will likely approach or exceed the 30 % benchmark, especially for the FHA scenario. Given the ample loan limits, conventional financing with a 10‑20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option for typical buyers. A 10 % down payment ($22,530) reduces the loan to $202,770 and the P&I to $1,373 (31.9 % of income), still acceptable for many households, while avoiding the mortgage‑insurance premium required on FHA loans. USDA eligibility may apply for rural parcels, offering zero‑down options, but the median home price already falls well within conventional limits, making USDA less critical. The 7.18 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $150–$200 compared with a 5 % environment, tightening affordability margins and underscoring the importance of a larger down payment to offset higher interest costs. **Practical takeaway:** securing at least a 10 % down payment keeps total housing costs near the conventional affordability ceiling and eliminates the added expense of FHA mortgage insurance, making conventional loans the preferred pathway for most Lewis County homebuyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Lewis County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Lewis County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Lewis County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Lewis County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Lewis County qualifies.
