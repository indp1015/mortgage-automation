📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Accomack County’s housing market exhibits strong financing coverage, with the 2026 FHA loan limit of $541,287 covering 265% of the median home value of $204,300. The conforming limit of $832,750 extends even further, covering 408% of the median price. Consequently, no high-cost county adjustments are necessary, and standard loan limits provide ample headroom for typical transactions. This structural adequacy ensures that the vast majority of local properties fall well below regulatory caps, allowing buyers to access standard financing products without requiring jumbo loans or specialized high-limit programs. The primary constraint for affordability is the current 7.40% interest rate, which significantly elevates monthly payment burdens relative to local income levels. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest (P&I) payment of $1,365, consuming 27.8% of the median household income of $58,993. While this exceeds the recommended 28% debt-to-income threshold when including taxes and insurance, it remains within the maximum 31% limit for many FHA applicants. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces the P&I to $1,131, or 23.0% of income, offering a more sustainable payment structure. However, the required down payment of $40,860 represents a substantial capital barrier. In contrast, the FHA minimum down payment of $7,150 is more accessible, though the higher monthly cost due to mortgage insurance premiums must be weighed against the initial cash outlay. Given the local price point and income profile, FHA financing is likely the most suitable option for typical buyers, as it minimizes upfront capital requirements while keeping loan amounts well within limits. The 5.4% five-year price CAGR suggests a steady appreciation trend, but the current high-rate environment dampens immediate affordability. Prospective buyers should note that while the median home value is low compared to national averages, the ratio of housing costs to income remains sensitive to interest rate fluctuations. A concrete insight for buyers is that securing a lower down payment option, such as FHA, is critical, but they must budget for the full monthly obligation, including property taxes and insurance, which will likely push the total housing expense above 30% of gross income, necessitating careful debt management.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Accomack County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Accomack County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Accomack County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Accomack County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Accomack County qualifies.
