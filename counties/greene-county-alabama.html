📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Greene County’s housing market presents a distinct affordability profile where loan limits vastly exceed typical property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 667% of the $81,200 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 1026%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces financing constraints due to loan caps. The primary financial challenge is not loan eligibility but the ratio of monthly debt service to household income. With a median household income of $29,200, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan at the median price is $542 per month. This represents 22.3% of monthly income, which aligns with standard debt-to-income guidelines but leaves limited margin for property taxes, insurance, and maintenance. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the P&I to $449, or 18.5% of monthly income, offering greater cash flow stability. Given the low median home price, FHA financing is likely the most accessible option for typical buyers. The requirement for only 3.5% down payment translates to a minimum cash outlay of approximately $2,842, a figure that is significantly more attainable than the $16,240 required for a 20% conventional down payment. While USDA loans may also be viable given the rural nature of the county, the FHA program offers broader lender participation and flexibility for buyers with moderate credit profiles. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lower-rate environments. At this rate, the monthly payment burden is elevated; had rates been 5.00%, the FHA payment would have been approximately $448, reducing the income percentage to 18.3%. This rate sensitivity underscores that even in a low-price market, high interest rates can erode disposable income. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that while the home itself is inexpensive, the total cost of ownership remains sensitive to interest rate fluctuations. Buyers should focus on securing the lowest possible rate and consider FHA financing to minimize upfront capital requirements. With the median home value well below all loan limits, the decision should be driven by down payment availability and credit score rather than loan cap restrictions. The 3.7% five-year price CAGR suggests a stable, slowly appreciating market, allowing buyers to prioritize long-term payment affordability over short-term equity gains. Ultimately, the feasibility of homeownership in Greene County hinges on managing the 22.3% payment-to-income ratio effectively, ensuring that total housing costs do not exceed 30% of gross income when taxes and insurance are included.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Greene County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Greene County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Greene County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Greene County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Greene County qualifies.
