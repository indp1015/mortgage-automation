📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is adequate: the FHA 1‑Unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price of $237,400 by 228%, and the conforming 1‑Unit limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 351%, confirming that both programs can underwrite typical purchases in Grimes County. Monthly payment burden is significant. With a median household income of $69,803 (≈$5,817 per month), the FHA‑insured loan requiring a $229,091 principal at 3.5% down results in a P&I payment of $1,586, consuming 27.3% of monthly income. The conventional 20%‑down loan on a $189,920 principal at the same rate yields $1,314 per month, or 22.6% of income. These figures indicate that even with low down payments, housing costs approach or exceed the 30% affordability threshold. Given the median price and available limits, an FHA loan is likely the best fit for typical buyers, as it permits a 3.5% down payment, which reduces upfront cash needs. Conventional loans require higher down payments, making them less accessible for first‑time purchasers. VA and USDA options are not referenced in the data, so FHA remains the most practical choice. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5% ($8,307) for FHA, 10% ($23,740) for conventional, and 20% ($47,480) for a conventional loan with 20% equity. A 3.5% down payment is potentially feasible with savings or assistance programs, whereas 10% and 20% present considerable barriers for many households. Current 7.40% interest rates increase monthly obligations compared with lower‑rate environments; a 5% rate would lower the FHA payment to roughly $1,200, reducing the income share to about 20.5% and improving affordability. The elevated rate therefore tightens budget constraints for median‑income buyers. Practical takeaway: Prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing to minimize down payment and keep housing costs near the 30% income guideline, while actively saving for the modest $8,300 down payment required at the median price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Grimes County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Grimes County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Grimes County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Grimes County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Grimes County qualifies.
