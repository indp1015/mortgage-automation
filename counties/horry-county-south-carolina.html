📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Horry County’s housing finance landscape is characterized by robust loan limit coverage, which effectively decouples typical transactions from federal lending caps. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 188% of the $287,700 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 289%. Consequently, the vast majority of buyers in this market will not encounter high-cost or jumbo loan restrictions, allowing for standard financing structures. This ample coverage ensures that the median-priced property remains well within the reach of standard government-backed and conventional loan programs, facilitating liquidity in the secondary market. Despite favorable loan limits, the current interest rate environment of 7.40% significantly impacts monthly affordability. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down requires a principal and interest payment of $1,922, consuming 34.5% of the median household income of $66,880. This ratio exceeds the conventional 30% debt-to-income guideline, suggesting that typical buyers may face strain unless they have lower existing debts or higher individual incomes. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $1,593, or 28.6% of median income, bringing the burden into a more sustainable range. However, this requires a substantial upfront capital outlay of $57,540. The minimum down payment for an FHA purchase is $10,070, which is more accessible but results in higher monthly obligations and mandatory mortgage insurance premiums. Given the rapid price appreciation, with a five-year CAGR of 11.6%, buyers must weigh the trade-off between entry barriers and ongoing costs. The 18.0% price surge in 2022 and continued double-digit growth through 2024 indicate a market where waiting may exacerbate affordability challenges. For typical buyers, conventional financing is likely the optimal path if they can accumulate a 20% down payment, as it minimizes monthly outflows and avoids FHA mortgage insurance. If down payment savings are limited, FHA loans provide a viable entry point, though the 34.5% payment-to-income ratio requires careful budgeting. A concrete takeaway for prospective buyers is that securing a 20% down payment is the most effective strategy to mitigate the impact of the 7.40% interest rate, reducing the monthly payment burden to a level that aligns with standard affordability metrics for the local median income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Horry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Horry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Horry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Horry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Horry County qualifies.
