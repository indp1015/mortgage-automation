📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a one‑unit property exceeds the median home value of $157,600 by 343%, confirming that the typical residence in Atoka County is fully covered by FHA financing. The conforming limit of $832,750 provides an even larger cushion, well beyond the median price. The median household income of $54,785 translates to a monthly income of $4,565. The FHA‑insured loan for the median‑priced home, requiring a 3.5% down payment and a $152,084 principal, generates a P&I payment of $1,052, representing 23.0% of monthly income. Conventional financing with 20% down yields a $126,080 loan and a $872 payment, equal to 19.1% of income. Given the availability of FHA financing that meets the median price, the 3.5% down payment requirement, and the moderate payment burden, FHA loans are likely the most appropriate for typical buyers. Conventional loans remain viable for those able to meet higher down payments, while VA and USDA options are not indicated by the data. To purchase at the median price, a buyer needs $5,516 for an FHA loan (3.5% down) or $15,760 for a 10% conventional down payment, and $31,520 for a 20% conventional loan; the 3.5% amount, while modest, may still pose a barrier for households with limited savings. At the current 7.40% interest rate, the FHA payment consumes nearly a quarter of median monthly income, exceeding the conventional 30% affordability guideline. A lower rate such as 5% would reduce the payment to approximately $840, decreasing the income share to about 18%. Prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing with the minimum 3.5% down payment to keep monthly costs within a manageable share of income, aligning with the county’s median home value and income levels. This approach balances affordability with the ability to leverage low down payment options available in the local market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Atoka County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Atoka County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Atoka County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Atoka County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Atoka County qualifies.
