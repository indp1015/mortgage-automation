📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Kaufman County’s housing market remains well within federal financing parameters, ensuring broad accessibility for standard purchase transactions. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 169% of the median home value of $320,900, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 260%. Consequently, no borrower purchasing at the median price faces loan limit restrictions, allowing full utilization of both government-backed and conventional financing options. This ample coverage indicates that financing availability is not a structural barrier to entry for typical buyers in this region. Monthly payment burdens present a moderate challenge relative to local income levels. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan on a median-priced home requires a minimum down payment of $11,212 (3.5%), resulting in a principal and interest payment of $2,144. This amount consumes 28.8% of the median household income of $89,485. While this ratio is generally considered manageable, it leaves less margin for property taxes, insurance, and maintenance compared to conventional scenarios. A conventional loan with 20% down requires a $64,180 upfront investment, reducing the monthly P&I to $1,777, or 23.8% of income. The significant difference in down payment requirements highlights the trade-off between initial capital outlay and long-term monthly obligations. Given the current market dynamics, conventional financing is likely the optimal fit for buyers with sufficient savings, as it avoids mortgage insurance premiums associated with FHA loans. However, for first-time buyers with limited capital, the FHA option remains viable due to the low barrier to entry. The 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a reduction to 6.00% would lower the FHA monthly payment by approximately $300, substantially improving cash flow. This rate sensitivity underscores that current affordability is tightly linked to interest rate fluctuations rather than just home prices. The 15.8% five-year price CAGR has outpaced income growth, further compressing affordability margins. Prospective buyers should prioritize building a down payment of at least 10% to access conventional rates without private mortgage insurance, which can improve long-term equity accumulation. At the median price, a 10% down payment of $32,090 reduces the loan to $288,810, yielding a $1,999 monthly P&I. This strategy balances upfront cost with manageable monthly outflows, positioning buyers to withstand potential rate volatility. Ultimately, the combination of high loan limits and moderate income levels suggests that while affordability is tighter than in previous years, it remains accessible for qualified borrowers who carefully manage their debt-to-income ratios and select appropriate loan structures.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Kaufman County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Kaufman County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Kaufman County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Kaufman County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Kaufman County qualifies.
