📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Presidio County is robust. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $121,300 by 446%, and the conforming loan limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 687%, confirming that both programs can fully underwrite a typical purchase. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home at the current 7.40% rate show a 3.5% down‑payment FHA loan requiring $810 in principal and interest, which equals 22.2% of the median household’s monthly income of $3,650. A conventional loan with 20% down, by contrast, demands $671 per month, or 18.4% of income, indicating a modest but manageable burden for most buyers. The conforming loan limit of $832,750 also fully encompasses the median home price, representing 687% of the median value, while the FHA limit, though lower in absolute terms, still provides more than four times the needed financing. Although VA and USDA programs are not specified in the data, the low down‑payment requirement of FHA makes it the most accessible option for the typical buyer. Given the price level and available limits, an FHA loan is likely the optimal choice for typical purchasers, as it permits a low down payment and aligns with the median home value. The minimum down payment required to buy at the median price is 3.5% ($4,246), which is generally more attainable than the 10% ($12,130) or 20% ($24,260) thresholds for conventional financing. However, the present 7.40% interest rate heightens the payment burden; lowering the rate to 5% would reduce the FHA payment to roughly $610, improving affordability. Prospective buyers should therefore prioritize securing an FHA loan with the smallest feasible down payment to mitigate the impact of high rates and rapid price appreciation, which has driven the 5‑year CAGR to 19.1%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Presidio County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Presidio County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Presidio County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Presidio County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Presidio County qualifies.
