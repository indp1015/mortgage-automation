📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Dubuque County, Iowa, demonstrates robust coverage for the typical homeowner. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 228% of the median home value of $237,300, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 351%. Consequently, the median-priced property falls well within both standard loan categories, ensuring that no buyer faces a high-balance loan restriction. This ample coverage means that financing availability is not a primary barrier to entry for the average household in the region. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,585, which represents 24.5% of the median household income of $77,630. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this figure to $1,314, or 20.3% of income. Both scenarios align with standard underwriting guidelines, indicating that the majority of local households can qualify for financing without exceeding typical debt-to-income thresholds. Given that the median home price is significantly below the FHA limit, the FHA loan is likely the most accessible option for first-time buyers due to its lower down payment requirement and flexible credit criteria. While conventional loans offer lower total interest costs, the $23,730 required for a 10% down payment or $47,460 for 20% down presents a higher initial capital barrier than the $8,306 required for an FHA loan. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% exerts noticeable pressure on affordability compared to historical low-rate environments. At this rate, the monthly payment for a median home is substantially higher than it would be at rates below 5%, directly impacting the percentage of income allocated to housing. However, the 5-year price appreciation of 8.6% annually has outpaced some rate increases, suggesting that while monthly costs have risen, asset values have also increased. Prospective buyers should note that the minimum down payment of $8,306 for an FHA loan is feasible for many households, particularly given the stable owner-occupancy rate of 73.3%. The primary practical takeaway is that while interest rates are elevated, the low median home price relative to national standards and sufficient loan limits preserve strong affordability metrics for Dubuque County residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Dubuque County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Dubuque County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Dubuque County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Dubuque County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Dubuque County qualifies.
