Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Butts County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Butts County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Butts County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Butts County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Butts County qualifies.
