📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Ketchikan Gateway Borough presents a distinct disconnect between loan limits and local home values. The 2026 FHA limit of $811,931 covers 206% of the median home value of $393,300, while the conforming limit of $1,249,125 covers 318%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market will encounter loan limit restrictions, as both government-backed and conventional options provide ample headroom above the median price. This abundance of limit coverage ensures that financing availability is not a barrier to entry for standard residential purchases, allowing buyers to focus primarily on creditworthiness and income verification rather than loan cap constraints. Monthly payment burdens remain the primary affordability challenge in this high-cost county. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on the median-priced home is $2,627, representing 33.9% of the median household income of $92,885. This ratio exceeds the standard 28% debt-to-income threshold often preferred by lenders, though it falls within the maximum 43% total debt-to-income limit for many FHA loans. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the P&I to $2,178, or 28.1% of income, aligning more closely with conventional underwriting preferences. The 2022 price surge of 14.5% significantly compressed affordability, and while growth has moderated to 3.8% in 2024, the baseline costs remain elevated relative to regional incomes. Regarding loan type recommendations, conventional financing is likely the most suitable option for buyers with at least 20% down payment due to lower overall costs and more favorable payment-to-income ratios. However, for buyers with limited savings, the FHA option remains viable, as the 3.5% down payment requirement on a $393,300 home is approximately $13,766. This amount is feasible for households saving consistently, though it represents a significant initial barrier compared to the median rent of $1,505. The high-cost county designation does not alter the standard loan limits but underscores the premium pricing environment. Prospective buyers should note that the current 7.40% rate significantly increases monthly obligations compared to lower-rate environments; a 100 basis point drop would reduce the FHA payment by approximately $150. The practical takeaway is that while loan limits are non-restrictive, the combination of high home values and elevated interest rates necessitates careful budgeting, with a 20% down payment being the most effective strategy to maintain a sustainable monthly debt load within the median income bracket.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Ketchikan Gateway Borough?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Ketchikan Gateway Borough is $811,931. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Ketchikan Gateway Borough?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Ketchikan Gateway Borough?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Ketchikan Gateway Borough qualifies.
