📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $208,400 in McLean County. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 is 260 % of the median price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 400 % of the median price, ensuring that virtually any buyer seeking a median‑priced property can obtain a loan without needing a high‑cost‑area waiver. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $1,362 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $201,106 loan) and $1,270 for a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment ($187,560 loan). With a median household income of $79,905, the monthly gross income is $6,658, making the FHA payment 20.5 % of income and the conventional‑10 % payment 19.1 % of income. A conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($166,720 loan) reduces the P&I to $1,129, or 17.0 % of income, well below the commonly cited 28 % affordability threshold for housing costs. For most first‑time or moderate‑income buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment offers the lowest monthly burden and avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums required on FHA loans. However, the $41,680 down payment (20 % of $208,400) may be a barrier for many households; the FHA’s 3.5 % requirement ($7,294) is far more attainable, albeit with higher monthly payments and insurance costs. The 7.18 % rate inflates monthly obligations by roughly $150–$200 compared with a 5 % environment, reducing purchasing power and emphasizing the importance of a larger down payment to offset interest costs. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers who can secure a 20 % down payment will achieve the most affordable monthly payment and avoid FHA insurance, but for those lacking that cash, the FHA program remains viable because the loan limit is far above median prices, though it carries a higher payment-to‑income ratio.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in McLean County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in McLean County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in McLean County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in McLean County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm McLean County qualifies.
