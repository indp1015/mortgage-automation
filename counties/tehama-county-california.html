📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Tehama County’s housing market demonstrates strong alignment between federal loan limits and local property values, ensuring broad accessibility for standard financing options. The 2026 FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 covers 159% of the median home value of $339,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 245%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this region faces loan limit constraints, as the median price sits comfortably below both thresholds. This ample coverage allows lenders to offer standard FHA and conventional products without requiring jumbo loans or high-cost county adjustments, which do not apply to Tehama County. However, the current interest rate environment significantly impacts monthly affordability. At a 30-year fixed rate of 7.32%, the principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on the median-priced home is approximately $2,251 per month. This amount represents 42.3% of the median household income of $63,784, a ratio that exceeds the conventional 28-36% debt-to-income guidelines for comfortable housing costs. Even with a 20% down payment on a conventional loan, the monthly payment of $1,866 still consumes 35.1% of median income. The rapid appreciation of home values, evidenced by a 12.2% five-year compound annual growth rate and a 19.2% jump in 2022, has outpaced income growth, tightening the affordability gap for median-income households. Given these dynamics, conventional financing with a 20% down payment offers the most manageable payment burden relative to income, though it requires a substantial upfront capital of $67,920. FHA loans, requiring only 3.5% down ($11,886), lower the entry barrier but result in a higher monthly outlay due to mortgage insurance and a larger loan principal. For buyers with limited savings, the FHA option is the most feasible path to ownership, provided they can secure supplemental income or reduce other debt obligations to meet lender requirements. The primary barrier is not the loan limit, but the high monthly payment relative to local wages. Prospective buyers should prioritize increasing their down payment to 10-20% to mitigate the impact of the 7.32% rate, as even modest increases in equity significantly reduce the principal balance and monthly P&I obligations. This strategy is essential for maintaining a sustainable debt-to-income ratio in a market where home values continue to rise faster than household earnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Tehama County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Tehama County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Tehama County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Tehama County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Tehama County qualifies.
