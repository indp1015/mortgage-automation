📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Bourbon County’s housing market exhibits strong financing coverage, as the median home value of $229,300 sits well below both the $541,287 FHA limit and the $832,750 conforming limit. The FHA ceiling covers 236% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 363%, ensuring that standard loan products are accessible for the typical property. Consequently, no high-cost county restrictions apply, and buyers face no structural barriers related to loan caps. This ample coverage means that financing availability is not the primary constraint; rather, affordability metrics driven by income and interest rates dominate the landscape. The monthly payment burden remains significant relative to local earning power. With a median household income of $61,354, an FHA loan at the median price requires a minimum down payment of 3.5%, resulting in a loan amount of $221,274. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the principal and interest payment is approximately $1,532 per month. This figure consumes 30.0% of the median household income, placing it at the upper threshold of conventional underwriting guidelines for debt-to-income ratios. In contrast, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $1,270, or 24.8% of income, offering a more comfortable margin. However, the initial cash outlay for a 20% down payment on a $229,300 home is $45,860, a substantial barrier for many local households. The 3.5% FHA down payment requires only $8,026, which is more feasible, though still a considerable sum when combined with closing costs. Given the market dynamics, the FHA loan is likely the most practical fit for typical buyers in Bourbon County. It minimizes the upfront capital requirement while keeping the monthly payment within a manageable, albeit tight, range of 30.0% of income. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to lower-rate environments; had rates been 5.0%, the FHA payment would be roughly $1,200, reducing the income burden to approximately 23.0%. The rapid price appreciation, with a 9.6% five-year CAGR, has outpaced income growth, making entry increasingly difficult. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for the 3.5% down payment and closing costs, as this path offers the lowest barrier to entry while maintaining compliance with standard debt-to-income limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Bourbon County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Bourbon County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Bourbon County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Bourbon County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Bourbon County qualifies.
