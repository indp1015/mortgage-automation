📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is ample: the FHA 1‑Unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $197,000 by 275%, and the conforming 1‑Unit limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 423%, confirming that both programs can fully finance a typical purchase. Monthly payment burden is driven by the current 7.40% rate. For an FHA loan requiring 3.5% down on the median price, the principal‑and‑interest payment is $1,316, representing 23.1% of the median household income of $68,370. A conventional loan with 10% down yields a $1,227 payment (18.0% of income), while a 20% down conventional payment is $1,091 (16.0% of income). These figures show that even the highest‑cost FHA payment consumes less than a quarter of monthly earnings, though it remains a substantial share. Given the median price and the size of the conforming loan limit, a conventional mortgage with a 10% down payment is likely the optimal fit for most buyers, balancing lower upfront cash needs with avoidance of mortgage insurance. VA and USDA loans are unnecessary since the median home price is well below the conforming limit and the county is not designated high‑cost, making conventional financing the most straightforward option. A 20% down payment would require $39,400, which may be difficult for many households. The 7.40% interest rate raises the monthly obligation compared with lower‑rate environments, reducing affordability relative to a 6% scenario where payments would be roughly 15‑18% lower. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize an FHA loan with the minimum 3.5% down payment to keep the payment under 25% of income, leveraging the ample FHA coverage and modest cash requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Tom Green County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Tom Green County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Tom Green County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Tom Green County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Tom Green County qualifies.
