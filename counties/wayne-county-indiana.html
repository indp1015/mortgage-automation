📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conventional loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $140,200 in Wayne County. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is 386 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 594 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can finance a typical home within the statutory caps. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment for a median‑priced home is $922 with an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $135,293 loan) and $764 with a conventional loan using a 20 % down payment ($112,160 loan). The FHA payment represents 19.9 % of the median household income ($55,692 annual, or $4,641 monthly), while the conventional 20 % scenario is 16.5 % of monthly income. Both ratios fall well below the conventional 28 % affordability threshold, indicating that the monthly debt service is manageable for the median household. Given the low median home price relative to loan limits, a conventional loan with a 10‑20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option because it avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums required on FHA loans. For first‑time or low‑cash buyers, the FHA program remains viable, but the required down payment of $4,907 (3.5 % of $140,200) may still be a barrier for households without savings. A 10 % conventional down payment ($14,020) is modest yet reduces P&I by roughly $63 per month and eliminates the 0.85 % annual FHA mortgage‑insurance fee. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by about $150 compared with a 5 % environment, shrinking purchasing power by roughly $15,000 at the median price point. Nevertheless, because loan limits are not constraining, buyers can still secure financing without resorting to high‑cost products. **Practical takeaway:** For most median‑priced homebuyers in Wayne County, a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment provides the lowest overall cost while keeping monthly payments comfortably under 20 % of income, even in the current high‑rate climate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Wayne County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Wayne County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Wayne County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Wayne County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Wayne County qualifies.
