📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Cambria County is robust: the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 equals 447 % of the median home price of $121,000, and the conforming 1‑unit limit of $832,750 equals 688 % of that price, confirming that both programs fully cover typical purchases. Monthly payment burden for a median‑priced home under current 7.40 % rates is $808 per month for an FHA loan requiring a 3.5 % down payment on a $116,765 loan, $754 per month for a conventional 10 % down loan on $108,900, and $670 per month for a 20 % down conventional loan on $96,800. These payments represent 16.6 %, 15.5 % and 13.8 % of the median household income of $58,418, indicating modest but manageable affordability. Given the low median price and the ability to qualify with as little as 3.5 % down, an FHA loan is the most suitable option for typical buyers, as it minimizes upfront cash requirements while still meeting lender underwriting standards. A 3.5 % down payment on a $121,000 home equals $4,235, which is about 7.3 % of annual income and therefore feasible for many households; higher down payments of 10 % or 20 % would demand 20‑41 % of income and are less realistic. The current 7.40 % rate makes the FHA payment roughly $150 higher than it would be at 5 % (≈$660), highlighting sensitivity to interest‑rate changes; lower rates would improve affordability substantially. At a median monthly income of $4,868, the FHA payment consumes roughly one‑sixth of earnings, staying well under the conventional 30 % housing cost benchmark, which underscores its suitability despite the higher interest rate. Because the median home value has risen 6.4 % annually over the past five years, the low down payment requirement helps buyers capitalize on appreciation without needing large cash reserves. VA and USDA financing are not necessary for this market, as the property type is primarily single‑family and the county does not qualify as high‑cost for special programs. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should pursue an FHA loan with the minimum down payment to keep monthly obligations near 17 % of income, balancing affordability with the ability to enter the market despite elevated rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Cambria County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Cambria County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Cambria County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Cambria County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Cambria County qualifies.
