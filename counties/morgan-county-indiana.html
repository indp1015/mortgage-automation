📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Morgan County's financing infrastructure comfortably accommodates the local market. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 228% of the $237,600 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 350%, eliminating loan-limit constraints for virtually all single-family purchases. This coverage depth renders jumbo financing unnecessary for typical buyers. At the current 7.40% rate, monthly principal and interest payments consume a meaningful but manageable share of the $79,429 median household income ($6,619/month). An FHA loan with 3.5% down ($8,316) yields a $1,587 payment (24.0% of gross income), while a conventional loan with 20% down ($47,520) reduces the payment to $1,316 (19.9%). Both fall within standard front-end DTI thresholds, though the FHA option approaches the 28% guideline when taxes and insurance are added. The minimum barrier to entry is the FHA's 3.5% down payment—approximately $8,300 plus closing costs—which is feasible for many households given the county's 82.1% owner-occupancy rate. Rate sensitivity remains the primary affordability lever. At 7.40%, the median buyer pays roughly $400 more per month in P&I than at the 5.5% rates seen in 2021, equivalent to a 7.2% reduction in purchasing power. For buyers with limited reserves, FHA's lower down payment and flexible credit requirements make it the pragmatic choice despite mortgage insurance costs. Buyers with 20% down benefit from conventional financing's lack of ongoing mortgage insurance. Given the 10.0% five-year price CAGR, prospective buyers should prioritize accumulating at least 5% down to access conventional 95% LTV programs, which eliminate upfront mortgage insurance and reduce monthly costs versus FHA.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Morgan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Morgan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Morgan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Morgan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Morgan County qualifies.
