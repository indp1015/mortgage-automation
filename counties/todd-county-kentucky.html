📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The mortgage financing landscape in Todd County, Kentucky, presents a distinct profile characterized by low absolute home prices relative to national lending limits. The 2026 FHA loan limit of $541,287 covers 325% of the median home value of $166,700, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 500%. Consequently, the median-priced property falls well within both standard financing tiers, meaning no buyer is constrained by high-cost county restrictions or limit caps. This ample coverage ensures that standard loan products remain accessible for the vast majority of local transactions, eliminating the need for jumbo loans or specialized high-limit programs for typical market participants. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though the current interest rate environment exerts significant pressure. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on the median home is $1,113, representing 21.9% of the median monthly household income derived from $61,103 annual earnings. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $923, or 18.1% of income. These figures fall within conventional debt-to-income guidelines, suggesting affordability is maintained despite elevated rates. However, the 12.0% five-year price CAGR indicates rapid appreciation, which has outpaced income growth in recent years. The 24.3% surge in median value in 2023 alone highlights how quickly entry barriers can rise, making the current 7.40% rate a critical factor in maintaining loan eligibility for first-time buyers. Regarding loan type recommendations, FHA financing appears most suitable for the typical buyer in this market. The low median home price allows buyers to leverage the 3.5% down payment requirement, resulting in an initial cash outlay of approximately $5,835. This is significantly more feasible than the 20% down payment of $33,340 required for optimal conventional terms. While USDA loans may be available depending on specific rural zoning, the FHA’s broad availability and lower down payment threshold offer greater flexibility. The primary barrier to entry is not the loan limit but the upfront capital required. Prospective buyers should note that while the monthly payment is affordable, the rapid price appreciation necessitates acting promptly, as the gap between median income and required down payments widens with each percentage point of home value increase. Strategic focus should remain on securing pre-approval before further price adjustments occur.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Todd County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Todd County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Todd County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Todd County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Todd County qualifies.
