📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a one‑unit property exceeds the median home value in Scott County by 379%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 582%, confirming that both programs fully cover the typical purchase price. For a median‑priced home of $143,000, the FHA loan requiring a 3.5% down payment leaves a principal balance of $137,995, resulting in a monthly principal‑and‑interest payment of $955, which represents 24.7% of the median household’s monthly income of $3,862. The conventional loan with 20% down on a $114,400 balance yields a $792 payment, or 20.5% of income, indicating that both financing options keep the payment burden well within conventional affordability thresholds. Given these figures, an FHA loan is likely the most suitable product for typical buyers, as it requires the lowest down payment, fits comfortably within the county’s loan limits, and produces a payment that consumes less than a quarter of monthly income. A 3.5% down payment on the median home equals roughly $5,000, a level that is generally attainable for households with modest savings, whereas a 10% down payment would demand about $14,300 and a 20% down payment about $28,600, representing significant financial barriers. The current 7.40% interest rate raises the FHA payment to $955, making it substantially higher than what would be expected at lower rates such as 5% or 4%, where the same loan would drop to approximately $720–$660 per month, thereby improving affordability. Although VA and USDA programs are not quantified in the provided data, the available limits and payment calculations indicate FHA remains the optimal fit for most purchasers. Prospective buyers should therefore prioritize securing an FHA loan with the minimum down payment to keep housing costs below 25% of income, leveraging the county’s generous loan limits to maximize purchasing power.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Scott County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Scott County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Scott County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Scott County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Scott County qualifies.
