📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Klickitat County, Washington, presents a distinct disconnect between loan availability and buyer affordability. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 127% of the $425,300 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 196%. Consequently, the typical median-priced property falls well within standard loan limits, eliminating high-cost county restrictions. However, adequate loan coverage does not equate to affordability. With a median household income of $71,042, the monthly principal and interest payment on an FHA loan for the median home is $2,841. This amount represents 48.0% of the median monthly income, significantly exceeding the conventional 28% debt-to-income threshold. Even with a 20% down payment on a conventional loan, the payment of $2,355 still consumes 39.8% of median income, indicating that typical local earners face substantial strain when financing at market median prices. Regarding loan type recommendations, conventional financing is generally more suitable for buyers with stronger credit profiles and higher incomes, as it avoids mortgage insurance premiums. However, for lower-income buyers typical of the county’s median, FHA loans offer the lowest barrier to entry with a 3.5% down payment. This requires approximately $14,886 in cash, a sum that is feasible for many buyers but remains a significant hurdle compared to the 20% down payment of $85,060 required for conventional loans without private mortgage insurance. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% exacerbates these burdens. In lower-rate environments, such as 6.0%, the FHA payment would drop by several hundred dollars, potentially bringing the debt-to-income ratio closer to acceptable levels. The rapid appreciation of home values, with a 14.2% five-year CAGR, has outpaced income growth, further tightening the affordability gap. Prospective buyers in Klickitat County must recognize that median-priced homes are largely unaffordable for median-income households at current interest rates. A concrete insight for potential purchasers is that targeting homes below the $350,000 mark or seeking additional income streams is critical for qualifying for standard financing. Without such adjustments, buyers may need to rely on co-signers, larger down payments to reduce loan amounts, or non-traditional financing structures. The data suggests that while loan limits are not the constraining factor, the combination of elevated home prices and high interest rates creates a rigid affordability ceiling that excludes a significant portion of the local population from purchasing at the median price point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Klickitat County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Klickitat County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Klickitat County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Klickitat County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Klickitat County qualifies.
