📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Green County’s mortgage landscape is characterized by generous federal loan limits relative to local home values. The FHA limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price of $125,000 by 433%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 666%. Both limits therefore provide ample financing coverage for typical purchases, and the median home comfortably falls within the FHA ceiling. Monthly payment burden is moderate when expressed as a share of median household income of $46,798. An FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment ($120,625 loan) requires $822 per month P&I, representing 21.1% of income. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment ($100,000 loan) lowers the P&I to $681, or 17.5% of income. Although both ratios sit above the traditional 28% front‑end guideline, they remain within acceptable debt‑service ranges for many borrowers. Given the price point and available limits, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment is likely the best fit for typical buyers. It reduces the monthly obligation and builds equity faster than an FHA loan, while still leveraging the high conforming limit if larger financing is needed later. FHA financing remains attractive for first‑time buyers who prefer a lower initial cash outlay. Down‑payment feasibility is a key consideration. The minimum required is 3.5% of $125,000, or $4,375, which is relatively accessible. However, a 20% down payment of $25,000 represents roughly 53% of median household income, posing a significant barrier for many families. The current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24% heightens affordability pressure. Compared with a hypothetical 6% environment, the same $120,625 loan would cost roughly $720 P&I, a $102 monthly savings that could shift the payment ratio from 21.1% to about 19% of income. Higher rates thus compress purchasing power and increase the importance of minimizing interest costs through larger down payments. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective Green County buyers should prioritize a conventional loan with at least a 20% down payment to mitigate the impact of the 7.24% rate and achieve a more sustainable monthly housing expense.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Green County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Green County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Green County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Green County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Green County qualifies.
