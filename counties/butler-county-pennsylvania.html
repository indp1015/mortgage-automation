📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA 1‑Unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price in Butler County by 84 %, covering 184 % of the $294,200 median value, while the conforming 1‑Unit limit of $832,750 reaches 283 % of the median price, confirming that both loan programs adequately cover typical purchases. For a median‑priced home, the Federal Housing Administration requires a 3.5 % down payment, translating to approximately $10,300 and a monthly principal‑and‑interest payment of $1,965 at the current 7.40 % rate. A conventional loan with 20 % down reduces the loan to $235,360 and lowers the payment to $1,629 per month. Both payments represent a substantial share of the median household income of $89,843, equating to 26.2 % for FHA and 21.8 % for the 20 % down conventional loan, indicating a significant affordability strain. Given the price level and the availability of FHA financing with a low down‑payment requirement, the FHA loan emerges as the most suitable option for typical buyers, although a conforming conventional loan remains viable for those able to meet a 10 % or 20 % down payment. The minimum down payment needed to purchase at the median price is $10,300 for FHA or $29,420 for a 10 % conventional loan, and $58,840 for a 20 % down payment; the former is generally attainable for first‑time purchasers, while the latter may present a barrier. The present 7.40 % interest rate inflates monthly costs compared with lower‑rate environments; for example, a 5.5 % rate would reduce the FHA payment to roughly $1,560, improving the payment‑to‑income ratio, whereas the current rate keeps the ratio above 25 %, limiting purchasing power. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize securing an FHA loan with the minimal down payment to mitigate the impact of high rates and preserve cash flow.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Butler County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Butler County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Butler County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Butler County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Butler County qualifies.
