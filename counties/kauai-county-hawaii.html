📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Kauai County’s housing market presents significant financing challenges, primarily driven by the divergence between median home values and federal loan limits. The 2026 FHA limit for a one-unit property is $811,931, which covers only 93% of the median home value of $873,200. Consequently, the typical buyer cannot utilize standard FHA financing for a median-priced home without exceeding the limit, necessitating a conforming or jumbo loan. While the conforming limit of $1,249,125 covers 143% of the median price, it does not alleviate the burden of high down payments or elevated monthly costs. The market’s classification as a high-cost county reflects these structural pressures, where standard government-backed options fall short for the average transaction. The affordability burden is severe when analyzing monthly payments against local income levels. At a current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the principal and interest payment for a median-priced home with a 20% down payment is $4,836. This amount represents 59.4% of the median monthly household income of $8,139 (derived from the annual median of $97,668). Even with a 10% down payment, the payment rises to $5,441, consuming 66.8% of monthly income. These figures far exceed the standard 28% front-end debt-to-income ratio recommended by most lenders. The rapid appreciation of home values, with a 5-year CAGR of 10.9%, has outpaced income growth, creating a widening gap between asset prices and local purchasing power. For typical buyers, conventional financing with a substantial down payment is the most viable path, as FHA limits are insufficient for median-priced properties. However, the minimum 20% down payment required to avoid private mortgage insurance amounts to $174,640, a substantial barrier for local households. Lower down payment options, such as 3.5% via FHA, are effectively unavailable for median-priced homes due to limit constraints. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly exacerbates this issue; a drop to 6.0% would reduce the 20% down payment scenario’s P&I to approximately $4,260, lowering the income burden to 52.3%. Prospective buyers should recognize that purchasing at the median price requires either income well above the local median or a down payment exceeding $174,000, limiting access to a narrow segment of the population.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Kauai County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Kauai County is $811,931. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Kauai County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Kauai County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Kauai County qualifies.
