📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The mortgage financing landscape in Burke County, North Dakota, demonstrates robust coverage for the typical homebuyer. With a median home value of $158,100, the property sits well below both the FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. The FHA limit covers 342% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 527%, ensuring that standard loan products are fully accessible without requiring specialized high-balance financing. This ample headroom indicates that financing constraints are not driven by loan caps but rather by borrower qualification metrics. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,056 per month, representing 13.8% of the median household income of $91,591. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $875, or 11.5% of income. These ratios fall within conventional affordability guidelines, suggesting that debt-to-income ratios are the primary barrier rather than absolute payment size. Given the low median price point, FHA loans are likely the most suitable fit for typical buyers due to their lower down payment requirements and flexible credit criteria. The minimum down payment for an FHA loan is approximately $5,534, a feasible amount for many households in this market. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts affordability compared to historical averages. If rates were lower, such as 6.0%, the monthly payment for the same FHA loan would decrease by approximately $100, further easing the burden. However, even at 7.40%, the payment-to-income ratio remains healthy. The 5-year price CAGR of 6.5% suggests steady appreciation, which may erode affordability over time if incomes do not keep pace. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing fixed-rate financing to mitigate interest rate volatility. The practical takeaway is that while entry costs are low, the rapid price appreciation observed between 2021 and 2024 necessitates prompt action for buyers, as waiting may result in higher principal balances that outpace income growth. Overall, Burke County offers a favorable entry point for first-time buyers, provided they can navigate the current interest rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Burke County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Burke County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Burke County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Burke County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Burke County qualifies.
