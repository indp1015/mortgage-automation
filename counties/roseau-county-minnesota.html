📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Roseau County’s housing market presents a favorable financing landscape, as the median home value of $183,700 sits well below federal loan limits. The FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 covers 295% of the median price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 453%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces high-cost county restrictions or limit constraints for a typical purchase. This ample coverage ensures that standard financing options remain accessible regardless of the specific loan program selected, provided credit qualifications are met. Monthly payment burdens remain moderate relative to local income levels. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on a median-priced home is $1,227 per month. This represents 19.8% of the median household income of $74,474. For conventional loans, the burden decreases significantly; a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $1,017, or 16.4% of income. These ratios fall within standard lender guidelines for debt-to-income ratios, suggesting that typical households can service these debts without excessive strain, even before accounting for property taxes, insurance, and HOA fees. Given the low median price point, the FHA loan is likely the most practical fit for first-time buyers due to its 3.5% minimum down payment requirement. This equates to approximately $6,430 for a median-priced home, a significantly lower barrier than the 20% down payment ($36,740) required for optimal conventional terms. While USDA loans may also be viable depending on specific rural eligibility maps, the FHA’s broad availability and low entry cost make it a primary contender. The current 7.40% interest rate introduces a notable affordability headwind compared to historical lows. A rate reduction of just 100 basis points would lower the FHA monthly payment by approximately $75, demonstrating significant sensitivity to interest rate fluctuations. Prospective buyers should prioritize improving credit scores to secure the best available rates, as the low absolute loan amounts mean that even small rate differentials translate to meaningful long-term savings. The combination of low home values and high loan limit coverage creates a resilient market where financing accessibility is less dependent on income growth and more on creditworthiness and down payment savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Roseau County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Roseau County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Roseau County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Roseau County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Roseau County qualifies.
