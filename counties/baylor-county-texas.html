📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Baylor County’s mortgage market is characterized by significant financing headroom, as the median home value of $103,100 falls well below both the FHA limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. This coverage ratio indicates that standard loan products are fully accessible to local buyers without requiring non-qualified mortgage alternatives. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% drives the principal and interest (P&I) burden, with an FHA loan at 3.5% down resulting in a monthly payment of $688. This amount represents 18.2% of the median household income of $45,370, a figure that remains within the conventional 28% debt-to-income guideline for housing costs. Conversely, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the P&I to $571, or 15.1% of income, offering greater cash flow flexibility. Given the low median price point, the FHA loan is likely the most suitable option for the typical buyer in this market. The 3.5% down payment requirement translates to approximately $3,609, a significantly lower barrier to entry than the $20,620 required for a 20% down conventional loan. While USDA loans may be relevant if specific rural eligibility criteria are met, the FHA’s broad availability and manageable down payment make it the primary vehicle for first-time buyers. The feasibility of this down payment is high relative to local income levels, facilitating higher owner-occupancy rates, which currently stand at 66.2%. The 7.40% interest rate exerts a notable pressure on affordability compared to historical lower-rate environments. Although the absolute dollar amount of the payment is modest due to the low home price, the percentage of income allocated to debt service is higher than it would be at sub-5% rates. This rate sensitivity means that buyers are more vulnerable to rate fluctuations, yet the low loan principal mitigates the total interest paid over the life of the loan. Prospective buyers should focus on securing pre-approval with FHA lenders to lock in current rates, as the small loan size makes the transaction less sensitive to total interest costs but highly sensitive to the initial down payment requirement. Ultimately, the combination of low home values and accessible loan limits creates a favorable environment for entry-level homeownership, provided that borrowers maintain stable income streams to service the 18.2% payment burden.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Baylor County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Baylor County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Baylor County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Baylor County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Baylor County qualifies.
