📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Irion County’s housing market presents a unique financing landscape where loan limits significantly exceed typical property values. The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home is $541,287, which covers 265% of the median home value of $204,400. Similarly, the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 407% of the median price. This substantial coverage indicates that no buyer in the median price range will encounter high-cost county restrictions or require jumbo loans. Consequently, standard financing products are fully accessible for the vast majority of local transactions, ensuring that regulatory caps do not hinder market participation for typical residents. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though interest rates exert significant pressure. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan on the median-priced home requires a monthly principal and interest payment of $1,365. This amount represents 23.3% of the median household income of $70,357. While this ratio is within the general 30% threshold for housing expenses, it leaves limited room for taxes and insurance. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $1,132, or 19.3% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. Given the low median home value, the minimum down payment for an FHA loan is approximately $7,154 (3.5% of $204,400), which is a feasible barrier for many households. However, the 2024 price surge of 33.9% has compressed affordability margins compared to the 2023 median of $152,600. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts purchasing power compared to lower-rate environments. A two-point decrease in interest rates would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $100, effectively increasing the affordable price range. For prospective buyers, the primary challenge is not loan eligibility but the rapid appreciation of home values. The 6.9% five-year compound annual growth rate has outpaced income growth, making the 2024 median price notably higher than historical norms. Buyers should prioritize conventional loans if they can secure 20% down payment to minimize monthly obligations and avoid mortgage insurance, whereas FHA loans remain a viable entry point for those with limited savings. The combination of high loan limit coverage and moderate income levels suggests that credit qualification, rather than price caps, is the primary determinant of access to homeownership in this rural market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Irion County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Irion County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Irion County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Irion County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Irion County qualifies.
