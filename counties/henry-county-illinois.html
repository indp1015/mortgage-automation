📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Henry County, Illinois, presents a highly accessible financing environment where federal loan limits significantly exceed local property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 357% of the median home value of $151,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 549%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces loan limit constraints for a typical purchase. This ample coverage ensures that both government-backed and conventional financing options remain fully available, eliminating the need for jumbo loans or high-cost financing structures that are common in higher-priced metropolitan areas. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though the current 7.40% interest rate exerts noticeable pressure. For the median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,012, representing 16.9% of the median household income of $71,911. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this burden to $839, or 14.0% of income. While these figures fall within standard affordability guidelines, the high interest rate means that buyers pay substantially more over the life of the loan compared to historical low-rate environments. The minimum down payment barrier is relatively low; an FHA buyer needs only $5,306 to purchase the median home, a sum that is feasible for many households with modest savings. This low entry point contrasts sharply with markets where down payments exceed $20,000, making homeownership more attainable for first-time buyers in Henry County. Given the local market dynamics, FHA loans are likely the optimal fit for typical buyers. The combination of a low 3.5% down payment requirement and the fact that the median home value is well below the FHA limit allows buyers to preserve liquidity while securing financing. Conventional loans with 20% down offer lower monthly payments but require a $30,320 upfront investment, which may be prohibitive for those at the median income level. The primary practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that the significant gap between local home prices and federal loan limits provides substantial safety margins. This structural advantage means that even with elevated interest rates, the absolute dollar amount of monthly payments remains modest relative to local wages. Buyers should prioritize maximizing their down payment to reduce the principal balance, as the high interest rate amplifies the cost of carrying debt. By leveraging the low barrier to entry provided by FHA financing, households in Henry County can maintain strong debt-to-income ratios despite the challenging interest rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Henry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Henry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Henry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Henry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Henry County qualifies.
