📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 covers only 82 % of Hood River County’s median home price of $660,000, while the conforming 1‑unit limit of $832,750 exceeds the median by 26 %. Consequently, most buyers will need a loan that meets the conforming threshold or a jumbo financing arrangement; FHA alone is insufficient. The estimated P&I payment for a median‑priced home under a 10 % down conventional loan is $4,112 per month, representing 55 % of the median household income of $88,947 (monthly $7,412). Even the 20 % down scenario ($3,655) consumes 49 % of income, indicating a substantial affordability strain. At the current 7.40 % 30‑year fixed rate, the $594,000 loan carries a P&I payment of $4,112, roughly 30 % higher than a 5 % rate would produce, thereby intensifying the burden on household budgets. Given the price level and the limited FHA coverage, a conventional loan is the most appropriate financing option. VA and USDA programs are not quantified in the data, but conventional loans permit down payments as low as 3 % and up to 20 %, allowing buyers to align loan size with the conforming limit while managing payment ratios. To purchase at the median price, a 3.5 % FHA down payment requires $23,100, which is feasible, yet the loan amount would exceed the FHA ceiling, forcing a higher‑cost loan. A 10 % conventional down payment equals $66,000 and a 20 % down payment $132,000; the latter may be challenging for typical households but is necessary to keep the loan‑to‑value under 80 % and avoid private mortgage insurance. Prospective buyers should target at least a 20 % down payment or secure a conforming‑limit loan to keep the loan‑to‑value below 80 % and mitigate the high payment‑to‑income ratio imposed by the 7.40 % rate. This insight underscores the importance of planning down payment savings before beginning the home search.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Hood River County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Hood River County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Hood River County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Hood River County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Hood River County qualifies.
