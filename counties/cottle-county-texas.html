📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Cottle County, Texas, is characterized by an extreme surplus of loan limits relative to local property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 913% of the median home value of $59,300, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 1,404%. Consequently, price-based loan limit constraints are non-existent for the typical buyer. This structural abundance ensures that any standard residential mortgage product can accommodate the median-priced home without requiring jumbo loan structures or high-cost county adjustments, which do not apply to this jurisdiction. Affordability metrics indicate strong payment capacity for local households. With a median household income of $59,063, the estimated monthly principal and interest payment for an FHA loan is $396, representing 8.0% of monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down requires a payment of $328, or 6.7% of income. Both figures fall well below the standard 30% debt-to-income threshold, suggesting that income is not a primary barrier to entry. Regarding down payment barriers, the minimum FHA requirement of 3.5% equates to approximately $2,076, while a 20% conventional down payment requires $11,860. Given the low absolute dollar amounts, these sums are significantly more manageable than in higher-cost markets, though they still represent a substantial lump sum for households with limited savings. Loan type selection is driven less by limit constraints and more by borrower eligibility and credit profiles. For buyers with credit scores between 580 and 619, FHA loans offer the lowest barrier to entry with the $2,076 down payment. However, for those with stronger credit and savings, conventional loans provide lower total costs due to the absence of mortgage insurance premiums. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% introduces significant rate sensitivity. Compared to historical averages below 4%, the current rate increases the monthly payment burden by roughly 50% for the same loan amount. Despite this, the low home price insulates buyers from the full impact of high rates seen in urban markets. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that while interest rates remain elevated, the exceptionally low median home value of $59,300 ensures that monthly payments remain a small fraction of local median income, maintaining high relative affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Cottle County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Cottle County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Cottle County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Cottle County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Cottle County qualifies.
