📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $147,800 in Graves County. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is 366 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 563 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that fully finances a typical property without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo products. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, principal‑and‑interest (P&I) obligations for a median‑priced home are $972 per month with an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $142,627 loan) and $805 per month with a conventional loan using a 20 % down payment ($118,240 loan). Relative to the median household income of $50,772 (approximately $4,231 monthly), the FHA payment represents 23 % of income and the conventional‑20 % payment 19 %. Both ratios sit below the conventional 28 % affordability threshold, indicating that the debt service burden is manageable for an average household, though the FHA option consumes a larger share of income due to the smaller down payment. For most first‑time buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective choice because it eliminates mortgage‑insurance premiums and yields the lowest P&I payment. However, the required $29,560 down payment (20 % of $147,800) may be a barrier for many residents; the median savings rate in the county suggests that accumulating this amount could take several years. An FHA loan, needing only $5,173 down (3.5 %), is more attainable but raises the monthly payment to 23 % of income and adds insurance costs. USDA or VA financing could be viable for eligible rural or veteran buyers, offering zero‑down options and comparable rates, but eligibility constraints limit their universal applicability. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $150–$200 compared with a 5 % environment, reducing purchasing power by about $15,000 of home value. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize saving a larger down payment to lock in a conventional loan and mitigate the impact of high rates on overall affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Graves County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Graves County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Graves County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Graves County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Graves County qualifies.
