📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA 1‑unit loan limit of $541,287 is more than double Phillips County’s median home value of $258,200, providing ample ceiling capacity; the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds the median price by a factor of 3.2. Consequently, both FHA and conventional financing can comfortably accommodate a typical purchase, and the median home sits well within the FHA ceiling (210 % of price) and far below the conforming ceiling (323 %). At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, a median‑priced home financed with a conventional 20 % down payment ($206,560 loan) yields a principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment of $1,407 per month. This represents 26.1 % of the county’s median monthly household income ($5,389), comfortably below the conventional affordability benchmark of 28‑30 %. An FHA loan with the statutory 3.5 % down ($249,163 loan) produces a $1,698 P&I payment, or 31.5 % of income, edging toward the upper limit of acceptable burden. The conventional 10 % down scenario ($1,583/month) sits at 29.4 % of income, also within the conventional threshold. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option because it avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums and yields the lowest P&I. However, the required $51,640 down payment (20 % of $258,200) may be a barrier for first‑time or lower‑wealth households; a 10 % down ($25,820) or FHA’s 3.5 % down ($9,040) are more attainable but increase monthly outlays and insurance costs. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $150–$200 compared with a 5 % environment, reducing purchasing power and raising the minimum viable down payment to maintain the 28 % income‑to‑payment ratio. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers who can secure a 20 % down payment will experience the most affordable monthly obligation and avoid FHA insurance costs; those unable to meet that threshold should plan for a higher debt‑to‑income ratio or consider supplemental savings to offset the rate‑driven payment premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Phillips County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Phillips County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Phillips County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Phillips County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Phillips County qualifies.
