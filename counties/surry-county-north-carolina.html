📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Surry County is robust. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $177,600 by 305%, confirming that an FHA loan fully encompasses the typical purchase. Likewise, the conforming loan limit of $832,750 represents 469% of the median price, providing ample capacity for conventional financing. Monthly payment burden is significant relative to household earnings. With a median household income of $55,656 annually, the monthly income is $4,638. The FHA‑insured loan requiring a 3.5% down payment on a $171,384 principal carries a P&I payment of $1,186, equal to 25.6% of monthly income. The conventional 20% down loan on a $142,080 principal yields a P&I of $983, or 21.2% of income. Both figures approach or exceed conventional affordability thresholds, indicating a substantial share of income is devoted to housing costs. Given the price level and available limits, an FHA loan is likely the optimal choice for typical buyers, as it permits low down payments and aligns with the median home value. A 3.5% down payment translates to $6,216, a figure that may be attainable through savings or assistance programs, whereas a 10% down payment ($17,760) and a 20% down payment ($35,520) present greater barriers. The current 7.40% interest rate increases monthly obligations compared with lower‑rate environments; for example, a 5% rate would reduce the FHA payment to approximately $950, reducing the income share to roughly 20% and making the purchase more attainable. While VA and USDA loans are not referenced in the data, they remain potential alternatives for eligible veterans and rural residents, though their eligibility criteria may limit applicability. Practical takeaway: prospective purchasers should prioritize FHA financing to minimize upfront cash requirements while recognizing that the present rate level consumes roughly one‑quarter of median household income, necessitating careful budgeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Surry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Surry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Surry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Surry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Surry County qualifies.
