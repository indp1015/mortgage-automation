📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Morgan County's financing infrastructure provides substantial coverage for the local market. The FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $130,800 by 414%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 provides 637% coverage. Both limits comfortably encompass the typical transaction, eliminating loan-limit constraints for virtually all buyers in this market. Monthly payment burden remains manageable relative to the $66,306 median household income ($5,526 monthly). At the current 7.40% rate, an FHA loan with 3.5% down ($4,578) yields $873 in principal and interest—15.8% of gross monthly income. Conventional financing with 10% down ($13,080) reduces P&I to $815 (14.7%), while 20% down ($26,160) lowers it to $724 (13.1%). All scenarios fall well below the 28% front-end DTI threshold, though the FHA option adds mortgage insurance premiums that increase total housing cost. For the typical buyer, conventional financing with 10% down presents the optimal balance: it avoids FHA's upfront and annual mortgage insurance while requiring a down payment ($13,080) that is approximately 19.7% of annual household income—a feasible target for many households. VA and USDA loans offer zero-down alternatives for eligible borrowers, particularly relevant given the county's rural designation. Rate sensitivity is pronounced at 7.40%. Each 100-basis-point decrease would reduce the 20%-down P&I by approximately $65 monthly. Compared to the 3% rate environment of 2021, the current rate adds roughly $220 monthly to the same loan amount, effectively reducing purchasing power by 25-30%. Practical takeaway: Buyers with 10% down payment capacity should prioritize conventional financing to minimize total borrowing cost; those constrained to 3.5% down benefit from FHA's accessibility but should budget for the added insurance expense.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Morgan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Morgan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Morgan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Morgan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Morgan County qualifies.
