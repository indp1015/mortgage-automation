📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Burleigh County is adequate for the typical home. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the 2024 median home value of $324,000 by 167%, while the conforming loan limit of $832,750 covers more than 257% of the median price. Consequently, both FHA and conventional financing can fully fund a purchase at the median price point. Monthly payment burden is moderate relative to household income. With a median household income of $86,851, the monthly income is approximately $7,238. The FHA‑backed payment for a $312,660 loan at 3.5% down is $2,164, representing 29.9% of income. A conventional loan with 20% down on a $259,200 loan costs $1,794, or 24.8% of income. These figures fall within typical affordability thresholds, though the FHA option carries the highest payment share. Given the price level and loan limits, FHA financing is likely the most suitable for typical buyers. The 3.5% down payment requirement ($11,340) is lower than the 10% ($32,400) or 20% ($64,800) needed for conventional loans, making it more accessible while still satisfying the FHA limit. A 3.5% down payment translates to roughly $11,300, which is attainable for many households with savings or assistance programs, though it remains a non‑trivial barrier. VA and USDA options are not quantified here, but the FHA product aligns with both price and down‑payment considerations. The current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.40% raises the payment compared with lower‑rate environments; a 1% reduction would lower the FHA payment by roughly $200 per month, improving affordability. At 7.40%, the FHA payment consumes nearly a third of monthly income, whereas a 5% rate would reduce it to about $1,950, a 10% decrease. Prospective buyers should therefore prioritize securing the lowest feasible rate and consider FHA loans to keep the payment under 30% of their monthly income, mitigating the impact of higher interest costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Burleigh County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Burleigh County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Burleigh County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Burleigh County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Burleigh County qualifies.
