📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Sarpy County’s housing market presents a favorable financing landscape relative to national loan limits, ensuring that the median home value of $314,100 remains well within both FHA and conforming boundaries. The FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 covers 172% of the median price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 extends to 265%. Consequently, no buyer purchasing at the median price faces high-cost county restrictions or limit-driven financing gaps. This structural coverage allows for a wide array of loan products, though the choice between FHA and conventional options depends heavily on down payment capacity and credit profile rather than loan ceiling constraints. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable for the average household, although the 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $2,098, consuming 24.4% of the median household income of $103,321. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $1,739, or 20.2% of income. While both ratios fall within standard underwriting guidelines, the higher rate environment means that buyers with lower incomes may find the 24.4% FHA ratio approaching the upper limits of debt-to-income ratios, particularly when factoring in property taxes and insurance. The 12.6% five-year price CAGR has compressed affordability margins, making the current rate environment a critical variable in determining loan eligibility. Regarding loan type recommendations, conventional financing is generally the most efficient option for buyers with at least 20% down payment, as it avoids mortgage insurance premiums and offers lower overall costs. However, for first-time buyers with limited capital, FHA loans provide a viable entry point with a minimum 3.5% down payment, equating to approximately $10,994 at the median price. This barrier is feasible for many households, especially when compared to the $62,820 required for a 20% conventional down payment. The primary practical takeaway is that while loan limits are not a constraint, the 7.40% interest rate necessitates careful budgeting. Prospective buyers should prioritize increasing their down payment to reduce principal balances, as even a 10% down payment on a conventional loan lowers the monthly P&I to $1,957, offering a modest improvement in cash flow without the recurring cost of FHA mortgage insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Sarpy County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Sarpy County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Sarpy County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Sarpy County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Sarpy County qualifies.
