📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Adams County, Indiana, demonstrates robust coverage relative to national loan limits. The median home value of $178,400 sits well below the 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. Specifically, the FHA limit covers 303% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 467%. This significant margin ensures that no typical buyer in this market faces loan cap restrictions, allowing for full financing of standard properties without entering the jumbo loan category. Consequently, the market is not constrained by high-cost county regulations, further simplifying the underwriting process for local lenders. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable for the median household, although the current interest rate environment exerts pressure on cash flow. At a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down on a median-priced home results in a principal and interest payment of $1,191 per month. This figure represents 21.9% of the median household income of $65,289. While this ratio falls within the typical 28% guideline for front-end debt-to-income ratios, it leaves less room for taxes, insurance, and other living expenses compared to lower-rate periods. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces the monthly P&I to $988, or 18.2% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. The shift from the 2020-2022 price appreciation surge, where values grew at rates exceeding 9%, to the current 3.1% year-over-year growth, suggests a stabilizing market that may ease entry barriers for first-time buyers. Regarding loan type recommendations, FHA financing is likely the most accessible option for typical buyers due to its low 3.5% down payment requirement. This equates to approximately $6,244 on a median-priced home, a sum that is feasible for many households with modest savings. Conventional options require higher initial capital, with 10% and 20% down payments totaling $17,840 and $35,680, respectively. Although USDA loans may be available given the rural nature of the county, FHA remains the primary vehicle for low-down-payment purchases. The current 7.40% rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a 1% decrease in rates would reduce the FHA monthly P&I by approximately $75. Prospective buyers should prioritize building savings to meet the minimum down payment threshold, as the low entry cost of the median home combined with FHA eligibility makes homeownership attainable despite elevated interest rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Adams County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Adams County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Adams County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Adams County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Adams County qualifies.
