📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750 both exceed the median Sullivan County home price of $197,100, covering 275 % and 423 % of the median value respectively, so financing coverage is adequate for typical buyers. The conforming loan limit therefore enables borrowers to finance homes well beyond the median price without requiring a jumbo loan, while the FHA limit comfortably encompasses the typical property. For a median‑priced home, the Federal Housing Administration requires a 3.5 % down payment, resulting in a $190,202 loan and a principal‑and‑interest payment of $1,316 per month. A conventional loan with 20 % down reduces the loan to $157,680 and the payment to $1,091 per month. Both payments represent 22.6 % and 18.8 % of the median household income of $69,764 (monthly $5,797), indicating a moderate but noticeable burden, especially for the FHA scenario which approaches the 28 % affordability threshold. Given the down‑payment requirements, a 3.5 % FHA down payment of roughly $6,900 is the most feasible entry point for most households, while the 20 % conventional down payment of $39,400 poses a significant barrier. The current 7.40 % interest rate inflates the FHA payment by about $125 compared with a 6 % rate, making the loan less affordable than it would be in a lower‑rate environment. Consequently, an FHA loan is likely the optimal choice for typical buyers, as it aligns with the median price, minimizes upfront cash needs, and keeps the payment within a manageable share of income. A concrete insight for buyers is to target an FHA loan with the minimum down payment, as this keeps the monthly obligation near 23 % of income, which is the most realistic affordability threshold under the present 7.40 % rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Sullivan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Sullivan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Sullivan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Sullivan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Sullivan County qualifies.
