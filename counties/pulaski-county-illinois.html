📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price in Pulaski County. The 2026 FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 is 636 % of the median value ($85,100), while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 979 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can finance a typical property without hitting statutory caps. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $559 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $82,122 loan) and $463 for a conventional loan with 20 % down ($68,080 loan). Relative to the median household income of $42,463 (≈$3,538 monthly), the FHA payment represents 15.8 % of monthly earnings, and the 20 % conventional payment 13.1 %. Both ratios fall well below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % of gross income, indicating that the monthly debt service is not a primary barrier for average earners. Given the low price point and the ample loan‑limit headroom, conventional financing with a 20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option because it avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums and yields the lowest P&I ($463). FHA financing remains viable for buyers who can only muster a 3.5 % down payment ($2,978), but the added insurance costs increase the overall expense. USDA eligibility is unlikely because the county’s median income exceeds the program’s 115 % poverty line, and VA loans apply only to eligible veterans. The minimum cash required to purchase at the median price is $2,978 for FHA or $8,510 for a conventional 10 % down payment; the 20 % conventional down payment ($17,020) is more demanding but still attainable for many households given the modest home price. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $80–$100 compared with a 5 % environment, yet the impact is muted because the loan balances are small relative to national markets. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers in Pulaski County can secure a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment and keep housing costs under 14 % of gross income, making homeownership financially realistic despite the elevated national interest rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Pulaski County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Pulaski County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Pulaski County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Pulaski County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Pulaski County qualifies.
