📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Union County, Mississippi, presents a favorable financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local market values. The 2026 FHA limit for a one-unit property is set at $541,287, which covers 331% of the median home value of $163,400. Similarly, the conforming limit of $832,750 represents 510% of the median price. Consequently, virtually all residential properties in this market fall well within standard non-jumbo financing parameters. This ample coverage ensures that typical buyers are not constrained by loan caps, allowing for straightforward access to both government-backed and conventional mortgage products without requiring specialized high-limit lenders. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though they require careful budgeting. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan on the median-priced home requires a minimum down payment of $5,719 (3.5%), resulting in a principal and interest payment of $1,091. This figure consumes 23.6% of the median household income of $55,505. While this percentage is near the upper threshold of standard debt-to-income guidelines, it remains feasible for qualified borrowers. Alternatively, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $905, or 19.6% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. The significant price appreciation, with a 12.1% five-year CAGR, has increased entry costs since 2017, when the median value was $92,500. However, the current stabilization at 2.7% year-over-year growth suggests a more predictable pricing environment for buyers. For typical buyers, the FHA loan is likely the most accessible option due to its low down payment requirement and lenient credit standards, provided the borrower can meet the 23.6% payment-to-income ratio. Conventional loans become more attractive for those with savings to meet the 20% down payment threshold, as the lower payment burden improves long-term cash flow. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a 100 basis point decrease would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $70, further easing the income requirement. Prospective buyers should note that while the absolute dollar amount of the down payment is modest, the primary barrier is the monthly cash flow requirement. Given the 74.7% owner-occupancy rate, the market supports strong housing demand, but buyers must ensure their debt-to-income ratios align with the current high-rate environment to secure financing for the median-priced home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Union County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Union County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Union County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Union County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Union County qualifies.
