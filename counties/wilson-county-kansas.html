📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed Wilson County’s median home price of $93,500. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 is 579 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 891 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that fully funds a typical purchase without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo financing. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $614 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $90,228 loan) and $509 for a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($74,800 loan). Both payments represent modest shares of the median household income of $60,677 (annual) or $5,056 monthly. The FHA payment equals 12.1 % of monthly income, while a conventional loan with 20 % down is 10.1 % of income, both well below the 28 % threshold commonly used to gauge housing affordability. Given the low price level relative to loan limits, conventional financing is the most efficient option for typical buyers. A 20 % down payment of $18,700 eliminates mortgage‑insurance premiums and yields the lowest P&I burden. However, the minimum down payment required to secure any loan is 3.5 % for FHA ($3,273) or 10 % for conventional ($9,350). For households earning the median income, a 3.5 % down payment is readily attainable, while a 10 % down payment may pose a modest barrier for first‑time buyers lacking savings. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $70–$80 compared with a 5 % environment, reducing purchasing power by about $12,000 in loan size for the same debt‑to‑income ratio. Nevertheless, because the median home price is far below both loan limits, the rate impact remains manageable for most qualified borrowers. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers in Wilson County can comfortably finance a median‑priced home with a conventional loan and a 20 % down payment, keeping housing costs under 11 % of monthly income even at today’s 7.24 % rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Wilson County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Wilson County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Wilson County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Wilson County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Wilson County qualifies.
