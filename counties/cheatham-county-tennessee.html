📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Cheatham County’s median home price of $338,700 is fully supported by both the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 (160% of median) and the conforming 1‑unit limit of $1,029,250 (304% of median), confirming that financing caps do not restrict typical purchases. For a median‑priced home, the Federal Housing Administration requires a 3.5% down payment ($11,848) on a $326,846 loan, resulting in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $2,263 per month, which equals 30.2% of the median household income of $89,852. A conventional loan with 20% down ($67,740) on a $270,960 loan yields a payment of $1,876, representing 25.1% of income, while a 10% down conventional loan ($33,870) produces a $2,110 payment, or 23.5% of income. Given the down‑payment requirements, a conventional loan with 10% down is the most balanced option for buyers who can accumulate roughly $34,000, allowing payments under 25% of income while avoiding the higher mortgage‑insurance costs of FHA. However, for households with limited savings, the FHA program remains viable because its lower upfront cost and higher loan‑to‑value capacity accommodate the median price, albeit with a payment burden that exceeds the 30% threshold. The current 7.40% interest rate amplifies affordability challenges compared with a 5% environment, where the same FHA loan would incur a payment near $1,900, reducing the income share to about 26%. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize saving for at least a 10% down payment to secure a conventional loan and keep the debt service ratio within prudent limits, while recognizing that FHA financing offers a feasible alternative for those constrained by upfront cash. At a 7.40% rate, the monthly payment for the median home exceeds the 25% threshold recommended for affordable housing, indicating that even a conventional 10% down loan may strain budgets unless income is higher. Prospective purchasers should therefore evaluate their cash reserves and debt‑to‑income ratios before committing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Cheatham County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Cheatham County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Cheatham County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,029,250 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Cheatham County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Cheatham County qualifies.
