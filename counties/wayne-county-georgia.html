📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Wayne County's mortgage landscape reflects strong financing coverage relative to local pricing. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 exceeds the $162,800 median home value by 332%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 provides 512% coverage. Both limits comfortably encompass the typical transaction, eliminating loan-limit constraints for virtually all buyers in this market. At the current 7.40% rate, monthly principal and interest payments consume a meaningful share of the $53,427 median household income. An FHA loan with 3.5% down ($157,102 loan) yields a $1,087 payment, representing 24.4% of gross monthly income. Conventional financing with 10% down ($146,520 loan) reduces the payment to $1,014, while a 20% down payment ($130,240 loan) lowers it to $901, or 20.2% of income. These ratios fall within standard underwriting thresholds, though they leave limited capacity for taxes, insurance, and existing debt obligations. Given the area's rural designation and 67.3% owner-occupancy rate, USDA financing emerges as the optimal primary option for eligible buyers, offering 100% financing with no down payment and reduced mortgage insurance costs. FHA serves as the primary alternative for those exceeding USDA income limits or purchasing non-eligible properties, requiring only $5,698 down at 3.5%. Conventional loans become advantageous only for buyers able to contribute 20% down ($32,560), eliminating private mortgage insurance entirely. The 7.40% rate environment significantly compresses purchasing power compared to the sub-4% rates prevalent during 2020-2021. A 3-percentage-point rate increase on the median loan adds approximately $300 monthly versus a 4.4% rate. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Wayne County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Wayne County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Wayne County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Wayne County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Wayne County qualifies.
