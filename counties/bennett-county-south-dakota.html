📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Bennett County is sufficient for the typical home. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $156,400 by 346%, and the conforming loan limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 532%, confirming that both programs can fully underwrite a purchase at the median price. VA and USDA loans are unnecessary since the median home price is well below their respective limits and the county is not designated high‑cost. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show a 30‑year fixed rate of 7.40% results in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $1,044 for an FHA loan requiring 3.5% down, $974 for a conventional loan with 10% down, and $866 for a conventional loan with 20% down. Compared with the median household income of $53,750 (approximately $4,479 per month), the FHA payment consumes 23.3% of monthly income, while the 20% down conventional payment consumes 19.3%, indicating a moderate but manageable burden. Given the price point and available limits, an FHA loan is likely the best fit for typical buyers, as it requires the lowest down payment and aligns with the income‑to‑payment ratio. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5% ($5,474), which may be challenging but achievable with savings or assistance; a 10% down payment ($15,640) or 20% ($31,280) would further reduce the payment‑to‑income ratio. The current 7.40% interest rate elevates the monthly obligation relative to lower‑rate environments, where the same loan at 5% would drop the payment to roughly $866, improving affordability. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize an FHA loan with the minimum 3.5% down payment to keep the payment under one‑quarter of income, or save for a larger down payment to lower the ratio and mitigate rate risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Bennett County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Bennett County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Bennett County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Bennett County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Bennett County qualifies.
