📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Metcalfe County, Kentucky, is characterized by substantial regulatory headroom relative to local property values. The 2026 FHA loan limit for a single-family home stands at $541,287, which covers approximately 491% of the median home value of $110,200. Similarly, the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds the median price by 756%. These figures indicate that no typical buyer in this market will face loan limit constraints, as even the most affordable loan programs comfortably accommodate the highest prices observed in the area. Consequently, the choice of loan type is driven by borrower qualification and down payment capacity rather than statutory caps. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable for the median household, which earns $51,473 annually. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down requires a principal and interest payment of $736 per month, equating to 17.2% of median monthly income. This ratio falls within standard underwriting guidelines, though it leaves less discretionary cash flow compared to conventional options. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $610, or 14.2% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. However, the primary barrier to entry is the initial capital required. The minimum down payment for an FHA loan is $3,857, while a conventional loan with 20% down requires $22,040. Given the median household income, saving the latter amount presents a significant multi-year hurdle, making the lower-down-payment FHA option the most accessible route for typical buyers. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows. At this rate, the monthly cost of debt is elevated, meaning that the 7.9% five-year price appreciation in the local market has outpaced the reduction in borrowing costs seen in prior years. Buyers are effectively paying more for the same square footage due to the combination of rising home values and high interest rates. Despite these pressures, the absolute dollar amounts remain low due to the county’s affordable median price. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is that while the 7.40% rate increases the total cost of financing, the low median home value ensures that monthly obligations remain a reasonable portion of income. Therefore, buyers with limited savings should prioritize FHA financing to minimize upfront capital requirements, while those with substantial liquidity may opt for conventional loans to reduce long-term interest costs and avoid mortgage insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Metcalfe County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Metcalfe County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Metcalfe County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Metcalfe County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Metcalfe County qualifies.
