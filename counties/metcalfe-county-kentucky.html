📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price in Metcalfe County. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is 4.9 times the median value of $110,200, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 7.6 times higher. Consequently, virtually any single‑family purchase in the county can be financed under either program without encountering the “high‑cost” ceiling that restricts many markets. At a 7.24 % 30‑year fixed rate, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $724 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down) and $600 for a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment. With a median household income of $51,473, the monthly income is $4,290; the FHA payment represents 16.9 % of income and the conventional 20 % payment represents 14.0 %. Both ratios fall well below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % for housing costs, indicating that the monthly debt service is manageable for a typical household. Given the low price level, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($22,040) yields the lowest P&I cost and avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums. However, the required cash outlay may be a barrier for many buyers. An FHA loan reduces the down‑payment to $3,857 (3.5 % of $110,200) and still keeps the payment under 17 % of income, making it the most accessible option for first‑time or cash‑constrained purchasers. USDA eligibility is also likely, but the analysis focuses on the two primary products. The current 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $100–$150 compared with a 5 % environment, raising the FHA payment from about $630 to $724 and the conventional 20 % payment from $540 to $600. Although the impact is noticeable, the low home price and high income‑to‑price ratio preserve overall affordability. **Practical takeaway:** Buyers who can secure a modest 3.5 % down payment can obtain an FHA loan and keep housing costs under 17 % of household income, making homeownership attainable even with today’s elevated mortgage rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Metcalfe County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Metcalfe County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Metcalfe County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Metcalfe County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Metcalfe County qualifies.
