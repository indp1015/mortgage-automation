📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Forrest County, Mississippi, presents a distinct affordability profile where federal loan limits significantly exceed local median values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 322% of the $168,000 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 496%. This substantial coverage indicates that no typical buyer in this market faces high-cost county restrictions or limit caps. Consequently, financing options are not constrained by price ceilings but rather by income and credit qualifications. The median household income of $53,640 supports these transactions, though the current interest rate environment imposes specific constraints on monthly budgeting. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the monthly principal and interest (P&I) payment for a median-priced home varies by loan type. An FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment requires a $1,122 monthly P&I payment, representing 25.1% of the median monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this burden to $930, or 20.8% of income. While the 25.1% ratio for FHA loans is near the upper threshold of standard debt-to-income guidelines, it remains within acceptable limits for many borrowers. The 20% down payment option offers greater financial cushion, requiring approximately $33,600 upfront, which may be a barrier for first-time buyers. In contrast, the $5,880 minimum down payment for an FHA loan is more accessible, leveraging the low entry requirement to facilitate homeownership despite elevated interest rates. The current 7.40% rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lower-rate environments. A 100 basis point decrease would reduce the FHA monthly P&I by approximately $70, improving the debt-to-income ratio to 23.8%. Given the 7.9% five-year price appreciation, buyers face a dual challenge of rising home values and high financing costs. However, the strong price growth since 2020 suggests a resilient local market. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is that FHA financing offers the most viable path to entry due to its low down payment requirement and high loan limit coverage. Buyers should prioritize improving credit scores to secure the best available rates within the FHA program, as even minor rate reductions yield substantial long-term savings on a $162,120 loan. The combination of low median prices and high loan limits creates a favorable structural environment, provided borrowers can manage the current interest rate burden within their income constraints.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Forrest County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Forrest County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Forrest County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Forrest County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Forrest County qualifies.
