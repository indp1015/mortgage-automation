📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Monterey County is adequate, as both the FHA 1‑Unit limit of $994,750 and the conforming loan limit of $994,750 exceed the median home value of $781,000 by 27%, satisfying the requirement that the loan amount be sufficient to purchase a typical property. Consequently, a buyer can qualify for either an FHA or a conforming loan without needing a jumbo financing solution. Monthly payment burdens are steep. For a median‑priced home, the FHA loan requiring 3.5% down results in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $5,177 per month, which equals 63.9% of the median household income of $97,230. The conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $4,291 per month, or 53.0% of income, still well above conventional affordability thresholds. These ratios indicate that even with the lower payment of the 20% down conventional loan, a large share of income would be devoted to housing costs. Given the loan limits and down‑payment requirements, a conventional loan with 10% down may offer the best balance for typical buyers, allowing a down payment of $78,100 while keeping the payment at $4,828 per month, or 49.6% of income. However, the high income‑to‑payment ratio suggests that many households will struggle to meet the payment burden at the current 7.32% interest rate. Lower rates would markedly improve affordability; for example, a 5% rate on the same 10% down loan would reduce the payment to approximately $3,800 per month, lowering the income share to roughly 39%. Additionally, the FHA loan obligates borrowers to pay mortgage insurance premiums, further increasing the effective monthly outlay beyond the $5,177 figure. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a 10% down payment to stay within the conforming limit and mitigate the high payment ratio, while monitoring interest‑rate trends to time financing decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Monterey County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Monterey County is $994,750. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Monterey County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $994,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Monterey County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Monterey County qualifies.
