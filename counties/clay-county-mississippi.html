📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Clay County, Mississippi, presents a distinct financing profile where loan limits significantly exceed local market values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 419% of the $129,100 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 645%. Consequently, loan limit constraints do not impede typical transactions in this jurisdiction. The primary financial challenge lies in the monthly payment burden relative to income. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan on the median-priced home requires a monthly principal and interest payment of $862. This amount represents 24.0% of the median household income of $43,125, placing the typical buyer near the upper threshold of conventional debt-to-income guidelines. A conventional loan with 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $715, or 19.9% of income, offering a more manageable burden. Given the low median home value and high loan limit coverage, FHA financing is likely the most accessible option for typical buyers due to its lower down payment requirements. While USDA or VA loans may offer zero-down alternatives for eligible residents, the FHA’s 3.5% down payment requirement is a realistic barrier for many. At the median price, this minimum down payment is approximately $4,519. For a household earning the median income, saving this amount is feasible but requires disciplined budgeting, especially given the median monthly rent of $817, which is nearly equivalent to the projected mortgage payment. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a rate reduction to 6.00% would lower the FHA monthly P&I by approximately $65, improving the debt-to-income ratio and enhancing purchasing power. The rapid appreciation in home values, with a 5-year CAGR of 9.7% and a 11.7% year-over-year increase in 2024, has outpaced income growth, tightening affordability margins. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing pre-approval with an FHA lender to leverage the low down payment requirement. Additionally, exploring local down payment assistance programs is essential, as the gap between the median rent and the mortgage payment is minimal, leaving little room for error in household budgeting. The combination of high loan limit coverage and low absolute home prices makes Clay County a viable market for first-time buyers, provided they can navigate the current interest rate environment and secure sufficient initial capital for closing costs and down payments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Clay County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Clay County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Clay County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Clay County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Clay County qualifies.
