📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Sangamon County, Illinois, presents a highly accessible mortgage market where federal loan limits significantly exceed local housing values. The 2026 FHA limit for a single-unit property is $541,287, which covers 307% of the median home value of $176,300. Similarly, the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 472% of the median price. Consequently, financing coverage is not a barrier for typical buyers, as the median home falls well within the eligibility thresholds for both FHA and conventional loans. This ample headroom ensures that even buyers targeting homes above the median can secure standard financing without requiring jumbo loans or high-cost alternatives. The monthly payment burden remains manageable for the median household, which earns $75,357 annually. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,177, or 18.7% of monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $976, or 15.5% of monthly income. While the current interest rate of 7.40% is higher than historical averages, the low property prices in Sangamon County mitigate this impact. For context, a 1% decrease in interest rates would lower the FHA payment by approximately $18, but the existing price-to-income ratio remains favorable compared to national averages. The minimum down payment for an FHA loan at the median price is $6,170, a feasible amount for many local households, whereas a 20% conventional down payment requires $35,260, which may present a higher barrier for first-time buyers. Given the local price points, FHA loans are likely the best fit for typical buyers seeking lower down payment requirements and flexible credit guidelines. The 3.5% down payment option allows buyers to enter the market with minimal initial capital, preserving liquidity for closing costs and moving expenses. Although conventional loans offer slightly lower monthly payments due to higher equity positions, the difference is modest in this low-price market. The primary constraint for affordability is not the loan limit or the down payment size, but rather the elevated interest rate environment. Prospective buyers should focus on competitive rate shopping, as even small reductions in the interest rate yield meaningful long-term savings on a $170,000 loan. The stability of the 5-year price CAGR at 5.3% suggests a predictable market, allowing buyers to plan finances without anticipating rapid appreciation that could outpace income growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Sangamon County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Sangamon County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Sangamon County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Sangamon County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Sangamon County qualifies.
