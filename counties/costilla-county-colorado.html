📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Costilla County’s housing market presents a distinct affordability profile where loan limits significantly exceed local property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 293% of the median home value of $184,700, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 451%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces ceiling restrictions based on property price. The primary constraint is not loan eligibility limits, but rather the ratio of monthly debt service to household income. With a median household income of $36,861, the financial burden of financing a median-priced home remains substantial despite the low absolute price point. Monthly payment burdens indicate tight margins for typical borrowers. An FHA loan requiring 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,224, consuming 39.8% of median monthly income. This exceeds the standard 36% debt-to-income threshold often preferred by lenders, suggesting that many median-income households may struggle to qualify without significant income supplementation or debt reduction. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $1,015, or 33.0% of income, which aligns better with standard underwriting guidelines. However, the required down payment of $36,940 represents a significant capital barrier for residents earning the median income. An FHA loan requires a minimum down payment of $6,465, a more accessible threshold that allows buyers to enter the market with lower initial capital. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.32% further compresses affordability. Compared to lower-rate environments, the high interest rate elevates monthly payments, making the 39.8% income allocation for FHA borrowers particularly precarious. The recent 10.1% five-year price CAGR has accelerated home values, yet income growth has not kept pace, widening the gap between asset prices and earning power. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is clear: while the median home price is low, the combination of high interest rates and median income levels makes the 20% down conventional option ideal for those with savings, but the 3.5% down FHA option is the most viable entry point for first-time buyers. However, even with FHA financing, borrowers must ensure their total debt obligations, including property taxes and insurance, do not push their total housing cost beyond sustainable levels relative to their $36,861 median income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Costilla County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Costilla County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Costilla County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Costilla County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Costilla County qualifies.
