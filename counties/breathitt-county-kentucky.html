📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both far exceed the median home price in Breathitt County. With a 2026 FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287, the limit is 770 % of the $70,300 median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 1,185 % of that price. Consequently, virtually any single‑family home in the county can be financed under either program without encountering the “high‑cost” ceiling that restricts many rural markets. At a 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $462 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $67,840 loan) and $383 for a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($56,240 loan). Relative to the median household income of $34,808 (≈$2,901 monthly), the FHA payment represents 15.9 % of monthly earnings, while a 20 % conventional payment is 13.2 % of earnings. Both ratios sit comfortably below the conventional 28 % housing‑cost threshold, indicating that, despite the high national rate, mortgage service remains affordable for a typical household in this market. Given the low price point, a conventional loan with a 10 %–20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option. The 10 % down scenario ($63,270 loan) yields a $431 P&I payment—still under 15 % of income—and avoids the upfront mortgage‑insurance premium required for FHA loans. USDA or VA financing could be attractive for eligible borrowers, but the conventional route offers the lowest overall cost because the loan‑to‑value ratio is well within the conforming ceiling and private‑mortgage‑insurance premiums are lower than FHA’s. The minimum cash outlay to purchase at the median price is 3.5 % for FHA ($2,460) or 10 % for a conventional loan ($7,030). For many local households, a 3.5 % down payment is attainable, but the 10 % conventional requirement may be a more realistic target when factoring for closing costs and reserves. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $40–$50 compared with a 5 % environment, but the impact is muted by the low loan balances. **Practical takeaway:** Buyers who can save a 10 % down payment will secure the cheapest monthly payment and avoid FHA mortgage‑insurance costs, making conventional financing the optimal choice for most purchasers in Breathitt County.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Breathitt County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Breathitt County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Breathitt County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Breathitt County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Breathitt County qualifies.
