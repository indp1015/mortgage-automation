📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $186,800 in Heard County. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is 290 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 446 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that fully finances a typical property without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo products. At a 7.24 % 30‑year fixed rate, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $1,228 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $180,262 loan) and $1,018 for a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($149,440 loan). With a median household income of $65,868, the monthly income is $5,489; the FHA payment represents 22.4 % of income and the conventional 20 % payment represents 18.5 % of income. Both ratios sit below the conventional 28 % housing‑cost threshold, indicating that the debt service burden is manageable for median earners, though the FHA option leaves less discretionary cash due to mortgage insurance premiums. For most first‑time and moderate‑income buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective choice because it eliminates FHA mortgage insurance and yields the lowest P&I. Buyers unable to meet the $37,360 (20 %) down‑payment requirement may consider an FHA loan, which requires only $6,538 (3.5 %). USDA or VA financing could be alternatives for eligible rural or veteran purchasers, but the median home price already falls well within conventional limits, reducing the need for those programs. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $150–$200 compared with a 5 % environment, shrinking affordability margins. Nevertheless, because loan limits are high relative to home values, the primary barrier remains the down payment. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving at least 10 % ($18,680) to secure a conventional loan with competitive terms, as this level balances affordability with lower ongoing costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Heard County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Heard County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Heard County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Heard County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Heard County qualifies.
