📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Chittenden County is adequate for the typical home. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the 2024 median home value of $439,200 by 23%, confirming that an FHA loan fully supports purchases at the median price. The conforming loan limit of $832,750 provides even greater coverage, representing 190% of the median value, though it is unnecessary for most buyers. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show that an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment requires a principal and interest payment of $2,934, which equals 36.4% of the median household income of $96,759. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $2,432, or 30.2% of income, a more manageable proportion. At a 7.40% interest rate, the FHA payment consumes over a third of monthly income; a 1% reduction to 6.40% would lower the payment to approximately $2,600, bringing the ratio down to about 27% of income. While the 3.5% down payment equals roughly 16% of annual household earnings, it remains attainable for households with disciplined savings, though it represents a substantial portion of their financial resources. Given these figures, the FHA loan is likely the best fit for typical buyers because it requires the lowest down payment and keeps the debt service ratio near the upper end of conventional affordability thresholds. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5% ($15,372), which is feasible for households that can accumulate this amount relative to their income. However, a 10% conventional down payment ($43,920) represents nearly half of annual earnings and may be challenging for many. The current 7.40% interest rate intensifies the payment burden; lower rates would proportionally decrease monthly obligations and improve affordability. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing or save for a larger down payment to lower the payment‑to‑income ratio, as the present rate environment makes even modest‑priced homes financially demanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Chittenden County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Chittenden County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Chittenden County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Chittenden County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Chittenden County qualifies.
