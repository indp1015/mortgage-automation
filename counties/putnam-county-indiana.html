📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both sit far above the median home price of $218,900 in Putnam County, indicating that financing capacity is not a constraint for the typical buyer. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 covers 247 % of the median price, while the conforming ceiling of $832,750 covers 380 %. Consequently, virtually any buyer who qualifies for either program can obtain a loan large enough to purchase a median‑priced home without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo financing. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($175,120 loan) is $1,186 per month, representing 18.2 % of the median household income ($78,378 annual or $6,531 monthly). An FHA loan with the minimum 3.5 % down ($211,238 loan) yields a $1,431 P&I payment, or 21.9 % of income. Both ratios remain comfortably below the conventional 28 % affordability threshold, though the FHA option consumes a larger share of earnings because of the higher loan balance and mortgage‑insurance premiums. For most first‑time and moderate‑income purchasers, a conventional loan with a 10–20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective choice. The 10 % down scenario ($197,010 loan) produces a $1,334 P&I payment (20.4 % of income) and avoids the upfront and ongoing FHA mortgage‑insurance fees. VA‑eligible borrowers would enjoy comparable or lower payments due to the absence of mortgage insurance, while USDA financing is not applicable because the county is not designated as a rural‑eligible area. The minimum cash outlay to acquire a median‑priced home is $7,662 (3.5 % FHA) or $21,890 (10 % conventional). Given the county’s median household income, a 10 % down payment is attainable for many families, whereas the FHA minimum may be more accessible for those with limited savings. Because the 7.18 % rate is roughly 1.5 percentage points higher than the 5‑year historic average, P&I payments are proportionally larger—about $150–$200 more per month than they would be at a 5.5 % rate. This rate environment modestly compresses purchasing power but does not push median‑price affordability beyond conventional limits. **Practical takeaway:** A conventional loan with a 10 % down payment allows a typical buyer to secure a median home while keeping monthly housing costs near 20 % of income, making it the most balanced option in the current high‑rate climate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Putnam County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Putnam County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Putnam County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Putnam County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Putnam County qualifies.
