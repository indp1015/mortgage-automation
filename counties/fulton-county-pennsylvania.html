📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a one‑unit property exceeds the median home value of $208,500 by 260%, confirming that the FHA program fully covers typical purchases in Fulton County. The conforming limit of $832,750 provides even greater coverage, at 399% of the median price, indicating that both FHA and conventional conforming financing are adequate for the market. Monthly payment burdens are significant relative to median household income of $65,836 (approximately $5,486 per month). The FHA‑insured loan requiring a 3.5% down payment on a $201,202 principal results in a P&I payment of $1,393, representing 25.4% of monthly income. Conventional financing with a 10% down payment on a $187,650 loan yields a $1,299 payment (23.6% of income), while a 20% down payment on a $166,800 loan produces a $1,154 payment (21.0% of income). At the current 7.40% interest rate, affordability is constrained, as the payment share exceeds the commonly recommended 28% threshold for housing costs. Given the median price and available limits, an FHA loan is likely the most suitable option for typical buyers, as it requires the lowest down payment (3.5%) and keeps the payment burden near the upper end of the affordability range. Conventional loans with 10% or 20% down remain viable for higher‑income households but demand larger upfront cash. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5% ($7,298) for FHA, 10% ($20,850) for conventional, and 20% ($41,700) for the most conservative conventional scenario. A 3.5% down payment is generally feasible for many households, though it may require savings or assistance programs. The 7.40% rate increases monthly payments compared with lower‑rate environments; a reduction to 5% would lower the FHA payment to roughly $1,060, improving the payment‑to‑income ratio. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing with the minimum 3.5% down payment to keep housing costs under 26% of their monthly income, thereby enhancing affordability in the current rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Fulton County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Fulton County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Fulton County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Fulton County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Fulton County qualifies.
