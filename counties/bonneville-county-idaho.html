📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Bonneville County’s median home value of $368,800 sits comfortably below both the 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. This ensures that the typical buyer can secure financing for a median-priced property without requiring a jumbo loan. The FHA limit covers 147% of the local median price, while the conforming limit covers 226%, indicating ample headroom for price appreciation before borrowers encounter higher-cost financing tiers. Consequently, standard government-backed and conventional loan programs remain fully accessible for the majority of the market. The primary challenge for prospective buyers lies in the monthly payment burden relative to local income levels. With a 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan with 3.5% down is $2,464 per month. This figure represents 37.4% of the median household income of $79,068, exceeding the standard 28% front-end ratio guideline for comfortable affordability. Even with a 20% down payment on a conventional loan, the payment of $2,042 still consumes 31.0% of median income. These ratios suggest that while financing is available, the debt-to-income requirements may limit eligibility for households earning near the median, particularly when factoring in property taxes, insurance, and HOA fees. Regarding loan type recommendations, conventional loans with higher down payments offer the most cost-effective long-term solution, as they avoid mortgage insurance premiums associated with FHA loans. However, for buyers with limited savings, the FHA option provides a viable entry point with a minimum down payment of $12,908 (3.5% of the median price). This amount is significantly lower than the $73,760 required for a 20% conventional down payment, making it more feasible for first-time buyers. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a 100 basis point decrease would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $120, improving the income ratio to a more manageable level. Practically, buyers in Bonneville County should prioritize increasing their down payment to reduce the loan-to-value ratio and mitigate the impact of high interest rates. Given the 18.0% five-year price CAGR, waiting for rates to drop may result in higher home prices that further strain budgets. Therefore, securing a pre-approval based on a realistic debt-to-income ratio and exploring local down payment assistance programs are essential steps for prospective buyers navigating this high-growth, high-rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Bonneville County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Bonneville County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Bonneville County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Bonneville County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Bonneville County qualifies.
