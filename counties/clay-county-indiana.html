📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $153,000 in Clay County. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 is 354 % of the median value, while the conforming ceiling of $832,750 is 544 % of that price, confirming that virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that meets the statutory maximum without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo financing. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) obligation for a median‑priced home is $1,006 per month with an FHA loan (3.5 % down) and $834 per month with a conventional loan funded at 20 % down. Relative to the median household income of $70,010 (≈$5,834 monthly), the FHA payment represents 17.2 % of income and the conventional‑20 % payment 14.3 %. Both ratios sit below the conventional 28 % front‑end affordability threshold, indicating that, despite the elevated rate, the monthly burden remains moderate for a typical household. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 10–20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option because it avoids the FHA mortgage insurance premium and yields a lower P&I payment ($938 at 10 % down, $834 at 20 % down). The minimum cash outlay to purchase at the median price is $5,355 (3.5 % down for FHA) or $15,300 (10 % down for conventional). Given the county’s median household income, a 10 % down payment is generally attainable, whereas the 3.5 % FHA requirement is even less restrictive but carries ongoing insurance costs. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $150–$200 compared with a 5 % environment, yet the low median home price and high owner‑occupancy rate (80.9 %) keep overall affordability within acceptable limits. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers who can allocate a 10 % down payment should prioritize conventional financing, as it delivers the lowest monthly cost while staying well under the 28 % income‑to‑housing ratio, even with today’s 7.24 % mortgage rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Clay County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Clay County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Clay County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Clay County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Clay County qualifies.
