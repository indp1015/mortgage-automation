📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Terry County, Texas, presents a distinct disconnect between national loan limits and local market realities. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 514% of the median home value of $105,400, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 790%. Consequently, loan limit constraints are not a barrier to entry for typical buyers. The primary challenge lies in debt-to-income ratios rather than maximum loan caps. With a median household income of $44,100, the estimated monthly principal and interest (P&I) for an FHA loan at 3.5% down is $704, representing 19.2% of monthly income. This figure sits comfortably within the standard 28% housing expense ratio, indicating that the median-priced home remains financially accessible for the average household in the county. Regarding loan type recommendations, FHA financing is likely the most suitable option for typical buyers due to its low down payment requirement. A 3.5% down payment on the median home requires only $3,689, a significantly lower barrier than the 20% down payment of $21,080 required for a conventional loan with no private mortgage insurance. While USDA loans may be available given the rural designation, the low entry cost of FHA makes it the most accessible entry point for first-time buyers. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% increases the monthly payment burden compared to historical lows, but the low absolute price point of the home mitigates this impact. For instance, a conventional loan with 20% down results in a $583 monthly P&I payment, which is only 15.9% of median monthly income. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that affordability in Terry County is driven by low home values rather than favorable interest rates. Even with rates above 7%, the small loan amounts keep monthly obligations manageable. Buyers should focus on securing pre-approval for FHA or USDA loans to capitalize on minimal down payment requirements. The recent 5.2% year-over-year decline in median home values, dropping from $111,200 in 2023 to $105,400 in 2024, further improves affordability ratios. This price correction, combined with stable incomes, suggests that the market remains resilient. Buyers should monitor the 5-year price CAGR of 9.2%, which indicates significant historical appreciation, but the current plateau suggests a more stable environment for entry. Overall, the combination of low median prices and generous loan limits creates a favorable environment for homeownership, provided buyers manage their overall debt loads carefully to maintain healthy debt-to-income ratios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Terry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Terry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Terry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Terry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Terry County qualifies.
