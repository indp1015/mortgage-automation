📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Blount County’s mortgage financing landscape is characterized by ample loan limit coverage relative to local property values. The median home value of $175,200 sits well below both the FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. Specifically, the FHA limit covers 309% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 475%. This significant buffer ensures that virtually all standard residential transactions in the county can be financed through government-backed or conventional channels without requiring jumbo loan structures. Consequently, financing availability is not a constraint for typical buyers, allowing for a broad range of loan options based on individual credit profiles and down payment capabilities. The primary challenge for affordability stems from current interest rates rather than loan limits. With the 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, the principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on the median home is approximately $1,170 per month. This figure represents 21.9% of the median monthly household income, derived from an annual median income of $64,190. While this ratio is within the general 28% debt-to-income guideline, it leaves limited room for property taxes, insurance, and other living expenses. In contrast, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $970, or 18.1% of income. The 2022 price surge of 15.7% has compressed affordability margins, making the current rate environment significantly more demanding than previous years. For most buyers in Blount County, FHA loans offer the most accessible entry point due to the low 3.5% down payment requirement, amounting to roughly $6,132 for a median-priced home. This barrier is more feasible for first-time buyers compared to the $35,044 required for a 20% conventional down payment. However, buyers with stronger credit and savings may find that conventional loans offer better long-term value by avoiding mortgage insurance premiums. The 7.40% rate significantly impacts total cost over time; a buyer should carefully assess their debt-to-income ratio to ensure that the higher monthly outlay does not strain household cash flow. Ultimately, the combination of low home prices and high loan limits creates a favorable structural environment, but the high interest rate necessitates prudent budgeting to maintain financial stability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Blount County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Blount County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Blount County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Blount County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Blount County qualifies.
