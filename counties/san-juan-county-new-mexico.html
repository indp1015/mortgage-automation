📝 Mortgage Market Notes

San Juan County’s housing market exhibits robust financing coverage, with the 2026 FHA single-family limit of $541,287 covering 279% of the median home value of $193,800. The conforming limit of $832,750 provides even greater headroom, covering 430% of the median price. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces loan limit constraints, ensuring that standard financing products remain accessible regardless of property value fluctuations. The county is not designated as a high-cost area, further simplifying the underwriting process for typical residential transactions. The primary challenge for prospective buyers lies in monthly payment affordability relative to local income levels. With a median household income of $55,872, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on a median-priced home is $1,294, representing 27.8% of monthly income. This figure sits near the upper threshold of conventional debt-to-income guidelines. In contrast, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment requires a minimum outlay of $38,760, resulting in a lower monthly payment of $1,073, or 23.0% of income. While the FHA option offers a significantly lower barrier to entry with a 3.5% down payment of $6,783, the higher interest rate and mandatory mortgage insurance increase the total cost of ownership. For typical buyers, FHA financing is likely the most practical fit, as it minimizes upfront capital requirements while keeping the loan amount well within the $541,287 limit. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows. Had rates been lower, the monthly burden would decrease, but at current levels, the payment strain is notable. The 5-year price CAGR of 6.2% indicates a steadily appreciating market, which may erode affordability if incomes do not keep pace. For prospective buyers, the concrete takeaway is that while the median home value of $193,800 is well below financing limits, the combination of rising prices and elevated interest rates necessitates careful budgeting. Buyers should prioritize saving for a larger down payment to reduce the loan principal, thereby lowering the monthly payment percentage and improving overall financial resilience in this moderately priced but rapidly appreciating market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in San Juan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in San Juan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in San Juan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in San Juan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm San Juan County qualifies.
