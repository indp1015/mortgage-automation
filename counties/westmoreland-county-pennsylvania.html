📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Westmoreland County is adequate for the typical home. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds 266 % of the median home value of $203,500, while the conforming 1‑unit limit of $832,750 covers more than 409 % of the median price, confirming that both programs can underwrite a standard purchase. Monthly payment burden is significant but manageable. For a median‑priced home, an FHA loan requiring 3.5 % down results in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $1,359 per month, representing 22.0 % of the median household income of $74,109. A conventional loan with 20 % down lowers the payment to $1,127, or 18.2 % of income, indicating that even with a modest down payment the debt service remains within a large share of earnings. Current 30‑year fixed rates of 7.40 % therefore increase the payment burden compared with lower‑rate environments, where the same loan would cost roughly $1,050 per month at 5 %. Given local prices and limits, an FHA loan is likely the best fit for typical buyers because it requires the lowest down payment and fully covers the median home price. Conventional financing with 10 % down is also viable for those able to save $20,350, while VA and USDA options are not quantified here but would similarly benefit from the low price point. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5 % ($7,123) for FHA or 10 % ($20,350) for conventional; both amounts are challenging but not impossible, especially with assistance programs. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing a loan that keeps the monthly P&I payment near 20 % of income, as the current 7.40 % rate pushes the FHA payment to that level, highlighting the need for careful budgeting and exploration of down‑payment assistance. Buyers should also monitor rate changes, as even a 0.25 % shift can alter monthly costs by over $50.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Westmoreland County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Westmoreland County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Westmoreland County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Westmoreland County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Westmoreland County qualifies.
