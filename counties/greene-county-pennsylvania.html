📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Greene County’s housing market exhibits strong financing coverage relative to federal loan limits. The median home value of $153,900 is significantly below both the FHA limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. Specifically, the FHA limit covers 352% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 541%. Consequently, no buyer in this market will encounter loan limit constraints for a typical single-family purchase. This ample headroom ensures that standard financing options remain accessible regardless of minor price fluctuations, as the local market remains far below high-cost county thresholds. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down on a median-priced home results in a principal and interest payment of $1,028 per month. This figure represents 18.1% of the median household income of $68,041, which sits comfortably within standard debt-to-income guidelines. For buyers opting for conventional financing with 20% down, the payment drops to $852, or 15.0% of income. Even with the higher down payment requirement, the conventional option offers a lower monthly cash flow, though the FHA route provides a lower initial capital barrier. The 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a 100 basis point decrease would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $60, illustrating the sensitivity of long-term debt to current rate environments. Regarding loan type recommendations, the FHA loan is likely the most suitable fit for typical buyers in Greene County. The minimum down payment of 3.5% equates to $5,387 on a median-priced home. This amount is feasible for many households, especially given the 77.1% owner-occupancy rate which suggests a stable base of current homeowners who may have accumulated equity. While USDA loans may be available depending on specific rural designations within the county, the FHA’s broad availability and low entry barrier make it the primary tool for first-time buyers. Conventional loans become more attractive only for buyers with substantial savings for a 10% or 20% down payment. The primary practical takeaway is that while interest rates are elevated, the low absolute price point of $153,900 keeps monthly obligations low. Buyers should prioritize building a down payment to at least 3.5% to unlock FHA financing, as the low home prices mitigate the impact of high interest rates on overall affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Greene County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Greene County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Greene County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Greene County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Greene County qualifies.
