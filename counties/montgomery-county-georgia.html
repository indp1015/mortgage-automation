📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Montgomery County’s median home value of $115,500 is well within both the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750, providing ample financing coverage. The FHA limit exceeds the median price by 469%, while the conforming limit exceeds it by 721%, indicating that neither limit constrains typical borrowing. For a median‑priced home, the estimated monthly principal‑and‑interest payments are $759 with a 3.5% down FHA loan, $708 with a 10% conventional loan, and $629 with a 20% conventional loan. These payments represent 17.5% and 14.5% of the median household income of $51,941, respectively, suggesting that even the higher FHA payment remains within conventional debt‑to‑income guidelines. Given the modest price point and the availability of low‑down‑payment options, an FHA loan is likely the best fit for typical buyers, as it requires only a 3.5% down payment ($4,042.5) and covers the full median price without exceeding the FHA limit. Conventional loans with 10% or 20% down are also viable, but the 20% requirement of $23,100 may pose a higher barrier for first‑time purchasers. The current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24% elevates monthly payments relative to lower‑rate environments; for example, a $111,458 FHA loan at 5% would reduce the P&I payment by roughly $150, improving affordability. The high rate also narrows the gap between FHA and conventional options, making the lower down‑payment route more attractive despite the higher interest cost. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing, as the 3.5% down requirement preserves cash while keeping the monthly payment under 18% of median income, even in the current high‑rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Montgomery County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Montgomery County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Montgomery County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Montgomery County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Montgomery County qualifies.
