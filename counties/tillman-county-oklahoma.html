📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Tillman County’s housing market presents a unique financing profile where federal loan limits significantly exceed local median home values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 678% of the median home value of $79,800, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 1044%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces a loan limit constraint, as even the most expensive typical transactions fall well below both thresholds. This ample coverage ensures that standard financing products are fully accessible to all potential purchasers, eliminating the need for jumbo loan options or high-cost county adjustments. Regarding monthly payment burden, the estimated principal and interest for a median-priced home remains low relative to local earnings. With a median household income of $48,939, an FHA loan at 3.5% down results in a monthly P&I payment of $533, representing 13.1% of income. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $442, or 10.8% of income. These figures sit comfortably within standard debt-to-income guidelines, suggesting strong baseline affordability despite the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%. The high owner-occupancy rate of 72.8% further indicates that local income levels generally support homeownership costs. For loan type recommendations, FHA loans are likely the best fit for typical buyers due to their lower down payment requirement of 3.5%. At the median price, this requires a minimum down payment of $2,793, a barrier that is far more feasible for households earning the median income than the $15,960 required for a 20% conventional down payment. While USDA loans may also be available given the rural population of 6,910, FHA offers broader lender participation and flexibility. Rate sensitivity remains a factor; the current 7.40% rate increases monthly costs compared to historical lows, yet the low absolute home prices mitigate this impact. The rapid price appreciation, with an 8.1% five-year CAGR, has pushed values up from $54,100 in 2017 to $79,800 in 2024. However, even with this growth, the payment burden remains manageable. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that the primary barrier to entry is not the loan limit or monthly payment capacity, but rather the initial cash required for closing costs and the down payment. Given the low median price, securing a modest amount of savings for the 3.5% FHA down payment and closing costs is the critical step to accessing this affordable market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Tillman County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Tillman County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Tillman County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Tillman County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Tillman County qualifies.
