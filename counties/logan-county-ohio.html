📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Logan County, Ohio, presents a robust financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local median home values. The 2026 FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 covers 266% of the $203,200 median home price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 410%. Consequently, virtually all residential transactions in the county fall well within standard agency and conforming guidelines, eliminating high-cost county surcharges or jumbo loan complexities. This ample coverage ensures that typical buyers face no structural barriers based on property value, allowing for straightforward underwriting processes across major loan types. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though interest rates exert significant pressure on cash flow. With a current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on a median-priced home is $1,357 per month. This amount represents 22.8% of the median household income of $71,551, a ratio that aligns with standard debt-to-income guidelines but leaves limited margin for additional housing costs. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down requires a $1,125 monthly payment, or 18.9% of income. The substantial price appreciation, driven by a 9.8% five-year CAGR, has increased entry costs, yet the high owner-occupancy rate of 77.6% suggests sustained local demand supports these financing structures. For the typical buyer, conventional financing is likely the most efficient option, provided they can secure a 20% down payment of $40,640. While this barrier is higher than the FHA minimum of 3.5% ($7,112), it avoids mortgage insurance premiums that would increase the effective monthly cost of the cheaper FHA option. Buyers with limited savings may prefer FHA loans to minimize upfront capital requirements, accepting the higher ongoing monthly burden. The current 7.40% rate significantly reduces purchasing power compared to lower-rate environments; a drop to 6.0% would reduce the FHA payment by approximately $160 monthly. Prospective buyers should prioritize building down payment reserves to access conventional rates, as the 3.9% difference in total monthly outlay between 3.5% and 20% down conventional loans offers long-term savings that outweigh the initial liquidity constraint.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Logan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Logan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Logan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Logan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Logan County qualifies.
