Mortgage Guide: Marion County, Arkansas

In Marion County, Arkansas, the 2026 FHA loan limit for a single-family home is $541,287, and the conforming loan limit is $832,750. The median home value in this county is $163,300, and median household income is $48,619.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Marion County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Marion County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Marion County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Marion County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Marion County qualifies.
