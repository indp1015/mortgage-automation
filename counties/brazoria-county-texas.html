📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Brazoria County’s housing market presents a favorable financing landscape, with loan limits significantly exceeding the median home value. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 179% of the median home price of $301,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 276% of that value. Consequently, no typical buyer in this county faces high-cost loan restrictions or limit constraints for standard single-family purchases. This ample coverage ensures that financing availability is not a primary barrier to entry for the median-priced home, allowing borrowers access to a wide spectrum of loan products without requiring jumbo financing. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable for the median household, though the current interest rate environment exerts pressure on affordability. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment results in a principal and interest payment of $2,015 per month. This amount represents 24.7% of the median household income of $97,993, which is near the upper threshold of conventional debt-to-income ratios. Conversely, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers the monthly P&I to $1,670, or 20.5% of income. For the typical buyer, the FHA option offers the lowest barrier to entry, requiring only $10,556 in down payment. This is feasible for many households, although it is notably higher than the $30,160 required for a 10% conventional down payment. Given the 12.9% five-year price CAGR, equity accumulation is robust, but entry costs have risen sharply since 2017 when the median value was $164,300. The 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts purchasing power compared to historical lows. At this rate, a borrower’s maximum affordable loan amount is constrained by their debt service capacity rather than local price ceilings. Prospective buyers should prioritize maximizing their down payment to reduce the principal balance, as each additional dollar of equity directly lowers the monthly obligation. For instance, moving from a 3.5% to a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I by $345, a substantial relief for households near the 25% income threshold. Furthermore, buyers should evaluate whether the lower upfront cost of an FHA loan justifies the higher monthly outlay over the long term, particularly given the stable income levels in the county. The practical takeaway is that while Brazoria County remains accessible due to generous loan limits, the current interest rate environment necessitates careful budgeting and a strategic approach to down payment sizing to maintain sustainable debt levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Brazoria County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Brazoria County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Brazoria County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Brazoria County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Brazoria County qualifies.
