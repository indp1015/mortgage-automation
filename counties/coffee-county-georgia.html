📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $128,900 in Coffee County. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is 4.2 times higher, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 6.5 times higher, confirming that financing capacity is not a constraint for a typical purchase; the median home sits well within the maximum loan size for any conventional or government‑backed program. At a 7.24 % 30‑year fixed rate, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) obligation for a median‑priced home ranges from $702 (20 % conventional down) to $847 (FHA with 3.5 % down). With a median household income of $50,683, the monthly gross income is $4,224. The FHA payment consumes 20.1 % of that income, while a conventional loan with 20 % equity requires only 16.6 %. Both ratios fall below the conventional 28 % affordability threshold, indicating that, despite the high rate, the payment burden remains moderate for an average household. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 10–20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective choice. The FHA’s lower down‑payment requirement (3.5 %) yields a higher monthly payment and adds mortgage‑insurance premiums, while the conforming limit is ample to support a conventional loan. USDA eligibility may apply in rural sections, but the median price already fits comfortably within conventional parameters, making USDA less compelling. VA financing, where available, would further reduce cash outlay, but its applicability is limited to eligible veterans. The minimum cash needed to acquire a median home is $4,512 (3.5 % FHA) or $12,890 (10 % conventional). For a household earning $50,683, a 3.5 % down payment represents 8.9 % of annual income, a level that many first‑time buyers can amass through savings or assistance programs; the 10 % conventional requirement rises to 25 % of annual income, presenting a more substantial barrier. Because the 7.24 % rate is roughly 1.5 percentage points above the 2022‑2023 low‑rate environment, monthly P&I costs are about 10 % higher than they would be at a 5.7 % rate. Nonetheless, the low median home price and strong income‑to‑price ratio keep overall affordability within acceptable limits. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers can secure a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment and still keep housing costs under 18 % of gross income, making conventional financing the most balanced option in Coffee County’s current market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Coffee County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Coffee County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Coffee County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Coffee County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Coffee County qualifies.
