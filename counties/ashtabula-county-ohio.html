📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Ashtabula County’s median home price of $159,500 is well within the reach of both FHA and conforming loan programs: the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 covers 339 % of the median price, while the conforming 1‑unit limit of $832,750 covers 522 %. With a 30‑year fixed rate of 7.40 %, the estimated principal‑and‑interest payment on a median‑priced home using an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $153,918 loan) is $1,065 per month, which equals 22.1 % of the median household’s monthly income of $4,811. A conventional loan with 20 % down ($127,600 loan) reduces the payment to $883 per month, or 18.4 % of income, and a 10 % down conventional loan costs $993 per month, representing 20.6 % of income. Even at the current rate, these payments approach or exceed the 30 % affordability threshold, indicating that higher rates diminish purchasing power relative to lower‑rate environments. The 5‑year price CAGR of 8.5 % per year further underscores the rapid appreciation, making current financing conditions especially relevant for prospective purchasers. Given the modest price point, an FHA loan with the low 3.5 % down payment appears most suitable for typical buyers, as the required $5,581 down payment equals roughly one month’s income and keeps the payment at 22 % of monthly earnings. A conventional 10 % down payment would demand $15,950, or 27.6 % of monthly income, presenting a greater barrier, while a 20 % down conventional loan reduces the payment to $883 per month, or 18.4 % of income, but requires a $31,900 down payment that is difficult for many households. The higher 7.40 % rate therefore increases the monthly cost by about 30 % compared with a 5 % rate, which would lower the FHA payment to approximately $820 and improve affordability. Prospective buyers should therefore prioritize saving for the minimum FHA down payment or consider a larger conventional down payment to bring the housing cost below 30 % of income, ensuring the home remains financially sustainable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Ashtabula County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Ashtabula County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Ashtabula County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Ashtabula County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Ashtabula County qualifies.
