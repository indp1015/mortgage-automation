📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both far exceed the median home price in Tipton County. The 2026 FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is **310 %** of the median value ($174,600), while the conforming limit of $832,750 is **477 %** of that price, confirming that virtually any single‑family purchase in the county can be financed under either program without hitting loan‑size caps. At a 30‑year fixed rate of **7.18 %**, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) component for a median‑priced home is $1,141 with a 3.5 % FHA down payment ($168,489 loan) and $946 with a 20 % conventional down payment ($139,680 loan). The FHA payment represents **18.0 %** of the median household income ($76,156/12 ≈ $6,346 per month), while a 20 % conventional payment is **14.9 %** of income. Both ratios sit comfortably below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % of monthly income for housing costs, indicating that, despite the high absolute rate, the low median price keeps debt service manageable for most households. For typical buyers, a conventional loan with a 10–20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option. The FHA’s lower down‑payment requirement (3.5 %) reduces upfront cash needs but adds mortgage‑insurance premiums that raise the effective monthly cost. USDA eligibility is limited to rural tracts and may not apply to the county’s more populated areas, while VA financing is only available to qualified veterans. Consequently, conventional financing, especially with a 20 % down payment, yields the lowest P&I and the most favorable loan‑to‑value ratio. The minimum cash outlay to acquire a median home is therefore $5,238 (3.5 % of $174,600) for FHA or $17,460 (10 % down) for a conventional loan. Given the county’s median household income and a relatively high owner‑occupancy rate (81.4 %), a 10 % down payment is realistic for many families, while a 20 % payment may be aspirational but achievable for higher‑income earners. Because the current 7.18 % rate is substantially above the historic low‑rate environment of the early 2020s, monthly payments are roughly 15 % higher than they would be at a 5.5 % rate. Nevertheless, the strong price‑to‑income ratio ensures that affordability remains intact. **Practical takeaway:** prospective buyers should target a conventional loan with at least a 10 % down payment; this balances modest cash requirements with the lowest possible monthly payment in the present high‑rate market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Tipton County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Tipton County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Tipton County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Tipton County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Tipton County qualifies.
