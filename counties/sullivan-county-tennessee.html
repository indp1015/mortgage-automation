📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Sullivan County’s median home value of $213,300 sits comfortably within both federal financing limits, ensuring broad access to standard mortgage products. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 254% of the local median price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 390%. Consequently, no buyer purchasing a typical property faces loan limit restrictions, allowing for flexible financing choices without the need for jumbo loans or high-cost county adjustments. This ample coverage indicates that the primary barrier to entry is not loan eligibility caps, but rather qualification metrics tied to income and credit. Monthly payment burdens remain significant relative to local earnings. With a 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment results in a principal and interest payment of $1,425 per month. This represents 29.1% of the median household income of $58,807, approaching the upper threshold of conventional debt-to-income ratios. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers this burden to $1,181, or 24.1% of median income. The high interest rate environment exacerbates these costs compared to historical lows, where payments might have been 30-40% lower. Therefore, while the FHA option minimizes upfront capital requirements, it maximizes monthly cash flow strain. Conversely, the conventional path requires substantial upfront equity but offers better long-term monthly affordability. For typical buyers, the FHA loan is likely the most accessible entry point due to its low 3.5% down payment requirement, which amounts to approximately $7,466 at the median price. This is a feasible barrier for many households, especially when compared to the $42,660 required for a 20% conventional down payment. However, the high monthly payment as a percentage of income suggests that buyers may need to supplement their income or seek co-signers to meet lender requirements. The rapid appreciation of 10.4% annually over the past five years has outpaced income growth, tightening affordability despite stable loan limits. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a larger down payment to reduce monthly obligations, as the current 7.40% rate makes high-leverage financing particularly expensive over the life of the loan. Ultimately, the combination of strong price growth and elevated interest rates necessitates careful budgeting to ensure sustainable homeownership in Sullivan County.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Sullivan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Sullivan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Sullivan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Sullivan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Sullivan County qualifies.
