📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Henry County, Ohio, presents a distinct financing profile where loan limits significantly exceed local price points. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 321% of the median home value of $168,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 494%. Consequently, virtually all residential properties in this market fall well within standard financing caps, eliminating high-cost county restrictions and ensuring broad access to federal and conventional loan programs. This ample coverage means that price-driven financing constraints are non-existent for the typical buyer. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. With a median household income of $80,099, an FHA loan requiring 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,126, or 16.9% of monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $933, or 14.0% of income. Both scenarios fall within standard debt-to-income guidelines, though the lower down payment requirement of FHA loans offers greater initial accessibility. Given the low median home price, USDA loans may also be viable for eligible rural areas, though FHA and conventional options provide the most straightforward paths for the majority of residents. The primary barrier to entry is the upfront capital required for down payments. For a median-priced home, the minimum FHA down payment is approximately $5,901, while a conventional 20% down payment requires $33,720. The FHA threshold is highly feasible for households with moderate savings, whereas the conventional option demands significantly more liquidity. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% increases the monthly cost compared to historical lows, but the impact is mitigated by the low absolute loan amounts. A rate of 7.40% on a $162,699 loan results in a manageable payment, whereas higher-priced markets would see a more severe affordability squeeze under the same rate environment. Prospective buyers in Henry County should prioritize FHA financing to minimize initial cash outlays, as the 3.5% down payment requirement preserves liquidity for closing costs and reserves. The combination of low home values and high loan limit coverage ensures that financing availability is not a constraint. Instead, the focus for buyers should shift to securing the necessary down payment funds and maintaining a debt profile that supports the 16.9% payment-to-income ratio, which remains comfortably within lender approval thresholds despite current interest rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Henry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Henry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Henry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Henry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Henry County qualifies.
