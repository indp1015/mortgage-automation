📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both far exceed Calhoun County’s median home price of $81,000. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 represents 668 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 1,028 % of that price, confirming that virtually any single‑family home in the county can be financed under either program without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo products. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $78,165 loan) is $529 per month, or 13.5 % of the median household income ($46,940 / 12 = $3,912). A conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($64,800 loan) yields a $438 P&I payment, equal to 11.2 % of monthly income. Both ratios sit comfortably below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % of gross income, indicating that the debt‑service burden is modest for median earners. Given the low price point, a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment ($72,900 loan, $493 P&I) is typically the most cost‑effective option because it avoids the FHA mortgage‑insurance premium while still delivering a payment well under the 28 % ceiling. USDA eligibility is also likely, but the marginal benefit over a low‑down‑payment conventional loan is minimal in this price range. VA financing would be optimal for eligible veterans, offering zero‑down terms and comparable P&I. The minimum cash outlay to purchase a median‑priced home is $2,835 (3.5 % FHA) or $8,100 (10 % conventional). For households earning the median income, a 3.5 % down payment represents roughly 7 % of annual earnings and is generally attainable, whereas a 20 % down payment ($16,200) would require saving 34 % of yearly income, posing a more significant barrier for many buyers. The 7.18 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $70–$80 relative to a 5 % environment, but because the loan amounts are small, the absolute impact remains limited. **Practical takeaway:** most median‑income buyers can secure a conventional loan with 10 % down and keep housing costs under 15 % of income, making homeownership financially realistic despite the elevated national rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Calhoun County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Calhoun County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Calhoun County qualifies.
