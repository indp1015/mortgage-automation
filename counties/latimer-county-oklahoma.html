📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750 both far exceed the median home price in Latimer County of $107,900, covering 502 % and 772 % of that value respectively, confirming that financing coverage is adequate for typical purchases. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show an FHA‑insured loan requiring a $104,124 principal and interest payment of $720 per month, which equals 20.9 % of the median household income of $41,405. A conventional loan with 20 % down on a $86,320 loan yields a $597 monthly payment, or 17.3 % of income, while a 10 % down conventional loan at $97,110 results in a $672 payment, or 19.4 % of income, which indicates that even the most conservative payment remains well below the 30 % threshold commonly used to assess affordability. At a 5 % rate the FHA payment would drop to roughly $580 per month, and at 6 % it would be about $650, showing that lower rates markedly improve affordability. Additionally, VA and USDA financing are not required for typical purchases because FHA already satisfies the loan limits and offers the lowest down payment. Given the price level and the availability of FHA financing with a low 3.5 % down payment, FHA loans are likely the best fit for typical buyers, as they require the smallest upfront cash outlay and stay within the county’s loan limits. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5 % ($3,777), which is generally feasible for households earning the median income, whereas a 10 % down payment ($10,790) or 20 % ($21,580) presents a greater barrier. Prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing with the 3.5 % down payment to keep monthly costs under 22 % of income, making homeownership attainable despite the elevated interest environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Latimer County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Latimer County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Latimer County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Latimer County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Latimer County qualifies.
