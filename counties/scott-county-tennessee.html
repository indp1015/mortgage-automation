📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Scott County, Tennessee, presents a distinct financing landscape where loan limits significantly exceed local property values, ensuring broad access to standard mortgage products. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 433% of the median home value of $124,900, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 667%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces financing constraints due to loan caps. The primary barrier is not eligibility but rather the cost of capital and down payment requirements. With a median household income of $44,711, the monthly principal and interest (P&I) payment for an FHA loan at the median price is $834, representing 22.4% of monthly income. This ratio sits comfortably within traditional debt-to-income guidelines, suggesting that the median-priced home remains financially accessible to local residents despite the current interest rate environment. Regarding loan type recommendations, the FHA option offers the lowest barrier to entry with a 3.5% down payment, requiring approximately $4,372. This is substantially more feasible for buyers with limited savings compared to the 20% down payment of $24,980 required for a conventional loan, which would reduce the monthly P&I to $691. While conventional loans offer lower monthly payments, the upfront capital requirement is a significant hurdle for households earning the median income. Therefore, FHA financing is likely the most practical fit for typical buyers in Scott County, balancing manageable down payments with acceptable monthly obligations. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% increases the total cost of borrowing compared to historical lows, but the low absolute price of the median home mitigates this impact. A 100 basis point decrease in rates would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $50, yet the current 22.4% income ratio indicates that affordability remains robust even at higher rates. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that the low median home value acts as a buffer against high interest rates. Because the loan amounts are small relative to national averages, the monthly payment burden remains proportionate to local incomes. Buyers should prioritize securing FHA pre-approval to leverage the low down payment requirement, as the $4,372 entry cost is far more attainable than conventional alternatives. Furthermore, the 7.6% five-year price CAGR suggests that while entry costs are rising, the gap between loan limits and home values continues to widen, maintaining long-term financing flexibility. Ultimately, the combination of low property values and high loan limits ensures that Scott County remains one of the more accessible markets for first-time homebuyers in the region, provided they can navigate the initial down payment hurdle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Scott County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Scott County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Scott County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Scott County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Scott County qualifies.
