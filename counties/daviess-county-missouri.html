📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Daviess County, Missouri, presents a highly accessible financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local market values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 335% of the median home value of $161,800, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 515%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces loan limit constraints, ensuring that financing availability is not a barrier to entry. This ample coverage allows for standard underwriting processes without the need for jumbo loan structures or high-cost county adjustments, which are inapplicable here. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though interest rates have tightened affordability margins. With a 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, an FHA loan on the median-priced home requires a minimum down payment of $5,663 (3.5%), resulting in a principal and interest payment of $1,081. This represents 20.3% of the median household income of $63,984. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $896, or 16.8% of income. While both figures fall within general debt-to-income guidelines, the 7.40% rate significantly increases monthly costs compared to prior lower-rate environments, effectively reducing purchasing power by approximately $15,000 to $20,000 in total loan capacity for the same monthly budget. For typical buyers, the FHA loan is likely the most practical option due to its low down payment requirement and broad availability in this non-high-cost area. The minimum down payment of $5,663 is feasible for households saving modestly, as it requires less than one month of median income. However, buyers should note that while the county’s 9.9% five-year price CAGR indicates strong appreciation, the current rate environment demands careful budgeting. A concrete insight for prospective buyers is that securing a 20% down payment, rather than the FHA minimum, reduces the monthly payment by $185 and lowers total interest paid over the life of the loan, providing a tangible buffer against future rate volatility or income fluctuations. This strategy aligns with the high owner-occupancy rate of 79.1%, suggesting that long-term stability is a primary driver for local residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Daviess County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Daviess County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Daviess County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Daviess County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Daviess County qualifies.
