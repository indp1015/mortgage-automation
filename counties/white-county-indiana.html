📝 Mortgage Market Notes

White County's financing infrastructure provides ample capacity for local buyers. The FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 covers 308% of the $175,900 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 473%, eliminating loan-limit constraints for virtually all single-family purchases. This coverage depth means buyers face no artificial ceiling on government-backed or conventional financing. At the current 7.40% rate, monthly principal and interest payments consume a meaningful but manageable share of median household income ($67,303 annually, or $5,609 monthly). An FHA loan with 3.5% down ($6,157) yields a $1,175 payment representing 21.0% of gross monthly income. Conventional financing with 10% down ($17,590) reduces the payment to $1,096 (19.5%), while 20% down ($35,180) brings it to $974 (17.4%). All three scenarios fall well within the 28% front-end DTI guideline, though the FHA option approaches the threshold when taxes and insurance are added. Given the median price point and strong loan-limit coverage, conventional financing with 5–10% down emerges as the optimal primary-residence choice for borrowers with credit scores above 680, avoiding FHA's lifetime mortgage insurance premium. USDA loans warrant consideration for eligible rural properties, offering 0% down and reduced guarantee fees. VA loans provide the strongest value for qualified veterans. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in White County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in White County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in White County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in White County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm White County qualifies.
