📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Maui County’s housing market presents significant financing challenges, primarily driven by the divergence between median home values and federal loan limits. The 2026 FHA limit for single-family homes is $844,675, which covers only 93% of the $904,700 median home value. Consequently, the typical median-priced property exceeds the FHA ceiling, forcing most buyers to seek conforming loans. The conforming limit of $1,299,500 adequately covers the median price, providing a 144% coverage ratio. However, because Maui is designated a high-cost county, the conforming limit offers a broader safety margin than the FHA limit, making conventional financing the primary viable option for median-priced purchases. The monthly payment burden remains severe relative to local income levels. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment on the median home results in a principal and interest payment of $5,011. This amount consumes 61.9% of the median household income of $97,161. Even with a larger down payment, the debt-to-income ratio remains well above the standard 28% threshold for front-end ratio qualification. For buyers attempting to use an FHA loan, the payment burden is even higher at 74.6% of monthly income, assuming the home price is adjusted to fit within the limit. The minimum down payment for a conforming loan at the median price is approximately $180,940 (20% for optimal rates, or 5% for minimums), representing a substantial capital barrier that is difficult for median-income households to accumulate. The current interest rate environment of 7.40% significantly exacerbates affordability issues compared to historical lower-rate periods. A 100 basis point decrease in rates would reduce the monthly payment by roughly $350, yet the structural gap between income and housing costs persists. The 5-year price CAGR of 9.7% indicates that home values have outpaced income growth, further straining affordability. For prospective buyers, the practical takeaway is that purchasing at the median price requires either a down payment exceeding $180,000 or a household income significantly above the median to qualify for standard underwriting guidelines. Buyers with incomes near the median may need to target properties below the $844,675 FHA limit to access lower down payment options, though such inventory may be limited in the current market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Maui County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Maui County is $1,299,500. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Maui County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,299,500 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Maui County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Maui County qualifies.
