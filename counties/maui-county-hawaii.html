📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits for Maui County are both $1,299,500 for a single‑unit residence, which is **144 % of the 2024 median home value of $904,700**. Consequently, the typical home is comfortably within the maximum loan size for both programs; borrowers can finance the entire purchase without needing a jumbo loan, and the limits do not pose a barrier for median‑priced properties. At a 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $5,949 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $873,036 loan) and $4,932 for a conventional loan with 20 % down ($723,760 loan). With a median household income of $97,161, the monthly income is $8,097. The FHA payment therefore consumes **73.5 % of monthly income**, while a conventional 20 % down payment consumes **60.9 %**. Both ratios exceed the conventional affordability threshold of 28–30 % of income for housing costs, indicating a substantial payment burden for the average household. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment is the most viable option because it yields the lowest P&I and avoids the mortgage‑insurance premium required on FHA loans. VA financing would be advantageous for eligible veterans, offering zero‑down options and lower cash‑flow requirements, while USDA loans are unavailable because Maui is not a designated rural area. FHA remains a fallback for borrowers unable to amass a 20 % down payment, but the high mortgage‑insurance cost further strains cash flow. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is **$31,665 (3.5 % for FHA)** or **$90,470 (10 % for conventional)**. Even the FHA minimum represents roughly **33 % of the median annual income**, a level that many households find difficult to achieve without significant savings or assistance. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by more than 1.5 percentage points relative to the 5.5 % environment of 2020‑2022, reducing affordability by roughly $600–$800 per month for comparable loan amounts. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective Maui buyers should prioritize saving for a 20 % conventional down payment; doing so reduces the P&I burden to just under 61 % of monthly income and eliminates mortgage‑insurance costs, making homeownership marginally more attainable in a market where loan limits already accommodate median prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Maui County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Maui County is $1,299,500. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Maui County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,299,500 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Maui County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Maui County qualifies.
