📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits for Maui County are both set at $1,299,500 for a single‑family residence, which is 144 % of the 2024 median home value of $904,700. Consequently, the typical home is comfortably within the maximum loan size for either program, eliminating a primary barrier that many high‑cost markets face where median prices exceed loan limits. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, a median‑priced home financed with an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $873,036 loan) requires $5,914 of principal‑and‑interest (P&I) each month. A conventional loan with 20 % down ($723,760 loan) reduces P&I to $4,903. Relative to the median household income of $97,161 (≈$8,097 monthly), the FHA P&I payment consumes 73.0 % of monthly earnings, while a conventional 20 % down loan consumes 60.6 %. Both ratios exceed the conventional affordability threshold of 28–30 % for housing costs, indicating a substantial payment burden for the median buyer. Given the price‑to‑limit relationship, conventional financing is the most efficient option for qualified borrowers because it avoids the FHA mortgage insurance premium and yields a lower monthly payment when a 20 % down payment is achievable. For first‑time or lower‑income purchasers, the FHA’s 3.5 % down requirement (≈$31,665) is the smallest cash outlay, but the resulting payment still strains income. USDA and VA programs are unavailable for most Maui residents due to geographic and eligibility constraints. The minimum cash needed to close at the median price is roughly $31,665 for an FHA loan, $81,423 for a conventional 10 % down, and $180,940 for a conventional 20 % down purchase. These amounts represent 3.5 %, 9.0 %, and 20 % of the home price, respectively; the 20 % figure is often cited as the threshold for avoiding private mortgage insurance and achieving a more affordable payment. Because the 7.18 % rate is more than 2 percentage points above the historic low‑rate environment of 2020‑2021, P&I costs are roughly 15 % higher than they would be at a 5 % rate, further compressing affordability. **Practical takeaway:** prospective buyers should aim to save at least a 20 % down payment; doing so not only lowers the monthly payment to a more manageable 60 % of income but also eliminates mortgage‑insurance costs that would otherwise exacerbate the affordability challenge in Maui’s high‑cost market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Maui County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Maui County is $1,299,500. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Maui County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,299,500 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Maui County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Maui County qualifies.
