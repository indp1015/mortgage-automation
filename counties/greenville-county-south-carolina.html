📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price of $299,000 by 181%, confirming that the typical residence is fully within FHA coverage, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 279% of the median price, also providing ample capacity for conventional financing. Monthly payment burden is significant: a 3.5% down FHA loan on the median home requires a principal and interest payment of $1,997, representing 31.1% of the median household income of $6,411 per month; the 20% down conventional loan reduces the payment to $1,656, or 25.8% of income, which aligns more closely with conventional affordability thresholds. At the current 7.40% interest rate, the FHA payment is substantially higher than what would be expected at lower rates (e.g., 5% would lower the FHA payment to roughly $1,500, lowering the income share to approximately 23% and enhancing affordability). Given the down payment requirements, a 3.5% down payment ($10,465) is the minimum needed to purchase at the median price via FHA, a figure that is challenging but achievable for many households; a 20% down payment ($59,800) is far less feasible relative to the median income. Consequently, FHA financing emerges as the most suitable option for typical buyers, as it offers lower down payment thresholds and sufficient loan coverage, while conventional loans remain viable for those able to meet higher down payments. The high interest rate amplifies monthly obligations, underscoring the importance of securing the lowest possible rate. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a minimum 3.5% down payment to keep housing costs near 31% of their gross income, enabling affordable entry into Greenville County’s market despite the elevated rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Greenville County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Greenville County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Greenville County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Greenville County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Greenville County qualifies.
