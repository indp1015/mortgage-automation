📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Durham County’s median home value of $389,400 sits comfortably within both federal financing thresholds. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 139% of the median price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 214%. Consequently, no buyer purchasing a median-priced property faces loan limit constraints, allowing flexibility in loan selection based on credit profile and down payment capacity rather than regulatory caps. The primary challenge remains monthly payment burden relative to income. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, a standard FHA loan requiring 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $2,601. This equates to 37.9% of the median household income of $82,316, exceeding the conventional 36% debt-to-income guideline for housing costs. A conventional loan with 20% down lowers the P&I to $2,156, or 31.4% of income, which is more manageable but requires a substantial $77,880 upfront. The minimum down payment barrier for an FHA loan is $13,629, a feasible sum for many local households, whereas the 20% conventional requirement presents a significant capital hurdle. Given the high interest rate environment, the FHA option offers the most accessible entry point for typical buyers in Durham. The lower down payment requirement mitigates initial capital barriers, and the loan limit provides ample headroom for price appreciation, which has averaged 14.7% annually over the past five years. However, the 7.40% rate significantly impacts affordability compared to lower-rate periods; a drop to 6.5% would reduce the FHA P&I by approximately $180 monthly. Prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing to minimize upfront costs, though they must ensure total debt obligations, including property taxes and insurance, remain within lender guidelines. The data indicates that while financing limits are not a constraint, the combination of high rates and rising median prices necessitates careful budgeting to maintain sustainable debt-to-income ratios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Durham County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Durham County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Durham County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Durham County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Durham County qualifies.
