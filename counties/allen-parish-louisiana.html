📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is not a constraint in Allen Parish. The FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 covers 518% of the $104,500 median home value, and the $832,750 conforming limit covers 797%. Every typical purchase therefore qualifies for both FHA and conventional financing, and no high-cost designation applies. Payment burden is moderate. At the 7.40% 30-year fixed rate, an FHA loan (3.5% down, $100,842 financed) produces $698 monthly P&I, or 18.0% of the $46,469 median household income. Conventional financing with 20% down ($83,600 financed) yields $578 monthly, or 14.9% of income. Both fall below the 28% housing-cost threshold commonly used in underwriting, though FHA borrowers also pay mortgage insurance premiums not reflected in these figures. Loan type fit varies by buyer profile. With a median value of $104,500 and 74.0% owner occupancy, conventional loans are viable for buyers with established credit and savings. However, USDA Rural Development financing—typically zero down with no monthly mortgage insurance—is likely the strongest fit where eligible, given the parish's rural character. VA loans serve eligible veterans similarly. FHA remains the accessible option for lower-credit or lower-savings buyers. Down payment barriers are modest in absolute terms: $3,658 for FHA (3.5%) or $10,450 for 10% conventional. Relative to median income, these are attainable, though the 7.40% rate amplifies long-run costs; a payment at 6.00% would be materially lower, so rate environment meaningfully affects purchasing power. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Allen Parish?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Allen Parish is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Allen Parish?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Allen Parish?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Allen Parish qualifies.
