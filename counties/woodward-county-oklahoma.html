📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Woodward County’s housing market demonstrates strong alignment between local property values and federal financing limits, ensuring broad accessibility for prospective buyers. The 2026 FHA one-unit loan limit of $541,287 covers 320% of the county’s median home value of $169,000, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 493%. Consequently, no typical residence in this market approaches the ceiling for either loan type, eliminating cap-related restrictions for the vast majority of transactions. This ample coverage confirms that financing availability is not a barrier to entry for standard single-family homes within the county. Regarding payment burden, the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% creates a manageable but noticeable cost structure relative to local incomes. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment results in a principal and interest payment of $1,129 per month. This figure represents 22.1% of the median household income of $61,417, falling within standard underwriting guidelines for debt-to-income ratios. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers the monthly P&I to $936, or 18.3% of income, offering improved cash flow flexibility. The recent 5.1% year-over-year decline in median home values from 2023 to 2024 has slightly improved affordability metrics, offsetting some of the pressure exerted by elevated interest rates compared to the low-rate environments of 2020 and 2021. For most buyers in Woodward County, the FHA loan is likely the optimal fit due to its lower minimum down payment requirement of 3.5%. At the median price point, this requires an initial investment of approximately $5,915, a sum that is significantly more accessible than the $33,800 required for a 20% conventional down payment. Given the county’s median household income, saving for a conventional down payment may delay homeownership by several years, whereas the FHA threshold aligns better with typical savings trajectories for local residents. While USDA loans may also be available depending on specific rural eligibility zones, the FHA’s universal applicability and competitive terms make it the primary recommendation. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that the current market conditions favor immediate entry via FHA financing, as the combination of low absolute home prices and substantial loan limit headroom ensures that financing constraints do not hinder access to the median-priced home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Woodward County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Woodward County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Woodward County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Woodward County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Woodward County qualifies.
