📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price of $117,900 in Logan County, Kansas. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 covers 459 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 706 %. Consequently, virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that meets the price of a typical home without needing a high‑cost‑area exemption. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment for a median‑priced home is $775 with an FHA loan (3.5 % down) and $642 with a conventional loan funded at 20 % equity. Both figures represent a modest share of the median household income of $75,662 (annual) or $6,305 monthly. The FHA payment equals 12.3 % of monthly income, and the 20 % conventional payment equals 10.2 %. These ratios sit well below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % of gross income for housing costs, indicating that the monthly debt service is not a primary barrier for average earners. Given the low median price relative to loan limits, a conventional loan with a 10–20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option. An FHA loan’s higher mortgage‑insurance premium and slightly larger P&I payment make it less attractive for borrowers who can meet the conventional down‑payment requirement. USDA eligibility is unlikely because the county’s median income exceeds the program’s 115 % poverty line, and VA financing applies only to eligible veterans. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is $4,127 (3.5 % for FHA) or $11,790 (10 % for conventional). For a household earning $75,662, a 10 % down payment represents roughly 15 % of one year’s income, which is generally attainable through savings or modest assistance programs. Rate sensitivity analysis shows that a 1 % reduction in the mortgage rate would lower the conventional 20 % P&I payment by about $70 per month, while a 1 % increase would raise it by a similar amount. Even at 7.24 %, the payment remains well within affordability limits, but higher rates would erode the margin for borrowers with tighter budgets. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers in Logan County can comfortably finance a median‑priced home with a conventional loan and a 10 % down payment; the resulting monthly payment consumes only about one‑tenth of household income, even at today’s 7.24 % rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Logan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Logan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Logan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Logan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Logan County qualifies.
