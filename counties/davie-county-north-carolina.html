📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 and the conforming loan limit of $832,750 both exceed the median home price in Davie County ($253,900), meaning the typical property is comfortably within financing coverage for both programs. The FHA limit represents 213% of the median price, while the conforming limit reaches 328%, confirming adequate coverage without requiring a high‑cost designation. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show that the FHA loan (3.5% down, $245,014 principal) requires a principal‑and‑interest payment of $1,696, which equals 27.1% of the median household income of $75,057. The conventional loan with 20% down ($203,120 principal) costs $1,406 per month, or 22.5% of income. Both figures fall near the conventional affordability benchmark of 30% of gross income, though the FHA payment is slightly higher. Given the limited down‑payment requirements, a buyer seeking the median home would need at least $8,887 for an FHA loan (3.5% down) or $25,390 for a conventional 10% down payment. The 3.5% down payment is generally feasible for many households, whereas the 10% down amount represents a substantial barrier for typical buyers. Consequently, an FHA loan appears to be the most suitable option for typical buyers in this market. At the current 7.40% interest rate, the FHA payment consumes a larger share of income than it would at lower rates; a reduction to 6% would lower the monthly P&I by roughly $200, improving affordability. Conversely, higher rates would further strain budgets, making the 27% income share less sustainable. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize an FHA loan with the minimum 3.5% down payment to keep monthly housing costs under 30% of their income, leveraging the program’s higher loan‑to‑value capability to overcome the down‑payment hurdle in this market. Additionally, the 82.2% owner‑occupancy rate indicates strong demand for primary residences, reinforcing the relevance of affordable financing options for local families.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Davie County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Davie County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Davie County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Davie County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Davie County qualifies.
