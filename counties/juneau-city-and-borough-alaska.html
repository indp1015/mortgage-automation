📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Juneau City and Borough, Alaska, demonstrates robust coverage for typical buyers. The 2026 FHA limit of $811,931 covers 181% of the median home value of $449,300, while the conforming limit of $1,249,125 covers 278%. Consequently, the median-priced property falls well within both loan categories, ensuring that standard financing options are accessible without requiring jumbo loans. This ample coverage mitigates structural barriers associated with high-priced markets, allowing most residents to utilize conventional or government-backed instruments for their primary residence. Monthly payment burdens remain significant relative to local income levels. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.32%, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment results in a principal and interest payment of $2,978, which constitutes 35.2% of the median household income of $101,661. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers this burden to $2,469, or 29.1% of income. While the conventional option offers better affordability metrics, the required down payment of $89,860 presents a substantial capital barrier. In contrast, the FHA minimum down payment of $15,725 is more accessible, though the higher monthly obligation may strain budgets for average earners. Given the high-cost county designation and the median price point, conventional loans with 10-20% down payments offer the optimal balance between monthly cash flow and total interest costs for qualified buyers. Rate sensitivity significantly impacts purchasing power in this environment. The 7.32% interest rate elevates monthly costs compared to lower-rate environments, making the difference between 10% and 20% down payments critical for maintaining debt-to-income ratios within lender guidelines. The 5-year price CAGR of 5.5% indicates steady appreciation, but the recent 3.9% increase in 2024 suggests a moderating trend. For prospective buyers, the concrete takeaway is that saving for a 20% down payment is the most effective strategy to secure a manageable monthly payment. This approach reduces the loan principal to $359,440, keeping the P&I payment at $2,469 and ensuring that housing costs remain below the 30% income threshold, thereby enhancing long-term financial stability in the Juneau market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Juneau City and Borough?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Juneau City and Borough is $811,931. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Juneau City and Borough?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Juneau City and Borough?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Juneau City and Borough qualifies.
