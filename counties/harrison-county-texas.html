📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Harrison County, Texas, presents a financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local market values, ensuring broad eligibility for standard mortgage products. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 300% of the median home value of $180,400, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 462%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces a ceiling issue; the median property is well within the reach of both FHA and conventional financing structures. This ample coverage means that loan limits are not a constraining factor for the average household, allowing buyers to focus primarily on income qualification and down payment requirements rather than product availability. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though the current interest rate environment exerts noticeable pressure. With a 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, an FHA loan on the median-priced home requires a minimum down payment of 3.5%, or $6,314, resulting in a principal and interest payment of $1,205 per month. This figure represents 21.9% of the median household income of $66,103. While this ratio stays within the general 28% front-end debt-to-income guideline, it leaves less room for other monthly obligations compared to lower-rate periods. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $999, or 18.1% of income, offering greater flexibility. However, the $36,080 upfront cash requirement for a 20% down payment is a substantial barrier for first-time buyers, making the lower-down-payment FHA option more accessible despite the higher monthly cost. The primary challenge for prospective buyers is not loan limit eligibility but the initial capital required to enter the market. The 5-year price CAGR of 9.4% has accelerated home values from $115,100 in 2017 to $180,400 in 2024, increasing the absolute dollar amount needed for any down payment. At the current 7.40% rate, affordability is tighter than in previous years; for instance, a 6.0% rate would have reduced the FHA monthly payment by approximately $100. Buyers should prioritize saving for the minimum 3.5% down payment, which is feasible for many households given the local income levels, while maintaining a debt-to-income ratio that comfortably supports the $1,205 monthly obligation. The market remains accessible, but the high interest rate necessitates careful budgeting to ensure long-term financial stability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Harrison County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Harrison County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Harrison County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Harrison County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Harrison County qualifies.
