📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price in Glenn County. The 2026 FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is **154 %** of the median value ($352,400), while the conforming limit of $832,750 is **236 %** of that price, confirming that virtually any median‑priced property can be financed under either program without needing a high‑cost‑county exemption. At a 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) obligation for a median home is $2,303 / month with an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $340,066 loan) and $1,909 / month with a conventional loan at 20 % down ($281,920 loan). Median household income of $67,139 translates to a monthly income of $5,595; therefore the FHA payment consumes **41.2 %** of income, while a 20 % conventional payment consumes **34.1 %**. Both ratios exceed the conventional 28 % affordability benchmark, indicating a notable payment burden for the typical household. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment offers the lowest monthly outlay and avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums, but the required $70,480 down payment (20 % of $352,400) represents **21 %** of median household income—an amount that many families cannot readily accumulate. An FHA loan reduces the down‑payment requirement to $12,334 (3.5 %), improving accessibility, yet the higher P&I and mandatory mortgage insurance raise the overall cost. USDA or VA loans could be viable for eligible rural or veteran purchasers, as they permit zero down and often lower insurance fees, but eligibility constraints limit their applicability. The 7.18 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $300–$400 compared with a 5 % environment, pushing affordability ratios above the 30 % threshold and underscoring the sensitivity of this market to rate movements. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers should prioritize securing a low‑down‑payment FHA loan or an eligible USDA loan to meet the 20 % down‑payment hurdle, while budgeting for a P&I payment that will likely exceed 30 % of household income at current rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Glenn County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Glenn County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Glenn County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Glenn County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Glenn County qualifies.
