📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both sit far above the median home price of $206,000 in Gray County, Kansas. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 covers 263 % of the median value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 404 %. Consequently, virtually any median‑priced property can be financed under either program without requiring a high‑cost‑area exemption. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, a conventional loan with 20 % down ($164,800 financed) yields a principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment of $1,123 per month, representing 17.0 % of the median household income ($79,122/12 = $6,594). An FHA loan with the minimum 3.5 % down ($198,790 financed) produces a P&I payment of $1,354, or 20.5 % of income. Both ratios remain below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % for housing costs, indicating that the median buyer can comfortably service the loan even with the elevated rate. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option because it avoids the FHA mortgage‑insurance premium and results in a lower monthly outlay. However, the required $41,200 down payment (20 % of $206,000) may be a barrier for first‑time purchasers. An FHA loan reduces the down‑payment requirement to $7,210 (3.5 %), making homeownership attainable for borrowers with limited cash reserves, albeit at a higher monthly cost. USDA or VA financing could be viable for eligible rural or veteran buyers, offering zero down and competitive rates, but eligibility constraints limit their applicability. The 7.24 % rate adds roughly $150–$200 to the monthly payment versus a 5 % environment, shrinking the affordability cushion but not pushing the median buyer beyond standard debt‑to‑income limits. **Practical takeaway:** prospective buyers who can marshal a 20 % down payment will benefit from lower monthly payments and no mortgage‑insurance fees; those unable to do so should consider FHA financing, which remains affordable relative to income despite the higher rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Gray County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Gray County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Gray County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Gray County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Gray County qualifies.
