📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Calhoun County’s housing market remains well within standard federal financing parameters, ensuring broad accessibility for prospective buyers. The 2026 FHA loan limit of $541,287 covers 346% of the median home value of $156,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 532%. Consequently, no buyer in this market faces a loan limit barrier, as the typical property value is a fraction of the maximum allowable financing. This ample coverage allows lenders to offer standard products without triggering high-cost county restrictions or requiring jumbo loan structures, which are unnecessary for the local price point. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though the current interest rate environment exerts significant pressure. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.32%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down on the median-priced home results in a principal and interest payment of $1,038 per month. This amount represents 22.6% of the median household income of $55,029, approaching the upper threshold of conventional debt-to-income guidelines. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the monthly P&I to $860, or 18.8% of income, offering a more comfortable margin. The high interest rate significantly increases the monthly outlay compared to lower-rate periods, making the initial down payment size a critical factor in long-term affordability. For the typical buyer, an FHA loan is the most accessible entry point due to the low 3.5% down payment requirement, which amounts to approximately $5,481 for a median-priced home. This barrier is feasible for many households, particularly those saving for a first purchase. However, buyers with at least 20% down ($31,320) should consider conventional financing to avoid mortgage insurance and reduce the overall monthly obligation. The 7.32% rate highlights the importance of creditworthiness, as securing a slightly lower rate through better credit scores can substantially reduce the total cost of ownership. Ultimately, the combination of high loan limit coverage and moderate home prices supports a stable financing landscape, provided buyers align their down payment capacity with their debt-to-income ratios to maintain financial resilience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Calhoun County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Calhoun County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Calhoun County qualifies.
