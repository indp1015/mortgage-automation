📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Calhoun County, Alabama, presents a distinct financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local market realities. The 2026 FHA limit for a single-unit property stands at $541,287, which covers 346% of the county’s median home value of $156,600. Similarly, the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 532% of the median price. Because the median home value is well below both thresholds, no buyer in the typical market segment faces loan limit constraints. This ample coverage ensures that financing availability is not a barrier for standard purchases, allowing lenders to focus on creditworthiness and income verification rather than appraised value caps. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though current interest rates exert noticeable pressure. At a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest (P&I) payment for an FHA loan with 3.5% down is $1,046. This figure represents 22.8% of the median household income of $55,029. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down requires a $867 P&I payment, or 18.9% of income. While both percentages fall within traditional affordability guidelines, the higher rate environment increases the absolute dollar cost of borrowing compared to historical lows. For instance, a 100 basis point drop in rates would reduce the FHA payment by approximately $70 per month, highlighting the sensitivity of affordability to interest rate fluctuations. For the typical buyer, FHA financing is likely the most accessible option due to its lower down payment requirement of 3.5%, which amounts to approximately $5,481 at the median price. This is substantially more feasible than the 20% down payment required for conventional loans without private mortgage insurance, which would total $31,320. Although USDA loans may be available in rural portions of the county, FHA offers broader availability and predictable underwriting standards. The primary barrier to entry is not the loan limit but the initial capital required for the down payment and closing costs. Prospective buyers should note that while the median home value has grown at a 7.3% compound annual rate over the last five years, the absolute price point remains low. Consequently, the strategic focus for purchasers should be on securing pre-approval for FHA loans to leverage the low down payment threshold, ensuring that the 22.8% income allocation remains sustainable even as rates fluctuate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Calhoun County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Calhoun County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Calhoun County qualifies.
