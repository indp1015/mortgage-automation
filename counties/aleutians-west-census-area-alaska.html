Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Aleutians West Census Area?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Aleutians West Census Area is $811,931. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Aleutians West Census Area?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Aleutians West Census Area?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Aleutians West Census Area qualifies.
