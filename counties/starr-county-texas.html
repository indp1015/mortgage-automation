📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is adequate for the typical home in Starr County. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $95,100 by more than fivefold, and the conforming loan limit of $832,750 covers 876% of the median price, confirming that both programs can fully finance a purchase at the median level. A 3.5% down FHA loan on a $91,772 principal carries a payment of $635 per month, which equals 20.2% of the median monthly household income of $3,136. Conventional financing with 20% down reduces the payment to $526 per month, or 16.8% of income, indicating that even the most conservative down payment remains affordable. Because the median household income is $37,639, a $3,300 down payment represents 8.8% of annual earnings and is generally attainable with modest savings. The 3.5% down requirement translates to a $3,300 outlay, which is manageable for many households given the median income and the availability of down‑payment assistance programs. Given the price point and down‑payment requirements, an FHA loan is likely the optimal choice for typical buyers, as it permits as little as $3,300 down and aligns with the county’s price limits. Conventional loans require higher upfront cash—$9,500 for 10% down or $19,000 for 20%—which may be challenging for households earning a median $37,639 annually. At the present 7.40% rate, the FHA payment of $635 consumes roughly one‑fifth of median monthly income, while a 5% rate would lower the payment to about $475, reducing the income share to 15% and improving affordability. VA and USDA loans are not required because the median home price is well below their eligibility thresholds and the county is not classified as high‑cost. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers can achieve homeownership with a down payment as low as $3,300 through FHA financing, making the median home attainable despite modest incomes and the elevated interest rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Starr County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Starr County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Starr County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Starr County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Starr County qualifies.
