📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Clarke County, Mississippi, presents a highly accessible market where federal loan limits significantly exceed local property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 458% of the median home value of $118,100, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 705%. Consequently, financing constraints are not driven by loan ceilings but by borrower qualification and down payment capacity. With a median household income of $46,108, the monthly principal and interest payment for an FHA loan at the median price is $789, representing 20.5% of monthly income. This ratio sits near the upper threshold of conventional underwriting guidelines, suggesting that while the home is within reach, the debt-to-income ratio leaves limited room for additional obligations. For the typical buyer, FHA financing remains the most viable option due to its low down payment requirement. A 3.5% down payment on the median-priced home amounts to approximately $4,134, a figure that is more feasible for local households than the $11,810 required for a 10% conventional down payment. The 20% down payment scenario, which reduces the monthly payment to $654, requires $23,620 upfront, a barrier that likely excludes many median-income residents. Although USDA loans may be available in rural areas, the specific data provided supports FHA as the primary accessible vehicle. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows. At this rate, the monthly payment burden is higher than it would be at 6.0% or below, effectively reducing the maximum loan amount a borrower can service. The market has experienced rapid appreciation, with a 10.4% five-year compound annual growth rate, yet the 2024 year-over-year growth slowed to 3.3%. This deceleration provides a slight reprieve for buyers facing high interest rates. However, the combination of a 7.40% rate and a 20.5% payment-to-income ratio indicates that affordability is tight. Prospective buyers should prioritize saving for the minimum 3.5% down payment to unlock FHA access, as the low entry cost aligns better with local income levels. Furthermore, buyers must carefully assess their total debt-to-income ratio, as the high interest rate environment leaves little margin for error. The practical takeaway is that while Clarke County remains affordable relative to national averages, the high interest rate environment necessitates strict budgeting and minimal additional debt to secure a mortgage at the median price point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Clarke County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Clarke County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Clarke County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Clarke County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Clarke County qualifies.
