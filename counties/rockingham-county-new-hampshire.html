📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Rockingham County’s housing market presents a distinct financing landscape where standard loan limits provide substantial coverage for the median-priced property. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers approximately 109% of the $497,500 median home value, ensuring that typical purchases do not require jumbo financing. Similarly, the conforming limit of $962,550 covers nearly 193% of the median price, offering ample room for higher-value transactions within standard agency guidelines. This robust coverage means that most buyers in the county can access conventional or FHA financing without encountering the higher costs or stricter underwriting associated with non-conforming loans. Consequently, the primary constraint for buyers is not loan availability but rather monthly debt service relative to income. The affordability burden is significant given the current 7.40% interest rate environment. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment results in a principal and interest payment of $3,324 per month. This figure represents 33.7% of the median household income of $118,331, a ratio that approaches the upper threshold of typical debt-to-income limits. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $2,755, or 27.9% of income, which is more manageable but requires a substantial upfront capital outlay of $99,500. Conversely, the minimum FHA down payment of $17,413 is more accessible for entry-level buyers, though the higher monthly obligation remains a barrier. The rapid appreciation of home values, averaging 11.0% annually over the last five years, has outpaced income growth, tightening the affordability gap. Given these dynamics, conventional financing is likely the optimal fit for buyers with sufficient savings, as it offers lower monthly obligations and avoids FHA mortgage insurance premiums. However, for those with limited capital, FHA loans remain a viable entry point, provided borrowers can secure incomes well above the median to offset the 33.7% payment burden. The current interest rate significantly impacts purchasing power; at 7.40%, a borrower’s budget for a $3,000 monthly payment is substantially lower than it would have been in a 4-5% rate environment. Prospective buyers should prioritize increasing their down payment to reduce principal balance and monthly costs, as this is the most effective lever available to improve affordability in this high-price, high-rate market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Rockingham County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Rockingham County is $625,657. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Rockingham County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $962,550 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Rockingham County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Rockingham County qualifies.
