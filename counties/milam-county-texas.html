📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Milam County’s housing market presents a distinct affordability profile where loan limits significantly exceed local price points, ensuring broad financing accessibility. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 285% of the $189,800 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 439%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces high-cost county restrictions or limit caps. This ample coverage means that the primary barrier to entry is not regulatory loan ceilings, but rather the borrower’s ability to meet down payment requirements and sustain monthly debt obligations under current interest rate conditions. The monthly payment burden remains manageable relative to local income levels, though it is sensitive to financing structures. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,268, consuming 23.0% of the $66,141 median household income. This ratio falls within standard underwriting guidelines, indicating that typical buyers can qualify without requiring income supplements. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the P&I to $1,051, or 19.1% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. Given the rapid appreciation, with a 16.5% five-year CAGR, the current 7.40% fixed rate significantly impacts total cost of ownership. Compared to historical lower-rate environments, the higher interest rate increases the monthly outlay by several hundred dollars, effectively reducing the maximum loan amount a buyer can afford while keeping payments within the 28% debt-to-income threshold. For most prospective buyers in Milam County, the FHA loan represents the optimal initial entry point due to its low 3.5% down payment requirement, which translates to approximately $6,643 for a median-priced home. This low barrier is feasible for many households, particularly given the 76.9% owner occupancy rate which suggests a stable pool of potential sellers. However, buyers with larger savings should consider conventional financing to avoid mortgage insurance costs and benefit from lower overall interest rates. The practical takeaway is that while the median home price of $189,800 is well within all major loan limits, the 7.40% interest rate necessitates careful budgeting. Buyers should prioritize saving for a conventional down payment if possible, as the 20% down option provides the strongest long-term equity position and lowest monthly burden in this rapidly appreciating market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Milam County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Milam County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Milam County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Milam County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Milam County qualifies.
