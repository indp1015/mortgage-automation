📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Based on the 2024 median home value of $120,600, the Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 represents 449 % of the typical price while the conforming loan limit of $832,750 covers 691 % of the median value, confirming that both programs fully accommodate the local market. The 5‑year price CAGR of 4.9 % per year indicates steady appreciation, supporting the view that current financing limits remain adequate. The median household income of $50,012 translates to a 19.3 % housing cost ratio for an FHA loan requiring a $116,379 principal (3.5 % down) with a monthly P&I of $805. A conventional loan with 20 % down reduces the principal to $96,480 and the payment to $668, representing 16.0 % of income. Given that the median price falls well below both limits and a 20 % down payment is the most affordable option, a conventional loan is the most suitable financing vehicle for typical buyers. An FHA down payment of $4,221 (3.5 % of median price) is feasible, but the resulting $805 monthly obligation consumes nearly 19 % of median income, whereas a 20 % down conventional payment of $668 limits housing costs to about 16 % of income. At the current 7.40 % 30‑year fixed rate, these payments are higher than they would be at lower rates, reducing affordability; a 1 % rate reduction would lower the FHA payment to roughly $720 and the 20 % conventional payment to about $605, illustrating the sensitivity to interest rates. Prospective buyers should therefore aim for a 20 % down payment on a conventional loan to keep monthly P&I near $668, maintaining housing costs near 16 % of median income and preserving purchasing power in a market where price growth has slowed to 0.8 % year‑over‑year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Russell County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Russell County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Russell County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Russell County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Russell County qualifies.
