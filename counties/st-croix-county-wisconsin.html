📝 Mortgage Market Notes

St. Croix County’s median home value of $377,700 sits comfortably within both the 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. The FHA ceiling covers 143% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 220%, ensuring that no high-balance financing is required for typical transactions. Consequently, standard loan products are fully accessible to the average buyer without triggering non-conforming or jumbo loan requirements. This ample coverage simplifies the underwriting process and expands the pool of eligible lenders for residents seeking to purchase homes at or near the market median. The primary constraint in this market is the monthly payment burden relative to income. With a 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, an FHA loan on the median-priced home requires a monthly principal and interest payment of $2,523. This figure represents 29.4% of the median household income of $103,046, approaching the 30% debt-to-income threshold often used by lenders. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces this burden to $2,092, or 24.4% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. However, the 10% down conventional option still demands $2,353 per month. Given the rapid 11.1% five-year compound annual growth rate in home values, the gap between rising home prices and static income levels has narrowed the margin for affordability. The current interest rate environment significantly impacts these calculations; compared to historical rates below 4%, the 7.40% rate increases monthly payments by roughly 30%, directly reducing the maximum loan amount a borrower can qualify for. For the typical buyer in St. Croix County, the FHA loan is likely the most accessible entry point due to its lower down payment requirement of 3.5%, which amounts to approximately $13,220. This is substantially more feasible than the $37,770 required for a 10% conventional down payment. While USDA loans may be an option for rural areas within the county, the high owner-occupancy rate of 78.3% suggests a robust conventional market. Prospective buyers should note that the minimum down payment barrier is low in absolute terms, but the high interest rate amplifies the total cost of ownership. A concrete insight for buyers is that increasing the down payment from 3.5% to 20% reduces the monthly payment by $431, a significant improvement in cash flow that can offset the higher initial capital outlay. This strategy allows borrowers to maintain a healthier debt-to-income ratio, providing a buffer against potential rate adjustments or unexpected expenses while securing a home in a rapidly appreciating market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in St. Croix County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in St. Croix County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in St. Croix County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in St. Croix County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm St. Croix County qualifies.
