📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Kewaunee County, Wisconsin, demonstrates robust coverage for typical home purchases. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 244% of the median home value of $222,200, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 375%. Consequently, the median-priced property falls well within standard loan ceilings, eliminating the need for jumbo financing. This ample coverage ensures that standard loan products remain accessible to the majority of local buyers without triggering high-cost county restrictions or specialized high-balance mortgage requirements. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. For a median home, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment results in a principal and interest payment of $1,484 per month. This figure represents 21.5% of the median household income of $82,725. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers the monthly P&I to $1,230, equating to 17.8% of income. Both scenarios align with conventional debt-to-income guidelines, indicating that the current pricing environment does not disproportionately strain household budgets compared to national averages. Given the low median home value, FHA loans are likely the most suitable option for first-time buyers due to the minimal 3.5% down payment requirement, which amounts to approximately $7,777. This barrier is significantly lower than the $22,220 required for a 10% conventional down payment or $44,440 for a 20% down payment. While USDA loans may be available in specific rural tracts, the broad accessibility of FHA financing makes it the primary vehicle for entry-level ownership. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% increases monthly costs compared to historical lows, yet the affordability metrics remain stable due to the county’s relatively low price points. The 5-year price CAGR of 7.3% suggests a steady appreciation trend, but the current valuation does not yet threaten baseline affordability. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing pre-approval for FHA financing to capitalize on the low down payment threshold. With the median home price significantly below loan limits, the primary constraint for entry is cash reserves for closing costs and down payments rather than loan eligibility. Buyers with limited savings should focus on down payment assistance programs that can further reduce the initial cash outlay, ensuring that the 21.5% income ratio remains sustainable even when taxes and insurance are included in the total monthly housing expense.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Kewaunee County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Kewaunee County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Kewaunee County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Kewaunee County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Kewaunee County qualifies.
