📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Pitkin County's median home value of $1,139,100 exceeds the FHA 1-unit limit of $786,337 by 45%, leaving FHA financing covering only 69% of the typical purchase price. The conforming limit of $1,209,750 adequately covers the median at 106%, positioning conventional financing as the primary viable option for median-priced homes. FHA loans are effectively constrained to the lower third of the market, while VA loans (no statutory limit with full entitlement) and jumbo products address the upper tiers. At a 7.40% 30-year fixed rate, the monthly principal and interest burden is severe across all structures. An FHA loan at 3.5% down requires $7,610 monthly—89.0% of median household income ($8,554/month). A conventional loan with 20% down ($227,820 upfront) reduces P&I to $6,309, still consuming 73.8% of gross income. Even at 10% down ($113,910), the $7,098 payment represents 83.0% of income. These ratios far exceed the 28% front-end DTI guideline, indicating that median-income households cannot conventionally afford median-priced homes without substantial down payment assistance or income well above the median. The minimum down payment to enter the conforming limit at median price is effectively zero (limit exceeds price), but the 20% threshold to avoid PMI demands $227,820—2.2 times annual median income. At 5%, the $56,955 requirement remains 55% of annual income. Rate sensitivity is acute: each 100-basis-point increase adds approximately $700–$750 to monthly P&I on a $900,000–$1,100,000 loan. Compared to a 5.5% environment, current rates increase monthly costs by roughly $1,300–$1,400. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Pitkin County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Pitkin County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Pitkin County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,209,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Pitkin County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Pitkin County qualifies.
