📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA one‑unit loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value in Chautauqua County by more than fourfold (423% of $127,900), while the conforming limit of $832,750 provides coverage over six times the median price (651%). The estimated FHA payment of $854 per month for a $123,424 loan equals 17.6% of the median household income of $58,351, and the conventional 20% down payment of $708 per month equals 14.6% of income. With a median monthly rent of $812 and an owner‑occupancy rate of 68.9%, the FHA payment is lower than typical rent, suggesting that purchasing could be more economical for many residents. Given the median home price of $127,900 and the FHA requirement of only 3.5% down, the necessary cash outlay is approximately $4,475, a one‑time amount that is generally attainable for households with the median monthly income of $4,862, representing less than 1% of their monthly earnings. In contrast, a conventional 10% down payment would demand about $12,790, roughly 26% of monthly income, and a 20% down payment would exceed $25,500, representing over 50% of monthly income, which is often beyond the reach of typical families. Although the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.40% results in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $854 per month for the FHA loan (17.6% of monthly income) and $796 per month for a 10% conventional loan (14.6% of income), the high rate significantly raises the burden compared with lower‑rate environments where payments could drop by several hundred dollars per month. The county’s 8.3% five‑year price CAGR shows that home values are rising quickly, meaning that waiting may increase the required down payment and monthly costs. Therefore, for the typical buyer in Chautauqua County, an FHA loan provides the most practical solution, balancing full coverage of the median home price, affordable monthly payments, and a down‑payment that aligns with typical household budgets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Chautauqua County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Chautauqua County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Chautauqua County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Chautauqua County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Chautauqua County qualifies.
