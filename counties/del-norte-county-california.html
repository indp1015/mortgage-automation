📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Del Norte County’s housing market presents a distinct financing landscape where loan limits significantly exceed local property values. The median home value of $342,000 sits comfortably within both the FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750, covering 158% and 243% of the median price, respectively. This ample coverage ensures that standard financing options are accessible without requiring jumbo loans, which are rarely necessary in this low-cost market. Consequently, the primary constraint for buyers is not loan eligibility but rather monthly debt service relative to income. The affordability burden is substantial due to the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.24%. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down requires a principal and interest payment of $2,249 per month. This figure represents 40.2% of the median household income of $67,058, exceeding the standard 36% debt-to-income threshold often preferred by lenders. Even with a 20% down payment on a conventional loan, the monthly payment of $1,864 still consumes 33.4% of median income. While this is marginally more manageable, it remains high for a household earning the area median, suggesting that typical buyers will likely need to stretch their budgets or seek lower-priced properties to maintain financial stability. Given these dynamics, FHA loans offer the most practical entry point due to the low 3.5% down payment requirement, which amounts to approximately $11,970. This barrier is significantly lower than the $34,200 required for a 10% conventional down payment. However, the high interest rate amplifies the total cost of borrowing compared to historical environments. A buyer at the median price faces a payment burden that is disproportionately high relative to local incomes, a direct result of the 13.0% five-year price appreciation compounding with elevated interest rates. Prospective buyers should recognize that while Del Norte County remains affordable in terms of absolute purchase price, the monthly cash flow requirements are stringent. The most critical insight is that income verification will be the primary hurdle; buyers must demonstrate earnings well above the $67,058 median to comfortably service a $342,000 mortgage at current rates, or they must target homes significantly below the median to align payments with typical local earnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Del Norte County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Del Norte County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Del Norte County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Del Norte County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Del Norte County qualifies.
