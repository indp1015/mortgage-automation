📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The financing landscape in Juab County, Utah, presents a favorable alignment between loan limits and local property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 127% of the median home value of $425,400, while the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 196%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces high-cost county restrictions or limit constraints for standard single-family purchases. This ample coverage ensures that both government-backed and conventional loan programs remain viable options for the average household. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though they require careful budgeting. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $2,842, which consumes 33.5% of the median household income of $101,786. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this burden to $2,356, or 27.8% of income. While the FHA ratio approaches the upper threshold often used for qualification, the conventional option offers a more comfortable margin. The current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts these figures; compared to historical lower-rate environments, the higher interest rate increases the absolute dollar cost of borrowing, making the initial down payment size a critical lever for reducing monthly obligations. Regarding down payment barriers, the minimum requirement for an FHA loan is approximately $14,889 (3.5% of $425,400). For a conventional loan with 10% down, the required amount is $42,540, while a 20% down payment necessitates $85,080. Given the median household income, saving $14,889 is a more feasible short-term goal than accumulating $85,080, particularly for first-time buyers. However, the rapid appreciation of home values, with a five-year CAGR of 19.3%, means that the barrier to entry rises annually. The 2024 median value was 15.0% higher than in 2023, indicating that waiting further increases the capital required for entry. The optimal loan recommendation for typical buyers in Juab County is the FHA loan. It provides the lowest entry barrier with a 3.5% down payment requirement, which is easily accessible for households earning the median income. The conforming limit is irrelevant for price constraints but offers lower monthly payments for those with substantial savings. The practical takeaway for prospective buyers is that while the market is not priced out of standard loan limits, the combination of high appreciation rates and elevated interest rates means that maximizing the down payment is the most effective strategy to ensure long-term affordability and avoid negative equity risks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Juab County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Juab County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Juab County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Juab County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Juab County qualifies.
