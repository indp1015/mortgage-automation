📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Greene County's financing infrastructure comfortably accommodates the local market. The FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 covers 137% of the $394,800 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 211%, eliminating loan-limit constraints for virtually all typical purchases. Both programs provide ample headroom for buyers financing at or near the median price point. Monthly payment burden remains elevated under current rate conditions. At 7.40%, an FHA loan with 3.5% down ($380,982 loan amount) yields $2,637 in principal and interest—consuming 36.7% of the $7,189 median monthly household income. Conventional financing with 20% down ($315,840 loan) reduces P&I to $2,186, or 30.4% of income, nearing the 28% front-end threshold but excluding taxes, insurance, and mortgage insurance. A 10% down conventional loan sits at $2,460 (34.2% of income). These ratios reflect the significant impact of the 7.40% rate; at 5.5%, the same 20% down payment would lower P&I to approximately $1,798 (25.0% of income), materially improving affordability. For the typical buyer, conventional financing with 10–20% down presents the optimal structure given the wide margin between median price and conforming limits, avoiding FHA's upfront and annual mortgage insurance premiums. VA-eligible buyers benefit from zero-down financing within the conforming limit, while USDA eligibility should be verified for specific rural addresses. The minimum down payment at median price is $13,818 (3.5% FHA), feasible for households with moderate savings but requiring sufficient reserves for closing costs and the 1.75% upfront MIP ($6,667). Given the 17.6% five-year price CAGR, buyers should stress-test budgets at current rates rather than anticipate near-term rate relief.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Greene County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Greene County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Greene County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Greene County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Greene County qualifies.
