📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price of $428,300 by 26%, and the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 94%, confirming that both programs fully cover typical purchases in King George County. Consequently, the median home falls comfortably within FHA eligibility and is well within the conforming loan ceiling, eliminating the need for jumbo financing for most buyers. Monthly payment burden can be measured against the median household income of $116,884, or $9,757 per month. A 3.5% down FHA loan on a $413,310 balance results in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $2,861, representing 29.4% of monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down on a $342,640 balance yields $2,372, or 24.4% of income, while a 10% down conventional loan at $385,470 costs $2,668 (27.3% of income). These figures indicate that even the most affordable conventional option remains near the 25‑30% threshold for housing cost relative to income. Given the availability of FHA financing and the modest down payment required, FHA loans are likely the best fit for typical buyers lacking large cash reserves, though those with sufficient savings may prefer a conventional loan to avoid mortgage insurance premiums. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5% ($15,000) for FHA, 10% ($42,800) for a low‑down conventional loan, and 20% ($85,600) for a conventional loan without PMI; the 3.5% amount is generally attainable for many households, whereas the higher down payments present a greater barrier. Current 7.40% interest rates increase the payment burden compared with lower‑rate environments. Reducing the rate to 6% would lower the FHA payment to approximately $2,500 (a 12% reduction) and the 20% down conventional payment to about $2,150, improving affordability substantially. Therefore, prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a larger down payment to lower both the loan amount and the payment‑to‑income ratio, or consider FHA financing if cash constraints limit the down payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in King George County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in King George County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in King George County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in King George County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm King George County qualifies.
