📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Henry County, Kentucky, presents a highly accessible financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local property values. The 2026 FHA single-family limit of $541,287 covers 274% of the median home value of $197,900, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 421%. Consequently, no standard buyer in this market will encounter loan limit restrictions, ensuring that both government-backed and conventional financing options remain fully available for the typical transaction. The primary affordability challenge stems from the current interest rate environment rather than loan availability. With the 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, the principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on a median-priced home is approximately $1,322 per month. This figure represents 25.0% of the median household income of $63,347, sitting at the upper threshold of standard debt-to-income guidelines. By contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down requires a $1,096 monthly payment, equating to 20.8% of income. While the 9.4% five-year price appreciation has increased entry costs, the payment burden remains manageable for households earning near the median, provided they maintain healthy credit profiles and low existing debt. Regarding loan type recommendations, FHA financing offers the most accessible entry point due to its 3.5% minimum down payment requirement, which amounts to $6,927 for a median-priced home. This low barrier to entry is crucial in a market where the median household income is $63,347, making the initial capital outlay feasible for first-time buyers. Conventional loans require higher upfront capital, with a 10% down payment costing $19,790. Given the strong price appreciation of 9.2% year-over-year in 2024, buyers must act decisively, as the rapid increase in home values directly inflates the required down payment and monthly obligations. The current 7.40% rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; a shift to a 6.0% rate would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $115, highlighting the sensitivity of local affordability to broader monetary policy. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing pre-approval to lock in current rates, as the combination of rising home values and high interest rates creates a narrow window for optimal financing terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Henry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Henry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Henry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Henry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Henry County qualifies.
