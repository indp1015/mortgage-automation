📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a one‑unit property exceeds the median home value in Kiowa County by more than fivefold (563% of $96,100), confirming that the FHA limit fully covers typical purchases. The conforming conventional limit of $832,750 likewise surpasses the median price by 867%, providing ample coverage for both FHA and conventional financing. For a median‑priced home, the estimated principal‑and‑interest payments are $642 per month for an FHA loan with 3.5% down, $598 per month for a conventional loan with 10% down, and $532 per month for a conventional loan with 20% down. These amounts represent 17.1% of the median household income of $44,962, 16.0% for the 10% down loan, and 14.2% for the 20% down loan, indicating a moderate payment burden relative to income. At the prevailing 7.40% rate, the FHA payment of $642 already incorporates the higher interest cost compared with a 3.5% rate, meaning that a lower rate would further improve affordability. Thus, the 3.5% down payment of $3,364 represents roughly 7.5% of the median household’s annual income, a level that is typically considered affordable. Given the median home price of $96,100 and the availability of FHA financing up to $541,287, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment is the most accessible option for typical buyers, requiring only $3,364 upfront and resulting in a payment that consumes 17.1% of monthly income. Conventional loans with 10% or 20% down are also viable, but the higher down payment reduces affordability, especially since a 20% down payment of $19,220 equals 42.7% of annual household income. The current 7.40% interest rate elevates the monthly P&I compared with lower‑rate environments; a 1% reduction would lower the FHA payment to approximately $560, decreasing the income share to about 15.6%. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing to minimize upfront cash requirements while maintaining a manageable payment ratio.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Kiowa County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Kiowa County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Kiowa County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Kiowa County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Kiowa County qualifies.
