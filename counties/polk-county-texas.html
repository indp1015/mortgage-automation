📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Polk County’s median home price of $184,800 is well within both the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming 1‑unit limit of $832,750, meaning that either loan type can fully finance a typical purchase without exceeding program caps. The FHA limit therefore covers roughly 293 % of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 451 %, confirming adequate financing coverage for most buyers. Monthly principal‑and‑interest payments on a median‑priced home at the current 7.40 % rate are $1,234 for an FHA loan requiring 3.5 % down ($178,332 loan) and $1,023 for a conventional loan with 20 % down ($147,840 loan). These amounts represent 23.8 % and 19.7 % of the median household income of $62,259, respectively, indicating a moderate but noticeable payment burden. Lower‑rate environments would reduce these percentages, improving affordability, whereas the present rate keeps monthly costs near the upper end of conventional debt‑to‑income guidelines. Given the price level and down‑payment constraints, an FHA loan is likely the best fit for typical buyers because it permits a low 3.5 % down payment and aligns with the median price. Conventional financing becomes more attractive for buyers able to provide at least 10 % down, which would lower the monthly payment to $1,151 (10 % down) and further reduce the income share to 18.5 %. The minimum down payment required to purchase at the median price is $6,468 (3.5 %) for FHA or $18,480 (10 %) for conventional; the former is generally feasible with assistance programs, while the latter may be challenging for many households. At 7.40 % the payment‑to‑income ratio is higher than in a 5 % rate scenario, where the same loan would drop to roughly $950 per month, underscoring the impact of interest rate changes on affordability. A concrete insight for prospective buyers is to prioritize saving for a 3.5 % down payment to access FHA financing, thereby keeping monthly obligations within a manageable share of income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Polk County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Polk County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Polk County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Polk County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Polk County qualifies.
