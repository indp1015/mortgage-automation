📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA 1‑unit loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price in Garfield County ($318,100) by 70%, and the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 162%, confirming that both programs fully cover typical purchases. Using the median price, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment ($306,966 balance) requires a principal‑and‑interest payment of $2,125 per month at 7.40%, which equals 41.2% of the median household monthly income of $5,156. A conventional loan with 20% down ($254,480 balance) reduces the payment to $1,761 (34.2% of income), while a 10% down conventional loan brings it to $1,982 (38.4%). Therefore, the high interest rate makes the payment burden substantial relative to income. Given the down‑payment requirements, the FHA product is likely the most accessible for typical buyers, because it permits entry with only 3.5% down ($11,133) while still meeting the loan‑limit criteria. Conventional loans demand substantially higher cash—$63,600 for a 20% down payment, which exceeds annual household income—making them less feasible. VA and USDA options are not quantified, but the existing FHA and conforming limits already provide viable pathways. At the current 7.40% rate, the FHA payment consumes nearly half of monthly income, indicating limited affordability; a drop to 5% would lower the payment to about $1,640, reducing the income share to roughly 32% and improving feasibility. If rates rise to 8%, the FHA payment would climb to roughly $2,250, pushing the ratio above 44% of income and further straining budgets. Prospective buyers should therefore prioritize an FHA loan with the minimum down payment and monitor rate trends to gauge long‑term payment capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Garfield County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Garfield County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Garfield County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Garfield County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Garfield County qualifies.
