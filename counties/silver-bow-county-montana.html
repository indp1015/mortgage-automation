📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Silver Bow County’s housing market demonstrates strong alignment between local property values and federal financing limits. The 2026 FHA single-family loan limit of $541,287 covers 212% of the median home value of $255,200, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 326%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this jurisdiction faces a financing ceiling that restricts access to standard market inventory. This ample coverage ensures that both government-backed and conventional loan products remain viable options for the vast majority of prospective purchasers, eliminating the need for jumbo financing for median-priced properties. The primary constraint on affordability is the current interest rate environment rather than loan limits. With a 30-year fixed rate at 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on a median-priced home is $1,705 per month. This figure represents 33.1% of the median household income of $61,754, a ratio that approaches the upper threshold of standard debt-to-income guidelines. In contrast, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment requires a $1,413 monthly payment, equating to 27.5% of median income. The significant price appreciation, with a five-year CAGR of 13.8%, has outpaced income growth, making the 2022-2024 surge in home values a key driver of current payment burdens. For typical buyers, FHA loans offer the most accessible entry point due to the 3.5% minimum down payment requirement, which amounts to approximately $8,932 for a median-priced home. This lower capital barrier is critical given that conventional loans with 10% or 20% down payments require $25,520 or $51,040, respectively. While VA and USDA loans may offer zero-down options, their eligibility criteria regarding military service or rural location restrict their applicability to a narrower segment of the county’s population. Therefore, FHA financing is likely the most broadly applicable solution for first-time buyers. The high interest rate significantly impacts affordability; compared to a 5% rate environment, the current 7.40% rate increases monthly payments by approximately $300 to $400, effectively reducing the maximum affordable home price for a median-income household. Prospective buyers should prioritize maximizing down payments to reduce loan principal and mitigate the impact of elevated interest rates on long-term debt servicing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Silver Bow County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Silver Bow County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Silver Bow County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Silver Bow County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Silver Bow County qualifies.
