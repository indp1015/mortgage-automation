📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is ample: the FHA 1‑Unit limit of $541,287 exceeds 402 % of the median home value of $134,800, while the conforming 1‑Unit limit of $832,750 represents 618 % of the same price. Consequently, both programs comfortably cover the typical purchase. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show a 3.5 % down‑payment FHA loan requiring $900 in principal and interest, which equals 16.0 % of the median household’s monthly income of $5,612. A conventional loan with 20 % down reduces the payment to $746, or 13.3 % of income, indicating that even the higher‑interest scenario remains within conventional affordability thresholds. The 3.5 % down payment translates to roughly 5.5 % of annual income, a level that many households can manage with savings or assistance programs. Given the local price point and the availability of low‑down‑payment options, an FHA loan is likely the most suitable financing choice for typical buyers, as it requires only 3.5 % down, approximately $4,700 on a $134,800 home, making the purchase feasible despite the median household income of $67,350. Conventional 10 % down ($13,500) is substantially larger and may pose a barrier, whereas VA and USDA programs are not referenced in the data. The current 7.40 % interest rate raises the FHA payment to $900, representing 16 % of income; a reduction of even 0.5 % would lower the payment by roughly $70 per month, improving affordability. Practical insight: prospective buyers should prioritize FHA financing to minimize upfront cash requirements while leveraging the county’s high loan limits, which comfortably exceed median home values, and consider down‑payment assistance to keep the monthly burden near 15 % of income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Towner County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Towner County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Towner County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Towner County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Towner County qualifies.
