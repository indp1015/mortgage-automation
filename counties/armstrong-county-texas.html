📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Armstrong County’s housing market presents a distinct affordability profile where federal loan limits significantly exceed local median home values. The 2026 FHA limit for a single-unit property stands at $541,287, covering 316% of the $171,500 median home price. Similarly, the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 486% of the median value. Consequently, no buyer in the typical price range faces financial constraints due to loan caps. This ample coverage ensures that financing availability is not a barrier for the vast majority of prospective purchasers in this rural Texas market. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though current interest rates exert noticeable pressure. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, the estimated principal and interest payment for an FHA loan on a median-priced home is $1,145 per month. This amount represents 18.9% of the $72,750 median household income, falling within the generally acceptable debt-to-income threshold but leaving less margin for other expenses compared to lower-rate environments. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the monthly P&I to $949, or 15.7% of income, offering greater financial flexibility. In contrast, a 2020-era rate of 3% would have reduced the FHA payment to approximately $710, highlighting how the current 7.40% rate increases the monthly burden by nearly $435. This rate sensitivity underscores the importance of locking in favorable terms, as higher rates significantly erode purchasing power even in low-cost markets. For typical buyers, the FHA loan is likely the most accessible entry point due to its 3.5% minimum down payment requirement. At the median home price, this equates to a down payment of $6,002.50. While this sum is feasible for many households with savings, it remains a significant upfront hurdle. Conventional loans require larger down payments, with 10% costing $17,150 and 20% costing $34,300, which may deter first-time buyers with limited liquidity. Given the 80.2% owner-occupancy rate and the 5.9% five-year price CAGR, the market has seen steady appreciation, though the 7.6% decline in 2024 suggests recent volatility. Prospective buyers should prioritize securing the lowest possible interest rate to mitigate the impact of high borrowing costs on their long-term affordability. The combination of low home prices and high loan limits creates a favorable environment, provided buyers can manage the upfront cash requirements and current rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Armstrong County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Armstrong County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Armstrong County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Armstrong County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Armstrong County qualifies.
