📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The mortgage landscape in Floyd County, Texas, is characterized by significant financing headroom relative to local property values. The 2026 FHA loan limit of $541,287 covers approximately 604% of the median home value of $89,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 929%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces loan limit constraints. This ample coverage ensures that both government-backed and conventional financing options remain fully accessible for the vast majority of residential transactions, eliminating cap-related barriers to entry for standard single-family homes. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment on a median-priced home results in a principal and interest payment of $598. This figure represents 12.3% of the median household income of $58,462, well below the standard 28% debt-to-income threshold. For conventional buyers, a 20% down payment reduces the P&I payment to $496, or 10.2% of income. Although the 7.40% interest rate is higher than historical averages, the low absolute price point of the median home mitigates the impact of elevated rates. The total monthly outlay remains affordable because the loan principal is small, meaning that even with a higher interest rate, the dollar cost of borrowing does not disproportionately strain household budgets compared to higher-cost markets. For typical buyers, the FHA loan is likely the most suitable option due to its low 3.5% down payment requirement. This minimum down payment amounts to approximately $3,136 for a median-priced home, a sum that is feasible for many local households given the median income level. While conventional loans offer slightly lower monthly payments due to higher down payment requirements, the FHA’s lower entry barrier makes it more accessible for first-time buyers or those with limited savings. The recent 11.3% year-over-year decline in median home values from $101,000 to $89,600 further enhances affordability by reducing the required loan amount. Prospective buyers should note that while current rates are high, the low entry price point ensures that monthly obligations remain a small fraction of income. This structural affordability provides a buffer against interest rate volatility, allowing buyers to secure financing without requiring excessive cash reserves or high credit scores associated with premium conventional products. The market’s stability and low cost base make it a resilient environment for mortgage acquisition despite broader national rate pressures.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Floyd County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Floyd County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Floyd County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Floyd County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Floyd County qualifies.
