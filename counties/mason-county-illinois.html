📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 for a single‑unit home is **523 % of the median home price** ($103,400) in Mason County, while the conforming ceiling of $832,750 is **805 %** of that price. Both limits therefore far exceed the typical purchase price, confirming that virtually any buyer can obtain a loan that fully covers the median‑priced home without resorting to high‑cost or jumbo financing. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of **7.24 %**, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $680 with an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $99,781 loan) and $634 with a conventional loan using a 10 % down payment ($93,060 loan). A conventional loan with a 20 % down payment ($82,720 loan) reduces P&I to $563. Relative to the median household income of $62,845 (≈$5,237 monthly), the FHA payment represents **13.0 %** of monthly earnings, while the 20 % conventional payment is **10.8 %**. Both ratios sit comfortably below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % for housing costs, indicating that monthly debt service is not a primary barrier for median‑income households. Given the ample loan limits, low down‑payment requirements, and modest income‑to‑payment ratios, an **FHA loan** is the most accessible option for first‑time or cash‑constrained buyers, requiring only $3,619 (3.5 % of $103,400) upfront. Conventional financing becomes attractive for borrowers who can allocate a 10 %–20 % down payment ($10,340–$20,680), thereby lowering monthly outlays and eliminating mortgage‑insurance premiums. USDA or VA programs could also be viable for eligible rural or veteran purchasers, but the FHA’s near‑universal eligibility and minimal down payment make it the baseline recommendation. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $70–$80 compared with a 5 % environment, yet the impact remains modest because the loan amounts are small relative to national averages. **Practical takeaway:** even with today’s high rates, a median‑priced home in Mason County can be financed with a down payment as low as $3,600 and a monthly P&I burden under 13 % of household income, keeping the purchase well within conventional affordability limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Mason County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Mason County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Mason County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Mason County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Mason County qualifies.
