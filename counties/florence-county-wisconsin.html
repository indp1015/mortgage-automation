📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price in Florence County by 313%, confirming that the FHA program fully covers typical purchases, while the conforming limit of $832,750 provides even greater coverage at 481% of the median price. Consequently, both financing options are adequate for the average buyer. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show that the FHA loan (3.5% down, $167,138 balance) requires a principal‑and‑interest payment of $1,157, representing 22.7% of the median household income of $61,086. Conventional financing with a 10% down payment ($155,880 balance) reduces the payment to $1,079 (17.8% of income), and a 20% down payment ($138,560 balance) lowers it further to $959 (15.8% of income). All scenarios remain below the conventional 30% affordability threshold, though the FHA option carries the highest burden. Given the local price point and loan limits, a conventional loan with a 10% down payment appears most suitable, balancing moderate payment size with the ability to stay within conforming limits without requiring the larger down payment typical of FHA‑eligible borrowers. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is 3.5% ($6,062) for FHA or 10% ($17,320) for conventional; the lower‑down‑payment FHA option is therefore the most feasible for typical buyers. At the current 7.40% interest rate, the FHA payment consumes a larger share of income than it would at lower rates (e.g., 5% would reduce the payment to roughly $870, or 14.3% of income). This rate sensitivity underscores that even modest increases in interest rates can erode affordability, making timely rate locks valuable for prospective purchasers. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize a conventional loan with a 10% down payment to minimize financing costs while staying comfortably within the conforming loan limit and maintaining a payment‑to‑income ratio below 20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Florence County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Florence County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Florence County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Florence County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Florence County qualifies.
