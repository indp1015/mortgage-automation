📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Lyon County, Kentucky, presents a highly accessible financing landscape where federal loan limits significantly exceed local market values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 286% of the median home value of $189,500, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 439%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces restrictions due to loan ceiling caps. The county is not designated as a high-cost area, ensuring standard mortgage terms apply without premium adjustments. This ample coverage allows buyers to finance median-priced homes with substantial headroom, reducing the likelihood of requiring jumbo loans or non-QM products. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down requires a principal and interest payment of $1,266 per month, which constitutes 23.3% of the median household income of $65,066. A conventional loan with 20% down lowers this burden to $1,049 per month, or 19.3% of income. These ratios fall within conventional affordability guidelines, suggesting that the typical household can sustain these obligations. However, the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40% significantly impacts cash flow. Compared to lower-rate environments, this rate increases monthly costs by several hundred dollars, making the initial down payment barrier more critical. The minimum down payment for an FHA loan at the median price is approximately $6,633, a feasible amount for many local households. In contrast, the 20% down payment for a conventional loan requires $37,900, a higher hurdle that may limit access to lower-rate options for some buyers. Given the strong price appreciation, with a five-year compound annual growth rate of 9.0%, buyers face rising entry costs. The median home value has increased from $122,900 in 2017 to $189,500 in 2024. For prospective buyers, the primary strategic consideration is balancing down payment size against interest rate exposure. While FHA loans offer lower entry barriers with minimal down payments, the higher loan balance at 7.40% results in a higher total monthly outlay. Buyers with sufficient savings for a 20% down payment should evaluate conventional loans to reduce principal exposure, whereas those with limited capital should utilize FHA options to enter the market before further price increases. The high owner-occupancy rate of 80.4% indicates a stable market, but the recent 11.9% price jump in 2023 underscores the need for timely financing decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Lyon County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Lyon County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Lyon County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Lyon County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Lyon County qualifies.
