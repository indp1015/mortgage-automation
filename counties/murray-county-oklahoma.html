📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is strong: the FHA 1‑Unit limit of $541,287 equals 339 % of the median home price of $159,800, and the conforming limit of $832,750 equals 521 % of that price, confirming that both programs fully support typical purchases. The estimated monthly principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment for a median‑priced home is $1,067 under an FHA loan with 3.5 % down and $885 under a conventional loan requiring 20 % down. With a median household income of $66,322 (≈$5,527 per month), these payments represent 19.3 % and 16.0 % of monthly income respectively, comfortably below the conventional 30 % affordability threshold. VA and USDA financing, while not quantified, would also meet the price criteria given the median value. Given the price point and down‑payment options, an FHA loan requiring the minimum 3.5 % down payment ($5,593) or a conventional loan with 10 % down ($15,980) are the most practical choices for typical buyers, as they balance low upfront cash requirements with manageable monthly obligations. A 20 % down payment would amount to $31,960, which may be difficult for many households, whereas the 3.5 % and 10 % levels are generally attainable through savings or assistance programs. Consequently, the minimum down payment needed to purchase at the median price is $5,593 for FHA or $15,980 for conventional financing. The current 7.40 % interest rate raises the FHA payment by roughly 30 % compared with a 5 % rate, tightening affordability; however, even at this rate the payment remains under one‑fifth of household income, indicating the market remains reachable for qualified buyers. Prospective purchasers should therefore prioritize securing a loan that allows a down payment of 3.5 %–10 % while monitoring rate trends, as the modest payment burden suggests the market remains reachable for qualified buyers. This insight underscores the importance of low‑down‑payment financing in a high‑rate environment for affordable homeownership in Murray County.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Murray County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Murray County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Murray County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Murray County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Murray County qualifies.
