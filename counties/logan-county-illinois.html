📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both far exceed the median home price of $134,400 in Logan County, with the FHA 1‑unit ceiling at $541,287 (403 % of the median) and the conforming ceiling at $832,750 (620 % of the median). Consequently, virtually any buyer purchasing a typical home can be financed under either program without encountering a loan‑size restriction. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 %, the principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment on a median‑priced home is $883 for an FHA loan (3.5 % down, $129,696 loan) and $824 for a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment ($120,960 loan). With a median household income of $66,358, the monthly income is $5,530; the FHA payment represents 16.0 % of income, while a conventional loan with 20 % down ($107,520 loan, $732 P&I) falls to 13.2 % of income. Both ratios are comfortably below the conventional 28 % housing‑cost threshold, indicating that monthly debt service is affordable for the median household. For most buyers, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option because it eliminates mortgage‑insurance premiums and yields the lowest P&I ($732/month). However, the required $26,880 down payment may be a barrier for first‑time or lower‑wealth purchasers. In that segment, the FHA’s 3.5 % down requirement ($4,704) provides a feasible entry point, albeit with higher monthly payments and mortgage‑insurance costs. USDA or VA loans could be viable for eligible rural or veteran borrowers, offering zero‑down financing and comparable rates, but eligibility constraints limit their universal applicability. The 7.24 % rate inflates monthly payments by roughly $150–$200 relative to a 5 % environment, reducing purchasing power by about $15,000 in loan size. Nonetheless, because loan limits are not binding and income ratios remain modest, affordability remains intact. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers who can secure a 20 % down payment will achieve the lowest monthly cost and avoid mortgage‑insurance, but those unable to meet that threshold should consider FHA financing, which still keeps the P&I burden at only 16 % of median household income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Logan County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Logan County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Logan County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Logan County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Logan County qualifies.
