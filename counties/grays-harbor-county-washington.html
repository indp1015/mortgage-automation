📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Grays Harbor County’s median home value of $314,000 sits comfortably within both the FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming limit of $832,750. The FHA limit covers 172% of the median price, while the conforming limit covers 265%, indicating that standard financing options are fully accessible for the typical property in this market. No high-cost county adjustments apply, keeping loan terms standard for the region. The primary challenge for buyers is the monthly payment burden relative to local income. With a 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan requiring 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $2,097. This amount consumes 39.1% of the median household income of $64,414, which exceeds the standard 36% debt-to-income ratio threshold often used by lenders. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces the payment to $1,739, or 32.4% of income, bringing the burden into a more manageable range. However, the required down payment of $62,800 represents a significant barrier for households earning the median income. In contrast, the FHA minimum down payment of $10,990 is more feasible, though the higher monthly obligation remains a strain. Given these figures, conventional financing is recommended for buyers who can secure the 20% down payment, as it offers lower interest rates and avoids mortgage insurance, thereby reducing the total monthly cost. For those with limited savings, FHA loans remain the most viable entry point, provided the borrower’s overall debt load allows for the higher P&I payment. The current 7.40% rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows; had rates been 5.00%, the FHA payment would have been approximately $1,650, a difference of over $400 monthly. This rate sensitivity underscores that even modest price increases or rate hikes can push the median home out of reach for typical earners. Practically, prospective buyers in Grays Harbor County should prioritize increasing their down payment to at least 10-20% to mitigate the high monthly payment load. A 10% conventional down payment results in a $1,956 payment, still high, but combining this with a lower existing debt load is essential for qualification. The rapid 14.2% five-year price appreciation has outpaced income growth, making the county less affordable than in previous years. Buyers must carefully evaluate their total debt obligations, as the 39.1% income ratio for FHA loans leaves little room for other expenses, potentially requiring co-signers or additional income documentation to secure approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Grays Harbor County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Grays Harbor County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Grays Harbor County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Grays Harbor County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Grays Harbor County qualifies.
