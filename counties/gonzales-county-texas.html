What is the FHA loan limit in Gonzales County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Gonzales County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Gonzales County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.
