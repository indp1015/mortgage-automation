📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit for a single‑unit dwelling in Stephens County ($541,287) is more than three times the 2024 median home value of $175,300, and the conforming limit ($832,750) exceeds the median price by a factor of 4.8. Both limits therefore comfortably cover the typical home, eliminating loan‑size constraints for most buyers. At the median price, a 30‑year FHA loan with a 3.5 % down payment ($169,164 financed) yields a principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment of $1,152 per month. A conventional loan with 10 % down ($157,770 financed) costs $1,075 per month, while a conventional loan with 20 % down ($140,240 financed) reduces P&I to $955 per month. Relative to the median household income of $54,754 (≈$4,563 monthly), the FHA payment represents 25.2 % of monthly earnings, and the 20 % conventional payment represents 20.9 %. Both ratios sit near the conventional 28 % affordability threshold, indicating that mortgage service is a significant but manageable expense for the median household. Given the ample loan‑size capacity, conventional financing is the most cost‑effective option for buyers who can meet a 10–20 % down payment, as it avoids mortgage‑insurance premiums inherent in FHA loans. For purchasers with limited cash, the FHA program remains viable because the required down payment is only $6,136 (3.5 % of $175,300). However, the $6,136 minimum may still be a barrier for households earning the median income, who must allocate roughly 13 % of annual earnings to the down payment alone. The current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.24 % inflates monthly payments by about 1.5 % compared with a 5.7 % rate that prevailed in 2022, reducing purchasing power by roughly $45–$60 per month across loan types. This rate sensitivity underscores the importance of securing the lowest feasible rate or increasing the down payment to lower the loan balance. **Practical takeaway:** For a median‑priced home, a conventional loan with a 20 % down payment keeps the P&I payment under 21 % of monthly income and eliminates mortgage‑insurance costs, making it the most affordable path for buyers who can save roughly $35,000 for the down payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Stephens County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Stephens County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Stephens County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Stephens County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Stephens County qualifies.
