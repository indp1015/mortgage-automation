📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Calhoun County’s housing market presents a distinct affordability profile where financing limits significantly exceed local property values. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 315% of the median home value of $172,100, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 484%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces loan limit constraints. The primary financial challenge is not loan eligibility but rather the monthly payment burden relative to local income levels. With a median household income of $58,682, an FHA loan at the median price requires a principal and interest payment of $1,149, consuming 23.5% of monthly income. A conventional loan with 20% down reduces this to $953, or 19.5% of income. While these ratios fall within standard underwriting guidelines, they leave limited discretionary income for taxes, insurance, and maintenance, particularly given the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%. Loan type selection in Calhoun County is driven more by down payment capacity than by loan limits. For buyers with limited savings, the FHA program offers a viable entry point with only 3.5% down, requiring an initial cash outlay of approximately $6,024 for the median-priced home. This is substantially more accessible than the $34,420 required for a 20% conventional down payment. However, the higher interest rates and mortgage insurance premiums associated with FHA loans increase the long-term cost of ownership. Buyers with sufficient liquidity may prefer conventional financing to avoid mortgage insurance and secure lower total interest costs over the life of the loan. The rapid appreciation, with a five-year CAGR of 10.6%, has outpaced income growth, compressing the affordability window for first-time buyers who lack significant equity in previous properties. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts purchasing power compared to historical low-rate environments. At this rate, a $100,000 loan costs roughly $632 per month, whereas a 3% rate would cost only $424. This differential means that the 7.40% rate effectively reduces the affordable home price by approximately $30,000 to $40,000 for a borrower with a fixed monthly budget. Prospective buyers should prioritize maximizing their down payment to reduce the principal balance, as this directly lowers the monthly payment without relying on rate fluctuations. Additionally, buyers should carefully calculate total housing costs, including property taxes and insurance, to ensure the full monthly obligation remains below 30% of gross income. Given the high owner-occupancy rate of 85.0%, competition for existing homes remains robust, making precise budgeting essential for successful acquisition in this rapidly appreciating market.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Calhoun County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Calhoun County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Calhoun County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Calhoun County qualifies.
