📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Barry County’s housing finance landscape is characterized by robust loan limit coverage relative to local price points. The 2026 FHA one-unit limit of $541,287 covers 205% of the $264,100 median home value, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 315%. Consequently, no borrower in this market faces high-cost county restrictions or limit-based exclusions for standard single-family purchases. This ample headroom ensures that financing availability is not a structural barrier for typical transactions, allowing buyers to access a wide range of loan products without exceeding regulatory caps. Monthly payment burdens remain manageable relative to local income levels, though the current interest rate environment exerts noticeable pressure. At a 7.40% fixed rate, a standard FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $1,764, representing 27.3% of the median household income of $77,500. While this ratio is within the general 30% threshold for qualified applicants, it leaves limited margin for taxes, insurance, and other living expenses. In contrast, a conventional loan with 20% down reduces the monthly P&I to $1,462, or 22.6% of income, offering greater cash flow flexibility. The 7.40% rate significantly increases costs compared to the sub-5% environments of recent years; a drop to 6.0% would reduce the FHA payment by approximately $185, highlighting the sensitivity of affordability to interest rate fluctuations. For the typical buyer, the FHA loan is the most practical recommendation due to its low down payment requirement of $9,244 at the median price. This entry barrier is significantly lower than the $26,410 required for a 10% conventional down payment, making homeownership accessible to first-time buyers with limited savings. However, the higher monthly outlay associated with FHA financing requires careful budgeting. Prospective buyers should prioritize increasing their down payment to at least 10% if possible, as this shift to conventional financing reduces the monthly payment burden and eliminates mortgage insurance premiums. Given the 12.5% five-year price appreciation rate, buyers must act decisively, as waiting increases the required down payment and loan amount. Ultimately, securing pre-approval for an FHA loan allows buyers to compete effectively in this appreciating market while minimizing initial capital requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Barry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Barry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Barry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Barry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Barry County qualifies.
