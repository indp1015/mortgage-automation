📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Kerr County’s median home value of $321,200 sits comfortably within both federal lending frameworks, ensuring broad access to financing options. The FHA single-family limit of $541,287 covers 169% of the local median price, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 259%. Consequently, no typical purchaser in this market faces a high-cost county designation or limit-induced financing gaps. This structural coverage allows buyers to utilize standard mortgage products without requiring jumbo loans or alternative financing structures to secure a median-priced property. The primary constraint on affordability is the current 7.40% interest rate, which significantly elevates monthly obligations relative to local income levels. For a median-priced home, an FHA loan with 3.5% down results in a principal and interest payment of $2,146, consuming 37.1% of the median household income of $69,395. A conventional loan with 20% down lowers this burden to $1,779, or 30.8% of income. While these figures exceed the traditional 28% debt-to-income guideline for housing costs, they remain within the 36% total debt-to-income threshold often used by underwriters, provided other debts are minimal. The rapid 14.4% five-year price appreciation has outpaced income growth, compressing the affordability margin. At 7.40%, every 100-basis-point decrease in rates would reduce the FHA monthly payment by approximately $140, highlighting the substantial sensitivity of local affordability to macroeconomic interest rate fluctuations. Given the low down payment requirements, FHA loans represent the most accessible entry point for first-time buyers in Kerr County. The minimum 3.5% down payment on a $321,200 home requires approximately $11,242, a sum that is more attainable than the $32,120 required for a 10% conventional down payment. While USDA loans may be available in specific rural tracts, the urbanization of Kerrville and surrounding areas likely limits eligibility. VA loans remain a strong option for eligible veterans, offering zero down payment. However, for the general population, the FHA program offers the lowest barrier to entry. Prospective buyers should prioritize increasing their credit scores and reducing existing debt to offset the high interest rate environment. The concrete takeaway is that securing a lower rate through refinance or waiting for market adjustments is more impactful on long-term cost than minimizing the down payment, as the interest component dominates the monthly outlay in this high-rate scenario.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Kerr County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Kerr County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Kerr County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Kerr County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Kerr County qualifies.
