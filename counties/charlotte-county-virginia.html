📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Charlotte County is robust. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 equals 376 % of the 2024 median home value of $144,100, while the conforming loan limit of $832,750 represents 578 % of the same price, confirming that both programs easily encompass the typical residence. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home show a 3.5 % down FHA loan requiring a $139,056 principal and interest payment of $962, which consumes 19.9 % of the median household income of $58,000. A conventional loan with 20 % down on a $115,280 loan results in a $798 P&I payment, or 16.5 % of income, both comfortably below the conventional 30 % affordability threshold. The high‑cost designation is absent, so no special loan limits apply, and the conforming limit also accommodates larger multi‑unit properties if needed. Given the price point and loan limits, an FHA loan with the minimum 3.5 % down payment is likely the most accessible option for typical buyers, as it requires the smallest cash outlay while still meeting the median home value. A 10 % down conventional loan provides a lower monthly payment and avoids mortgage insurance, but the higher upfront cash requirement may be a barrier. The current 7.40 % interest rate raises the FHA payment to $962, making it 19.9 % of income; a reduction to 5 % would lower the payment to roughly $720, improving affordability. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is $5,043 (3.5 % for FHA) or $14,410 (10 % conventional), both feasible for households with modest savings. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should pursue an FHA loan with 3.5 % down to keep monthly housing costs under 20 % of income, leveraging the ample loan limits and maintaining affordability despite the elevated interest rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Charlotte County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Charlotte County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Charlotte County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Charlotte County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Charlotte County qualifies.
