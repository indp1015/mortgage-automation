📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Johnson County’s mortgage landscape is defined by modest home prices relative to federal loan limits. The FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $94,900 by 570%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 878%. Both limits comfortably cover the typical home, confirming that financing constraints are not a barrier for most buyers. Monthly payment burdens illustrate the impact of current interest rates. At a 7.24% rate, the FHA estimate of $624 P&I represents 14.7% of the median household income of $51,028 ($4,252 per month), whereas a conventional loan with a 20% down payment lowers the payment to $517, or 12.2% of income. The higher rate compresses affordability compared with lower‑rate environments, where payments would be proportionally smaller and debt‑to‑income ratios more favorable. For typical buyers, an FHA loan is the most practical option. The minimum down payment of 3.5%—$3,321 on a $94,900 home—poses a relatively low barrier, and the resulting payment remains within a manageable share of income. Conventional financing becomes competitive only for borrowers able to muster a 20% down payment of $18,980, which reduces both the loan amount and monthly obligation. VA or USDA loans could further lower costs for eligible veterans or rural buyers, but FHA remains the baseline solution for the majority of purchasers. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers in Johnson County can secure financing with as little as a 3.5% down payment through an FHA loan, keeping the monthly P&I payment under 15% of median household income despite the current 7.24% rate environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Johnson County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Johnson County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Johnson County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Johnson County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Johnson County qualifies.
