📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is robust: the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home value of $105,300 by more than fivefold (514%), and the conforming limit of $832,750 exceeds it by nearly eight times (791%). Consequently, both loan categories comfortably encompass the typical property in Bailey County. Monthly payment estimates for a median‑priced home illustrate the affordability impact of the current 7.40% rate. An FHA loan requiring a 3.5% down payment on a $101,614 principal results in a principal‑and‑interest payment of $703 per month, representing 13.7% of the median household income of $61,420. Conventional financing with 10% down ($94,770 loan) yields a $656 monthly payment (11.4% of income), while a 20% down payment ($84,240 loan) reduces the payment to $583 (11.4% of income). These ratios remain within conventional affordability thresholds, though the FHA payment is comparatively higher because of the lower down payment. Given the price level and loan limits, an FHA loan is likely the most appropriate vehicle for typical buyers, as it demands the smallest down payment and aligns with the county’s price point. The minimum FHA down payment of $3,686 (3.5% of the median price) is generally attainable for households with modest savings, and while VA or USDA loans are not constrained by the county limit, they require specific eligibility criteria that are not indicated in the local data. The 7.40% interest rate elevates monthly costs relative to lower‑rate environments; a reduction to 5% would lower the FHA payment to approximately $540, decreasing the income share to about 10.4%. Practical takeaway: prospective buyers should prioritize securing an FHA loan to minimize upfront cash requirements and maintain payment‑to‑income ratios below 15% of household income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Bailey County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Bailey County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Bailey County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Bailey County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Bailey County qualifies.
