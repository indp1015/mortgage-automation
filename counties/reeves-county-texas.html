📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage is ample: the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 equals 459 % of the median home price of $118,000, and the conforming 1‑unit limit of $832,750 represents 706 % of that price, confirming that both programs fully cover typical purchases. For a median‑priced home, the Federal Housing Administration loan (3.5 % down, $113,870 principal) requires a monthly principal‑and‑interest payment of $788, which consumes 14.7 % of the median household income of $64,297. A conventional loan with 20 % down (principal $94,400) yields a $653 payment, or 12.2 % of income, while a 10 % down conventional loan (principal $106,200) costs $735 per month (11.5 % of income). Given the size of the loan needed and the availability of low‑down‑payment options, a conventional loan with a 10 % down payment appears to be the most suitable fit for typical buyers, balancing affordable monthly obligations with manageable upfront cash requirements. The minimum down payment to purchase at the median price is $4,130 (3.5 % for FHA) or $11,800 (10 % for conventional); both amounts are attainable for households earning the median income, especially when assisted by savings or gift funds. At the current 7.40 % interest rate, the payment burden is higher than in lower‑rate environments—e.g., a 5 % rate would reduce the 20 % down payment monthly cost to roughly $580, cutting the income share to about 9 % compared with the 12.2 % share at 7.40 %. Practical takeaway: a buyer can secure ownership of the median $118,000 home with as little as $4,130 down using an FHA loan, keeping the monthly payment under 15 % of household income despite the elevated interest rate. Since the median household income is $64,297, the $4,130 down payment represents roughly 6.4 % of annual earnings, a level that most families can achieve within a year or two of saving.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Reeves County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Reeves County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Reeves County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Reeves County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Reeves County qualifies.
