📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA loan limit of $541,287 exceeds the median home price of $356,500 by 152%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 234% of the median value, confirming that both financing categories adequately support typical purchases in Llano County. The median household income of $67,530 equals $5,627 per month. At a 30‑year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan requiring a 3.5% down payment on a $344,022 loan yields a principal‑and‑interest payment of $2,381, or 42.3% of monthly income. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment on a $285,200 loan produces a payment of $1,974, or 35.1% of income, still above the commonly advised 30% threshold. Even a 10% down conventional loan, requiring a $35,650 down payment, results in a $2,221 payment, representing 39.3% of income. Given the high income‑to‑payment ratio, a conventional loan with a 20% down payment offers the most balanced fit for typical buyers, as it lowers the payment relative to income while staying well within the conforming limit. The minimum down payment required at the median price is $12,478 for FHA (3.5%) or $35,650 for a 10% conventional loan; the 20% requirement of $71,300 is likely unattainable for many households. The current 7.40% rate amplifies affordability challenges compared with lower‑rate environments, where the same loan amount would incur roughly $400‑$500 less in monthly principal‑and‑interest, potentially shifting the payment‑to‑income ratio below the 35% benchmark. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize saving for a larger down payment to qualify for a conventional loan, or consider a smaller‑priced property to keep the loan size and monthly cost within sustainable limits. Prospective buyers should therefore aim for a 20% down payment to keep monthly costs near 35% of income, or accept the higher payment ratio associated with FHA financing while monitoring rate fluctuations that could further affect affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Llano County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Llano County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Llano County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Llano County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Llano County qualifies.
