📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Wells County's mortgage landscape presents favorable financing coverage relative to local pricing. The 2026 FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the $197,600 median home value by 274%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 provides 421% coverage. Both limits comfortably encompass the typical transaction, eliminating loan-limit constraints for the vast majority of buyers. The county's rural designation (population 28,420) also preserves USDA eligibility, offering a zero-down alternative for qualified borrowers. Monthly payment burden remains manageable by historical standards. At the current 7.40% rate, an FHA loan with 3.5% down ($190,684 loan amount) yields $1,320 in principal and interest—22.0% of the $5,996 median monthly household income. Conventional financing with 20% down ($158,080 loan) reduces P&I to $1,094, or 18.2% of income. Both ratios fall well below the 28% front-end DTI threshold, though taxes, insurance, and mortgage insurance premiums will increase total housing costs. The minimum down payment barrier is $6,916 (3.5% FHA) or $19,760 (10% conventional), feasible for households with moderate savings given the median income level. Rate sensitivity analysis underscores the affordability impact of the current environment. At 7.40%, the 30-year fixed payment on the median-priced home is approximately 45% higher than at the 2021 trough near 3.0%. However, the low absolute price point mitigates this effect; a 1% rate reduction would lower the FHA payment by roughly $120 monthly. For typical buyers, conventional financing with 5-10% down likely optimizes the trade-off between upfront cash and monthly cost, avoiding FHA's lifetime mortgage insurance while preserving liquidity. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Wells County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Wells County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Wells County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Wells County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Wells County qualifies.
