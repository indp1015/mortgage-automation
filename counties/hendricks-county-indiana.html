📝 Mortgage Market Notes

The FHA and conforming loan limits both comfortably exceed the median home price in Hendricks County. The FHA 1‑unit ceiling of $541,287 is 176 % of the median value of $307,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 is 271 % of that price, confirming that virtually any median‑priced property can be financed under either program without needing a jumbo loan. At the current 30‑year fixed rate of 7.18 %, a median‑priced home financed with a conventional loan and a 20 % down payment ($246,080 loan) yields a principal‑and‑interest (P&I) payment of $1,667 per month. This represents 19.8 % of the median household income ($101,144 annual or $8,428 monthly). An FHA loan with the minimum 3.5 % down ($296,834 loan) results in a $2,010 P&I payment, or 23.8 % of monthly income. Both ratios fall well below the conventional affordability threshold of 28 % for housing costs, indicating that mortgage service is affordable for a typical household. Given the ample loan limits, conventional financing with a 10‑20 % down payment is the most cost‑effective option. The 10 % down scenario ($276,840 loan) produces a $1,875 P&I payment (22.3 % of income) and avoids the FHA mortgage‑insurance premium, while still staying under the conforming limit. FHA financing remains viable for buyers with limited cash, but the higher monthly burden and mandatory insurance reduce its attractiveness for those who can meet a 10 % down payment. USDA or VA loans would only be relevant for eligible rural or veteran purchasers; they do not offer a distinct advantage in this market. The minimum cash required to purchase at the median price is $10,766 (3.5 % FHA) or $30,760 (10 % conventional). For many households, a 10 % down payment is feasible given the median income and high owner‑occupancy rate (78.2 %). Because the 7.18 % rate is roughly 1.5 percentage points above the historic low‑rate environment of 2022‑2023, monthly payments are about 10 % higher than they would be at a 5.5 % rate. Nonetheless, the strong income base and modest price‑to‑income ratio keep overall affordability intact. **Practical takeaway:** Prospective buyers who can allocate a 10 % down payment should prioritize a conventional loan, as it delivers the lowest monthly cost while staying well within income‑based affordability limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Hendricks County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Hendricks County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Hendricks County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Hendricks County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Hendricks County qualifies.
