📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Scott County is exceptionally robust. The 2026 FHA 1-unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the $96,400 median home value by 562%, while the conforming limit of $832,750 provides 864% coverage. Both limits comfortably encompass the entire local market, eliminating loan-limit constraints for virtually all buyers. The median-priced home falls well within standard agency guidelines, ensuring broad access to government-backed and conventional financing. Monthly payment burden remains low relative to income. At the current 7.40% rate, an FHA loan with 3.5% down ($93,026 loan amount) yields $644 in principal and interest, consuming 11.6% of the $5,559 median monthly household income. A conventional loan with 20% down ($77,120 loan) reduces P&I to $533, or 9.6% of income. Even the FHA option sits well below the 28% front-end DTI threshold, indicating strong payment affordability despite elevated rates. The minimum down payment barrier is modest: $3,374 for FHA (3.5%) or $9,640 for conventional 10%, both feasible for median-income households with moderate savings. Given the rural character (population 4,937) and 79% owner-occupancy rate, USDA loans merit primary consideration for eligible buyers, offering 100% financing with no down payment and reduced mortgage insurance costs. FHA serves as a strong alternative for those with credit scores below 640 or higher DTI ratios. Conventional financing becomes optimal for buyers with 20% down seeking to avoid ongoing mortgage insurance. Rate sensitivity analysis shows that at 7.40%, the median buyer's P&I represents roughly half the burden seen in higher-cost markets; a 100-basis-point rate decline would lower the FHA payment by approximately $55 monthly. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Scott County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Scott County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Scott County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Scott County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Scott County qualifies.
