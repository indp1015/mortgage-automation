📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Fentress County’s median home price of $168,200 is well below both the FHA 1‑unit limit of $541,287 and the conforming loan limit of $832,750, meaning that either program can fully finance a typical purchase. Because the FHA loan ceiling exceeds the median value by more than three times, the agency’s financing coverage is adequate for most buyers, and the conforming limit similarly provides ample capacity without requiring a high‑cost designation. The monthly principal‑and‑interest payment on a median‑priced home varies with the loan structure. An FHA loan with a 3.5 % down payment (loan $162,313) carries a payment of $1,123, representing 26.3 % of the median household’s annual income. A conventional loan with 10 % down (loan $151,380) costs $1,048 per month, or about 24.6 % of annual income, while a 20 % down conventional loan (loan $134,560) reduces the payment to $931, equivalent to 21.8 % of income. At the current 7.40 % interest rate, these payments are roughly 20‑25 % higher than they would be at a 5 % rate, underscoring the material impact of rate changes on affordability. Given the low down‑payment requirement, the FHA loan is likely the most accessible option for typical buyers, though eligible veterans could consider a VA loan and income‑qualified purchasers might qualify for USDA financing. The minimum down payment needed to purchase at the median price is $5,887 for FHA (3.5 %) or $16,820 for a 10 % conventional loan; both amounts are attainable for households with modest savings, especially when assisted by gift funds or local down‑payment programs. Consequently, prospective buyers should prioritize securing an FHA loan at today’s rates, as the modest increase in monthly cost relative to lower‑rate environments remains manageable within the median income bracket. This approach balances affordability with the ability to build equity early, making homeownership a realistic goal for the county’s residents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Fentress County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Fentress County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Fentress County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Fentress County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Fentress County qualifies.
