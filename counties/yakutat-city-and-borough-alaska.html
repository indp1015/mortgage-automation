Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Yakutat City and Borough?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Yakutat City and Borough is $811,931. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Yakutat City and Borough?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $1,249,125 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Yakutat City and Borough?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Yakutat City and Borough qualifies.
