📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Broadwater County’s financing landscape is characterized by ample loan limit coverage but significant payment burdens relative to local incomes. The 2026 FHA limit of $541,287 covers 127% of the median home value of $426,600, while the conforming limit of $832,750 covers 195%. Consequently, no typical buyer in this market faces high-cost county restrictions or limit constraints. However, the affordability challenge lies in the ratio of monthly principal and interest (P&I) payments to household income. At the current 30-year fixed rate of 7.40%, an FHA loan with a 3.5% down payment on the median-priced home results in a monthly P&I payment of $2,850. This amount represents 48.6% of the median household income of $70,374. Even with a more substantial 20% down payment on a conventional loan, the P&I payment of $2,362 still consumes 40.3% of median income. These figures indicate that the typical household in Broadwater County is stretched to the maximum or beyond standard debt-to-income ratios, making financing difficult without supplemental income or larger down payments. Regarding loan type recommendations, conventional loans are likely the most suitable option for qualified buyers in this market. Because the median home price is well below both the FHA and conforming limits, the primary differentiator is the down payment requirement and resulting monthly obligation. While FHA loans offer a lower 3.5% down payment threshold, the resulting higher loan balance and mortgage insurance premiums exacerbate the payment burden. A conventional loan with a 20% down payment reduces the principal to $341,280, lowering the monthly P&I to $2,362. Although this still represents a high percentage of income, it is the most efficient path to minimizing monthly cash flow requirements among standard options. USDA loans are likely inapplicable given the urbanization of the county seat and typical income ceilings, while VA loans remain an option for eligible veterans but do not alter the fundamental price-to-income disconnect. The current 7.40% interest rate significantly impacts affordability compared to historical lows. In a 4% rate environment, the 20% down conventional payment would be approximately $1,500, representing only 25.8% of income. The current rate increases the monthly cost by nearly 57%, pushing many median-income households out of the qualifying range. The minimum down payment barrier for an FHA loan is $14,931 (3.5% of $426,600), which is feasible for many, but the resulting payment burden remains prohibitive. For prospective buyers, the concrete takeaway is that purchasing at the median price requires either a household income significantly above the median or a down payment exceeding 20% to bring the debt-to-income ratio into a manageable range for standard underwriting guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Broadwater County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Broadwater County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Broadwater County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Broadwater County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Broadwater County qualifies.
