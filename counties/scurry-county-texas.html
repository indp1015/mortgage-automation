📝 Mortgage Market Notes

Financing coverage in Scurry County is adequate for the typical home. The Federal Housing Administration’s 1‑unit limit of $541,287 exceeds the 2024 median home value of $113,800 by 476%, while the conforming loan limit of $832,750 exceeds it by 732%. Consequently, both FHA and conventional financing satisfy the price point without requiring a high‑cost designation. Given the median household income of $60,039, the estimated monthly principal‑and‑interest payment for an FHA loan (3.5% down, $109,817 loan) is $760, representing 15.2% of monthly income, while a conventional 20% down loan ($91,040) costs $630, or 12.6% of income. These figures indicate a manageable burden, though the 7.40% interest rate elevates payments relative to lower‑rate environments such as 5% or 4%, where the same loan would drop to approximately $620 and $540 respectively. The minimum down payment for the median price is 3.5% ($3,983) under FHA, 10% ($11,380) under conventional 10% down, and 20% ($22,760) for a conventional 20% down loan; the 3.5% requirement is generally feasible, whereas the higher tiers may pose a barrier for typical buyers. The 4.9% annual price appreciation over the past five years has pushed median values upward, making timely financing decisions crucial for buyers seeking to lock in lower rates before further gains erode purchasing power. At a 7.40% rate, the monthly payment consumes roughly half of the 30% affordability benchmark commonly used by lenders, underscoring the advantage of lower‑down‑payment options like FHA. Prospective buyers should therefore prioritize FHA financing to overcome down‑payment constraints while monitoring rate fluctuations that directly affect affordability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FHA loan limit in Scurry County?

The 2026 FHA limit for a single-family home in Scurry County is $541,287. This is above the national floor of $524,225.

What is the conforming loan limit in Scurry County?

The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 for a single-family home. Loans above this amount are considered "jumbo" and typically require stricter credit and larger down payments.

Can I use a VA or USDA loan in Scurry County?

VA loans have no county limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement. USDA loans are available in eligible rural areas—check the USDA's eligibility map to confirm Scurry County qualifies.
